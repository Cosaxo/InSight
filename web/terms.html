What InSight is

A question app. It asks you one question a day, shows you how everyone else answered, and builds a picture of you out of the answers you accumulate — against your city, your country and everyone. You can also play the same question against people you know, in a group or one-on-one, with everybody's picks sealed until the next day.

It is a public app. Your answers, your display name and the profile details you fill in are readable by anyone signed in to InSight, and the counts they feed are published exactly. Showing how one person's answers connect to everyone else's is the whole point of the product; if you want a private place to record what you think, this is not one. The privacy policy sets out precisely who can read what.
