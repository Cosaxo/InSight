# The 2026-09-06 design — what moved, and what it costs to build

**Status: built (D391 steps 1–2, D392 steps 3–6); §7's two owner rows
stand open.** All six build steps shipped 2026-09-06 — the floor (§1)
and the Patterns instrument on paper (§2) under D391, the header dial
(§3), the feed (§4), the ballot (§5) and the polish pass (§6) under
D392 — each section carrying its as-built note; what remains of this
plan is §7, waiting on the owner.
Every item is measured against the tree with the constraint that shapes
it and its backend half named (D167); §8 is the build order as worklist
lines and §9 the gate per step. The source is the owner's
`InSight_9.html` upload of 2026-09-06 — ninth in the numbered series
(`InSight_8` was never uploaded here, like 5 and 6) — extracted to
[`design/standalone-2026-09-06/`](../design/standalone-2026-09-06/).
That directory's README is the inventory (every changed file, the
patches, the unchanged list); this file is the plan.
`docs/VISUAL-VISION.md` names this design as the current vision (D361's
standing rule; the arrival record is D390).

## What was measured

**Everything below was checked against the tree, not assumed.** Against
the latest recorded design state (v18 + v28 patches + the seven later
extractions + the two READMEs' recorded hunks), **89 of 114 modules are
byte-identical** and eight more differ only by hunks the record already
carries in prose. What actually moved is **seventeen modules, seven
stylesheets and one new stylesheet**, and they read as one direction —
**ink on paper** — in four moves:

1. **Paper becomes the ground.** `lens-paper`, the hook nothing set
   since 09-02, goes on the app root — the Patterns field trades its
   dusk disc for paper — and the boxes go with it everywhere: feed
   cards sit on a top-rule ground, the result card loses its card, the
   profile overlay flattens to hairline sections, the two-option ballot
   becomes one hairline row, the test picker becomes rows, the Mirror's
   lens row becomes a hairline row. Figures **draw themselves onto the
   page**: the Patterns ring's arcs, chords and dots, and the Map
   constellation's limbs and nodes, arrive as ink strokes
   (`inkDraw`/`inkIn`/`inkDot`, reduced-motion off).
2. **A 12px floor under every piece of microtype**, app-wide. The
   Patterns tab has stated "no type below 10.5px" since 08-20; this
   raises it to 12 and makes it global — `tokens.css` moves `.kicker`,
   `.klabel`, `.legend`, `.h-meta`, the tab bar and the search groups
   (trackings 0.09–0.1em → 0.07em), five more sheets sweep their own
   9–11.5px labels, and every touched module's inline sizes follow.
3. **The serif voice reaches further** (09-02 gave prompts the voice;
   this extends it): Circle and 1v1 prompts, the Oracle's option labels
   *inside the disc halves*, the Map's hub figure, the result card's
   identity line and tagline, the tests' read line, the People rows'
   shared-answer line.
4. **Chrome collapses into words.** The standing lens legends, titles
   and one-sentence explainers retire behind one ⓘ per lens (`.pt-info`,
   a `guide` prop); the Oracle's field captions (*SEALED GUESS*, *tap
   to pick*, *sealed here*) are deleted — a 1·2·3 strip explains the
   game on demand instead; the daily's ⓘ icon becomes the underlined
   words *why this question* (and the design draws *answer anonymously*
   beside it — an owner decision, §7.1); the feed's topic rail folds
   behind an *all topics* chip; the Patterns lens picker moves into the
   header as a dial that docks like the daily's ruler.

None of it needs a new read, a rules change, a new collection or a
schema. Three things need care: **the repo's `--field-size` token is a
different token than the prototype's** (§5.1 — same name, 16px font
size versus 56px tap height; blind porting would draw a 16px-tall
ballot), the **anonymous-answers words are an open owner decision**
drawn for the third time (§7.1), and the **paid model the data file
extends has been partly overtaken by D371–D377** — what survives is the
subscription seat-split answer 08-26 §2.2 has waited on (§7.2).

## 1 · The 12px floor

`tokens.css` raises the shared microtype classes to 12px and eases
their tracking (0.09–0.1em → 0.07em): `.kicker`, `.klabel`, `.legend`,
`.h-meta`, the four/five-tab bar labels, the accent chip,
`.search-group`. Five more sheets sweep their own labels to 12
(`map.css`, `arena.css`, `oracle.css`, `lens.css`, `patterns.css` — in
the tree: `src/v2/styles.css` and `ui/patterns.css`), and the touched
modules move their inline 9–11.5px sizes with them (the Mirror ruler's
off-stop labels, the 1v1 and Circle chips and counters, the feed's
comment chrome, the person-card chips).

Measured against the tree: the same classes live in
`src/v2/styles.css` (`.kicker` at its D-era 10.5/0.09em) and
`ui/patterns.css`; the sweep is mechanical but **first-paint bytes** —
`styles.css` is eager, the ceiling is 642 KB and the tree drifts ~2.7
KB/day toward it (D365), so the PR quotes the measured delta (a sweep
of font-size values should be ~0 net; the quote proves it). The floor
is also the tree's own direction — D310 lifted `--ink-3` for AA
contrast on "the 10–11px captions it carries"; this retires that size
class instead. Two prototype stragglers stay under the floor in the
upload itself (the Oracle's *you* tag at 11, the hub's *OF N* at 9.5);
port them AT the floor (12) rather than reproducing the miss — the
same call D362 §4.1 made at 10.5.

**As built (D391).** ~80 sites swept; eager graph 602 → 601 KB against
the 607 ceiling ("10.5px" → "12px" is a character lighter). The Mirror
ruler's one-tier adaptation landed at on 13 / off 12 (the design's
four-tier 14/13/12.5/12, folded onto the tree's two sizes). Three
things stay under the floor on purpose, each with its reason at the
site: the Mirror lens row's seven-tab ladder (10.5 is what makes
"Foresight" fit at 320px — D135's measurement, which the upload's own
`.mm-lensbtn` twin also leaves alone), two glyph marks (mirror-field's
9px "+", the 1v1 rail's overflow badge — marks, not reading text), and
the daily's 11.5px context ⓘ, which §5 replaces with words rather than
resizing first. `.pp-kick` swept with the rest: the upload has no rule
of that name, but it is the tree's `.ar-kick`, which the design took to
12.

## 2 · The Patterns instrument on paper

### 2.1 · The Map ring quiets

`question-map.jsx` (→ `ui/PatternsMap.tsx` + `ui/patterns.css`):

- **Short topic groups get their name inside the rim** — straight,
  anchored toward the hub, candidate texts (full name, then the first
  word) tried across four radii, refused when they would touch the hub,
  reach the dots, leave the wedge or collide with a placed label
  (`lblBoxes`). Long groups keep their name on the arc. The port is a
  pure layout addition to the memoised ring builder — it depends only
  on pool identity and topic order, so it still never re-runs on a
  vote.
- **The hub goes serif**: a 36/500 figure over *OF N* (9.5 → port at
  12 per §1), r 44, ring stroke gone. The `qm-ink` class marks hub,
  labels and arcs for the ink-in animation.
- **The field quiets**: idle chords 0.13 → 0.05 opacity in `--ln-ink`,
  the strongest-ten (`qm-fig`) at 0.5 with thinner strokes; arcs go
  4px and **draw in** (`qm-arc`, `pathLength: 1`, staggered ~50ms);
  dots pop in by rim angle (`qm-dot`). The tree already has one
  draw-in (`qmDraw`, `ui/patterns.css:113`, the answered-burst) — the
  port unifies it with the new `inkDraw` family rather than shipping
  two keyframe sets that do the same thing.
- **The on-field callout pill is deleted**, and the beacon keeps its
  pulse but loses its *Answer next →* text. The strongest-link
  sentence compacts into the card: *X on short → Y on short* — serif
  17, the option words sans 13.5/800 in their topics' inks, the
  percentage 28/700 **in ink, not accent**. It reads the questions'
  `short` names (in the bank since the 08-26 record) with `prompt` as
  the fallback.
- **`.ln-head` is deleted and the card loses its box** (`ln-card`
  without `card`). The title and the one-sentence explainer move into
  the guide legend (§2.4). **The D146/D310 basis sentences survive**:
  *"counted over everyone who answered both"* stays on the tie card
  (with D310's bounded-sample wording live), and the legend keeps the
  link-line sentence — behind the ⓘ, but stated.

### 2.2 · The Oracle's options move into the halves

`oracle.jsx` (→ `ui/PatternsOracle.tsx` + `ui/patterns.css`): option
labels render inside the disc halves at serif 17–20/500, up to two
lines broken at the most even space (`orLines`); the rim and a now
full-height seam become quiet ink hairlines (0.16 opacity); the picked
half tints 8% until the reveal lands; the disc rests lower (cy +66);
the kicker becomes *N of M* (`PAT.stats()`, figures the tab holds);
the prompt grows to serif 24. Deleted: *tap to pick*, *SEALED GUESS* /
*ITS GUESS*, *sealed here*, the confidence word under the disc, and
the *sealed* chip. On demand instead (§2.4): a **1·2·3 strip**
(`.or2-how` — *it guesses, sealed · you tap a half · did it have
you?*), the ledger key, and the pre-answer explainer paragraph.

Measured against the tree: the seal, the grade and the working are the
same calls — **the pin in `data/patterns.test.ts` must not move** (the
standing rule since 08-26). The confidence word (`orSure`) loses its
last render site; whether it stays exported for the working's copy is
the PR's call, said in the commit. The `options.slice(0, 2)` guard and
its reasoning (D362 §1.6) survive — the stacked `.qm-opt` fallback is
still drawn for a hypothetical three-option pool. The uppercase-by-CSS
rule from D362 stops applying to the half labels (they are serif
mixed-case now); the accessible name is the option's real text either
way.

### 2.3 · The People lens: names on tap, rows under the field

`people-lens.jsx` (→ `ui/PatternsPeople.tsx` + `ui/patterns.css`, the
`.ln-list` family): no name is spotted on the field — the tapped
person alone is named beside their dot; two faint guide rings (r/3,
2r/3) frame the disc; the paper palette retunes (split to warm-grey
hue 80, disagree to hue 35, both chroma-eased); the *Most like you*
chip rail becomes **three rows** — avatar tinted by the person's
agreement step, name, *agrees a of b*, and a shared-answer line
(*Both said X* / *You split on everything you share*, `tieFor` — a
fold over figures the lens already holds).

Measured against the tree: `agree` / `shared` / `tieFor` all exist in
`data/peopleMap.ts` and the lens; the rows are a re-dress of the rail
D362 built. Two D362 rules carry unchanged: nameless accounts are
*Someone* (never an invented initial avatar — the avatar tint is the
agreement step, so a Someone row still reads honestly), and the
`listitem` role sits on a wrapper, not the button. The floor sentence
(*everyone who answered at least k of your M*) moves from the foot
card into the guide legend — behind the ⓘ but stated, with `placed.
length` beside it (§2.4's rule).

### 2.4 · The guide ⓘ — what retires, and what may not

Every lens's meta row leads with `.pt-info` (a 32px serif-italic *i*)
toggling one `guide` state per tab; the always-on `.ln-key` legends,
`.ln-hint` lines, `.ln-head` titles, the Map's *● answered · ○ open ·
ties* facts line and the Oracle's progress track all retire into it.
The hub, the kickers and the rows now carry the numbers.

The rule for the port, so D146 survives the quieting: **a basis
sentence may move behind the ⓘ; it may not be deleted.** The legend
under `guide` carries — per lens — the link-line sentence, the
colour/size key with the floor sentence, the ledger key, and the 1·2·3
strip. `check:a11y` wants the ⓘ labelled (`aria-label="Legend"`,
`aria-expanded`) and 32px wants `.tap44` or a grown box per
`check:tap-targets`. The `guide` state is per-tab and ephemeral — no
device key, so `check:purge`'s subject set does not grow.

### 2.5 · The ruler docks, and drags ride the axis

`patterns-tab.jsx` (→ `ui/PatternsTab.tsx`): the in-flow ruler folds
away once scrolled past (hysteresis: gone at the top edge, 8px back
releases) OR after using a lens (pointerup inside the stack — the
lenses fill the screen and rarely scroll); a wheel-up or a 36px pull
at the top brings it back; `onDock` tells the shell either way. The
drag SKIP list drops `svg` — the lenses are tap-only, so a horizontal
drag starting on a disc rides the axis (the 09-02 build's `.or-lens`
special case generalises to all three) — and adds `.ln-rail`.

Measured against the tree: `ui/PatternsTab.tsx` already owns the axis,
the spring and the far-end `NAV.goNav('track:world')` exit (D166's
licensed joint; its two pins — far-end calls once, first-stop calls
nothing — must survive). The dock listener attaches to the `.app`
scroll host in capture phase, the same pattern the daily uses. The
ruler's `aria-hidden` follows `hid`.

### 2.6 · As built (D391) — what step 2 settled

Step 2 shipped §2.1–§2.4 plus §2.5's drag-ownership half (the SKIP list
rode the same file) and §3's `lens-paper` line; the docking and the
header dial stay step 3's. Six things the build settled that this plan
had left open or under-specified:

1. **The repo's floors beat the design's sizes three more times**: the
   ⓘ draws at 44px, not 32 (`check:tap-targets`' class-sized
   convention, the same call the topic pill made), the sub-row keeps
   its 44px min-height against the design's 34 for the same reason,
   and the Oracle's *YOU* tag ports at 12 (§1's rule).
2. **A control that loses its words may not lose its name** — the
   beacon (role, tab stop, `Answer next: <prompt>` as its label) and
   the Oracle's field (the sealed state lives in the svg's accessible
   name once the chip is gone) both gained the pin. `check:a11y`'s
   baseline did not move.
3. **The People fold lost its label solver whole** — `near`, `lab`,
   `PEOPLE_LABELS` and ~60 lines of spot-scoring placement, D362
   §1.6's own precedent (a layout engine whose surface is gone is
   residue). `PEOPLE_ALIKE = 3` caps the rows; the likeness-not-layout
   ranking and the named-only rule (D167) carried into them unchanged,
   and the guide legend prints `basis`/`minShared`, never the
   prototype's own-answers count — the overstatement the foot card was
   corrected for does not come back one tap away.
4. **The strongest-link basis line stays against the design**, which
   deletes it: *counted over the N people in both samples* is D310's
   bounded-sample wording, and §2.4's rule cuts both ways. The compact
   sentence carries full prompts — the bank has no `short` names — via
   the design's own fallback.
5. **One ink-in family, not two**: the tapped-chord draw the ring
   shipped with (`qmDraw`) folded into `inkDraw` rather than keeping a
   twin keyframe set.
6. **The 1·2·3 strip and the pre-tap explainer are guide-gated; the
   post-tap verdict is unconditional** — what a sentence claims about a
   record that exists still ships without a tap.

The suites hold at +5 pins (the in-rim label's geometry, the map
legend's gating, the option words in the halves, the guide's
persistence across a lens swap, the People rows' shared-answer line);
`data/patterns.test.ts` is byte-identical; `check:globals` rule 4
stands at its baseline; the eager graph did not move (the chunk is
lazy).

## 3 · The shell: the dial in the header

`app.jsx` (→ `spec/app-shell.jsx`, the converted shell): the Patterns
lens state lifts into the shell (`ptLens`, default `map`), and the
header dock slot — which today serves only the daily
(`DOCK_STOPS`, `app-shell.jsx:217`; the slot at 680) — serves **track
and patterns**: the daily's stops renamed `DAILY_DOTS` (same three,
same accents), a `PT_DIAL` of *Oracle · Questions · People* (short
labels; the in-page ruler keeps *Question map* / *People map* — the
tree's `LENSES` order already matches), haptic ticks on both,
`data-docked` on either tab, reset on every tab change. The daily
passes `hideSwitcher: true` — the dots do its switcher's job once
docked. `lens-paper` joins the app-root class list (§0's first move):
`ui/patterns.css:200`'s *"nothing sets that class"* comment moves to
say the shell sets it, and the dusk branch of the `--ln-*` tokens
becomes the dead one.

Measured against the tree: the lens state crosses from shell to tab as
**props** (`lens` / `onLens` / `onDock` on `PatternsTab`), exactly as
`dailyMode` already crosses to the daily — no window global, so
`check:globals` rule 4 does not move. `PatternsTab` keeps an internal
fallback state for a bare mount (the mount fixtures render it without
the shell). The dial buttons are header chrome: `check:tap-targets`
already governs `.h-dockstop`. The upload's `dots.css` carries an
unused `.h-dots` family — the dial actually wears the existing
`.h-dockruler` / `.h-dockstop` classes; **do not port the dead block**
(the README records it).

### 3.1 · As built (D392) — what step 3 settled

Three places the tree's standing contracts beat the design's lines,
each recorded at its site:

1. **The docked twins keep their in-flow rulers' names.** The design
   renames the dial *Which daily* / *Which lens*; the tree's rule is
   that a docked copy IS its ruler, docked — one control with two
   names reads as two controls — and `smoke-nav` pins the daily pair
   by the shared label. The renames stay unported.
2. **The dock slot may not go `aria-hidden` while undocked.** Tried,
   and it broke `daily-modes` and `smoke-nav` in the same run: the
   crossfade contract needs the ruler present and role-queryable the
   whole time, and the mode suites drive the daily through those very
   stops. The tree's own discipline — `tabIndex` `docked ? 0 : -1`,
   no `aria-hidden` — carries the state instead, against the design's
   always-tabbable stops.
3. **The fold effect owns nothing it cannot find.** It reads the
   `.app` scroll host optionally (a bare `PatternsTab` mount has no
   shell), so the wrap's own listeners always attach and the
   host-scroll hysteresis arms only where a host exists.

The controlled-lens shape is the one the hooks lint accepts: the tab's
internal state stays the slide-direction source and adopts the shell's
prop one effect later, seeded from it on mount — deriving during
render is the set-state-in-render shape the compiler refuses. The
shell resets `docked` on every tab change, and the dial's accent
follows the stop (`--dacc`), both pinned. Rule 4's baseline did not
move.

## 4 · The feed on paper, and the folded topics

`world-feed.jsx.patch` (→ `spec/world-feed.jsx`, an adapted port —
both surfaces diverged at conversion, so this is an adaptation, not a
paste):

- **Boxed card types join the bare ground**: where the tree draws a
  boxed `card` skin (rankings and kin — not paid, not collapsed, not
  focus), the card goes transparent — no border, radius or shadow, a
  top rule (`WF_LINE`) and air (22/26 padding), the same ground the
  bare cards have worn since the v2 pass. The paid card keeps its box
  on purpose: a sponsored slot that dissolves into the editorial
  ground would blur D375's one-product line.
- **The topic rail folds**: a *the feed* kicker on the left, the sort
  chip, and one disclosure chip — *all topics* / *N of M topics*, a
  chevron, `aria-expanded` — that opens the rail (anchor sites in the
  tree: the rail at `spec/world-feed.jsx:4428`, the sort chip at
  4431). Open-state chips go neutral — ink ground, surface text, a
  check glyph — dropping the per-topic colours (*"the per-topic
  colours read as clutter here"*; the topic hue still lives on every
  card's kicker, so the colour is not lost, only de-duplicated).
- **The `headHold` guard**: opening the rail grows the sticky head;
  scroll anchoring then nudges `scrollTop`, which read as a
  scroll-down and hid the bar mid-use. A 500ms hold after the toggle
  ignores that echo, and the bar never hides while the rail is open.

The vote path, the kickers' form words, the serif prompts — all the
09-02 grammar — are untouched by this patch beyond the floor sizes.

### 4.1 · As built (D392) — what step 4 settled

The fold moved a door six suites walked through: *+ Add a topic* now
sits behind the disclosure, so each of those cases opens the rail
first, `smoke-daily`'s feed witness became the disclosure chip itself
(a better witness — it exists before any interaction), and the fold
gained its own pin (closed by default, `aria-expanded` truthful, the
count label naming *N of M* only when some are off). The `headHold`
guard reads the toggle stamp AND `topicsOpen` — the bar never hides
while the rail is open, not just in the 500ms echo window. One scope
call recorded here because it will look like an omission: the feed's
~60 inline sub-12px sizes on surfaces this patch does not touch are
the design's own leftover — the floor rule (§1) ports touched
modules' stragglers and leaves untouched chrome as the design left
it, so they stay until a patch reaches them.

## 5 · The daily: the ballot as a hairline row, and the words below it

### 5.1 · The ballot

`daily-split.jsx.patch` (→ `spec/daily-split.jsx:1064`, the boxed seam
block the 09-02 build landed): the box goes — `.ds-ballot` is
borderTop + borderBottom, halves transparent (`.ds-half`), the seam an
inner hairline, labels centered, the colour dots gone (the option's
hue survives in `--oc`, drawn only on press: a 9% tint and a 3px
underline). The reveal block and the stacked ≥3-option rows keep their
shapes; *· you* stays the ink pill at the floor size.

**The trap: `--field-size` is two different tokens.** The prototype's
(56px, *"every tappable input sits on this height"*) is the ballot's
min-height; the repo's (`styles.css:303`, **16px**) is the input FONT
size that stops iOS zooming — a blind port draws a 16px-tall ballot.
Port the height as its own token (`--ballot-h: 56px` beside the ballot
rules) or a literal, and leave `--field-size` alone. D86's long-press
edit lives in the reveal block and must survive the re-layout; D312's
pending-answer mirror is below this layer and untouched. The mount
suites walk the split ballot before and after a vote, so a crashed
ballot is a red `ErrorBoundary`, not a silent miss.

### 5.2 · The words under it

The ⓘ icon button (`spec/daily-split.jsx:1034`, `tap44`, *About this
question*) becomes the underlined words **why this question** in the
row under the ballot — same `ctx` sheet, keep `tap44`, and keep the
`ctxLabel` distinction (*What you need to know* on a sponsored card).
Beside it the design draws **answer anonymously** → *anonymous · on* →
*answered anonymously* — the toggle VISION-2026-08-26 §1 designed and
the 09-02 design drew on the feed. **That control does not build until
the owner answers the D98-amendment row** (§7.1): the words are the
surface of a data-model change (an answer a stranger cannot attribute)
that is exactly the still-open decision. The port ships the ballot and
*why this question*; the anon words land in the same slot the day the
row is ticked.

### 5.3 · As built (D392) — what step 5 settled

The height shipped as a **literal 56 with the trap comment at the
site**, not a new token — one consumer does not earn a token, and the
comment names §5.1's collision so the next reader cannot reach for
`var(--field-size)`. The split-ballot suite's shape-finder split into
`dailyBallot()` / `feedBallots()`, pinning what is now the design's
