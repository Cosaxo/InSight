# Bridge verdicts

Central's rulings on the lanes' REQUESTS queues (CHARTER §8), one
entry per request: **worth-building** (with the cost/benefit argument),
**not-yet** (with what would change the answer), or **needs-owner**.
Worth-building verdicts surface in the digest; a person carries them to
InSight's governed process on `main`. No theory lane implements its own
request.
