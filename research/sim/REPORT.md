Read against G7 §3: every row holds as the paper states it EXCEPT the context row. A context (group) term shared by several pairs is confounded on groups of three whose units are in no other group; it is identified on groups of four or more with no crossing at all, because two pairs with no member in common share the context and nothing else; and it is identified on groups of three where units cross groups. The paper's row — "identified only where units cross contexts" — was too strong and is corrected.
