# Measured ability meets the genome

*Paper 1 of the axiom series: what a keyed reasoning test and imported genome files make knowable together, and what the pair gives back to every other axis.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 0 carries the general argument this paper stands on; a claim made there is used here by section number rather than restated.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because wherever ability is not measured, something else stands in for it, and any population that pairs a repeatable keyed reasoning test with genome files people already hold can measure ability genetics directly rather than through a stand-in.

**Abstract.** Where thinking ability is not measured on the populations genetics needs, a stand-in is used, and the stand-in that is free to record is how long a person stayed in school. It carries everything that decides who stays in school. This paper describes a population in which ability is measured directly, by one repeatable, language-light reasoning test, on people who already hold their own genome files, and argues that the pair makes five things knowable that neither axis, nor a proxy, can reach: the genetics of ability as the test measures it, separated from the genetics of attainment on one population; how a genetic prediction of ability behaves when it crosses a country and when it crosses an ancestry, which are two different experiments; the genetics of how ability changes, once the test is repeated under a design that separates practice from growth; the genetics of the gap between believed and measured ability; and within-family ability genetics, assembled from the genomes themselves wherever relatives are present. It then argues the half that matters more to the rest of the series: a keyed ability score crossed with a genome lets an unkeyed opinion bank learn which of its items are ability-loaded, lets self-report trait scales be read with ability partialled out, lets reciprocal guessing games separate a perceptive guesser from a clever one, and gives the ability predictor a phenotype to be judged against. The conditions are stated as part of the theory: what an unsupervised test measures, what a consumer genome file does and does not carry, and which readings no population size identifies without a design device.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A genetic architecture is read from a trait measured on very many people who are also genotyped. Traits that are cheap to record are read this way at scale. Ability is not cheap to record: it is a test, and a test costs the taker an hour of attention. So wherever ability genetics is read at scale, a stand-in is used, and the stand-in that costs nothing is how long a person stayed in school.

Attainment carries everything that decides who stays in school and that is not ability: family wealth and parental education, patience and persistence, health in childhood, the schooling system of the country, the value a culture puts on credentials, and luck. Each of these has its own genetics and its own environment, and each enters the genetics of the stand-in. What is read is therefore the genetics of a mixture, and nothing inside the mixture says what fraction of it is ability. Where a small tested sample is compared with a large attainment sample, the comparison runs across different people, which is inference by subtraction rather than measurement.

Three further things are unreadable through a proxy. Whether ability's genetics is the same across populations, because the proxy's own meaning changes at every border and the tested populations are narrow. How ability changes with age, because attainment is fixed by early adulthood and cannot have a trajectory. And how a person's belief about their own ability relates to their measured ability, which is not a question a proxy can form.

**2 · The two axes alone, and where each stops**

The reasoning test is one keyed measurement. Keyed means graded against an answer the person does not control, which distinguishes it from every self-report in the system. It is repeatable in principle, built to lean as little as possible on language, and normed against a population. Alone and at one moment it is a ranking. Repeated, it can describe a change, but it cannot say what produced one, where the ability comes from, or whether the thing it measures in one culture is the thing it measures in another. Its ceiling is causal.

The genome, imported as the raw file a consumer service already produced, is a fact about the person rather than a report by them. Alone it predicts nothing that can be checked, because a genetic prediction is only as good as the phenotype it was trained against, and the file arrives with no phenotype attached. Read against weights trained on a proxy it inherits the proxy's mixture. Its ceiling is meaning: it needs something measured well, on the same people, to be about.

Each axis's ceiling is the other axis.

**3 · The crossing, and one exclusion it imposes**

Paper 0 states the general argument for why crossing axes multiplies rather than adds. Two of its parts are specific to this pair and are stated here.

A keyed measurement anchors a reported one, because it supplies a truth the report can be checked against, and a reported measurement gives a keyed one meaning, because it says what the graded quantity is about in a life. And a covariance between two measurements on the same people is a third measurement that exists only in the pair: the genome alone is text, the test alone is a score, and their covariance across a population is the architecture, a fact neither axis contains.

A third axis separates a fused component only where it carries the signal without the same bias. Attainment proxies ability with wealth; a keyed test carries ability without wealth, so the two together separate what neither can alone. Distinct pairings grow with the square of the number of axes, and each pairing separates a component only where that independence holds. Two self-report axes sharing one self-presentation norm separate nothing, however many pairs they form.

One prior is excluded by construction. Paper 0 argues that each axis is a prior on the others and that a prior lowers the cost of measurement. Here that argument stops: the genome may not inform the ability measurement it will later be read against, because a score that contains its own predictor cannot test it. The ability phenotype is measured blind to the genome, or every reading in section 4 is inflated by construction.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

Each reading carries its own requirement. Scale removes precision limits and removes nothing else: where a reading is not identified, the fix is a design device or an outside anchor, and more people do not supply one.

*4.1 The genetics of ability separated from the genetics of attainment, on one population.* The population carries an education band as a profile fact and a measured reasoning score as a keyed fact, on the same people, beside the genome. The genetic signal that predicts the band can be split into the part that also predicts the score and the part that does not. The remainder is defined relative to this test: it is everything about attainment that is not ability as this test measures it, so it also absorbs the test's unreliability and whatever facets of ability the test does not cover. The decomposition is therefore reported disattenuated, with the test's reliability and its construct coverage stated as part of the number, and it is read within a country before it is read across, because a band names a national credential ladder and not a common quantity. Two further limits are design limits, not size limits. Telling a transmitted genetic effect from an environment a parent's genotype produced needs the family reading of 4.5. And a change across birth cohorts is confounded with age and with calendar time, which are exactly collinear, so a cohort claim needs an outside anchor or a design that breaks the collinearity.

*4.2 How a genetic prediction of ability behaves when it crosses a border, and when it crosses an ancestry.* A prediction built on one population degrades when applied to another. The degradation has several sources at once: differences in which variants travel together, differences in allele frequency, a phenotype that does not mean the same thing, gene-by-environment interaction, assortative mating and residual structure. A single decay curve cannot separate the same causal variants tagged worse from different effects. So the reading is two experiments. Held at one ancestry and moved across countries, the decay reads as measurement and environment: does the test mean the same thing there, and does the same genetic signal express the same way. Held in one country and moved across ancestries, the decay reads as tagging. Telling weaker tagging from different effects needs the predictor re-estimated within each group, or effects contrasted along local ancestry within admixed people, and a population spread across both countries and ancestries is what makes both experiments available on one instrument.

*4.3 The genetics of change.* The curve is the test axis's own: repeated, the test gives each person a level and a slope, and over a long enough window a shape. The genome supplies the question about the curve's parameters, which is a different question from the genetics of the level. Two design conditions make the parameters phenotypes at all. Practice must be decorrelated from age and calendar time by design, first exposure staggered across people, because a retest gain that is measured and subtracted takes real signal with it, and general test familiarity is largest on the first sittings and is itself correlated with ability. And each parameter is reported with its own reliability: a slope from few occasions carries little, and its correlation with the level is read against the regression-to-the-mean floor. Peak age and the shape of a decline are population curve parameters until a person has been measured across decades, and they meet the age, period and cohort collinearity of 4.1.

*4.4 The genetics of self-assessment.* Several self-report trait scales contain items about how able a person believes they are. The quantity is belief residualised on measured ability, disattenuated for both, not a raw difference: a raw difference is less reliable than either part and is bounded by the measured score in a way that manufactures the pattern it is meant to find. So defined, the residual is a trait of the person, and whether it is heritable, whether it clusters in families, whether the genetic signal that predicts measured ability also predicts underrating it, and whether it is the same trait in every culture, become askable because belief, measurement and genome sit on the same people. Effort is the confound to name here: a low-effort sitting reads as overconfidence, which is why section 6 requires effort to be modelled from the response record.

*4.5 Within-family ability genetics, assembled from the genomes.* Relatedness is read out of the genomes themselves, out to distant cousins, so the within-family design assembles wherever relatives happen to be present in the population, and a declared family tie adds interpretation rather than detection. What the design buys is control: within a family, stratification, assortative mating and the environment a parent's genotype produced are held fixed, at the price of power per pair and of the between-family component, which the design cannot estimate. Parent-offspring pairs are what let 4.1 tell a transmitted effect from a produced one. A population in which relatives use the same system is a family study that recruited itself.

**5 · The return path: what the crossing gives back to every other axis**

The readings above are what the pair knows about ability. The larger claim is that a keyed ability score, once it exists beside a genome, changes what each other axis can say. The axes are named here by what they are.

*The opinion bank, an unkeyed bank of opinion and preference items with no ceiling on its size.* Opinions have no right answer, so the bank has no key. Location, how the population splits on an item, the answer distribution already gives. What the ability score adds to each item is a discrimination: how sharply its split moves with ability once the profile facts and the dispositions the two share are conditioned out. Almost every attitude item tilts with ability through education, age, income and culture, so the raw tilt says nothing; the residual tilt says the item is ability-loaded. It does not say the item has a right answer. Across an unbounded bank that residual discrimination is a map of which opinions are partly reasoning and which are wholly taste, and no bank can compute it about itself.

*Self-report trait scales.* Every self-report personality scale carries some ability variance somewhere, most visibly where openness shades into intellect and where items about learning, curiosity and mental speed sit inside trait scales. With a keyed ability score beside every profile, the ability component can be read out of each scale and disposition read on its own. The self-assessment reading of 4.4 becomes a routine correction rather than a study.

*Sealed reciprocal guesses between people who know each other.* Such a record grades each person's guesses about their partner against the partner's sealed answer, so it knows who guesses well and cannot say why. Regressing accuracy on ability and calling the residual perceptiveness would leave noise, target effects and acquaintance in the residual. The separator is a contrast the record already contains. Split each guess's accuracy into the part that tracks the population's modal answer, which anyone clever can infer, and the part that tracks this target's departure from the mode, which only someone who knows the person can supply. Ability should buy the first and not the second. A guesser who buys the second is perceptive, and the contrast, crossed with ability, is what separates the two.

*The genome layer itself.* Two different qualities are at stake and the pair validates only one of them. The technical quality of an imported file is checked on its own terms: call rate, duplicate concordance, whether the file's sex and ancestry agree with the person. The predictive quality of the layer is checked against whichever phenotype the system holds with the strongest and most portable predictor, and a reported height band is a far better check on whether an imported file is what it claims to be than a reasoning score is. What the reasoning score calibrates is the ability predictor, and it cannot validate the layer it depends on: a phenotype the pair exists to study is not the phenotype that vouches for the pair.

*Interests.* What a person is into correlates with ability, and the correlation is read in most settings as a proxy. The crossing lets it be decomposed: whether the association between an interest and ability is genetic or environmental, and whether interests mediate between ability genetics and attainment, which feeds 4.1 directly, because an interest that carries ability genetics into staying in school is part of the remainder that reading is trying to name.

*Daily self-reported state.* Once the test repeats, a sitting is an occasion, and the body's occasions, sleep, illness and energy, can be crossed with it to separate cognitive state from cognitive trait. Two conditions make the reading honest. The occasion reading and the trajectory reading want opposite schedules, dense enough to catch a bad day and sparse enough not to teach the test, so they run on different forms or different people. And the state report is taken before the test, not after, or the direction is not readable: a bad score colours the report it follows, and a common cause such as an illness that lowers both is indistinguishable from the body affecting reasoning unless the order is fixed and the illness is recorded as an event.

**6 · What would have to hold**

The readings are claims about capability under conditions, and the conditions are part of the theory rather than caveats to it.

The test measures reasoning with a stable meaning across the languages and cultures of the population. It is built language-light, and intent is not invariance: that a given score means the same ability in every country is established rather than assumed, per test form and per population, before 4.2's first experiment is a reading rather than an artefact.

An unsupervised test measures ability under whatever effort the taker brought. Effort is modelled from the response record, timing, abandonment and the consistency of response patterns, or the ability phenotype carries the very dispositions 4.1 is trying to remove from attainment, and 4.4 reads a lazy sitting as overconfidence.

Consumer genome files are genotyped at common markers and imputed to millions of common variants. Imputation quality falls with allele frequency and with distance from the reference panel, so the coverage limit is itself ancestry-dependent and entangled with 4.2. Chip, version and vendor are batch structure that aligns with country and ancestry and enters every model. The files carry no quality metrics and no proof of whose they are, so identity is established from the data, by sex check, relatedness and ancestry consistency, before a file is a phenotype's partner. Every architecture claim is a common-variant claim; rare-variant effects are out of reach by construction.

Ancestry is modelled, or population structure produces associations that are not effects.

Selection is anchored from outside. Who takes a reasoning test inside a voluntary system is not a random sample of anyone, and a self-selected population cannot validate its own selection model without an outside anchor, a probability sample or a register. The bound travels with every number.

Consent is familial-aware. A genome describes relatives who did not consent, and the consent that opens the door names them.

**7 · The potential, at full population**

Fully populated, this crossing is an ability-genetics resource in which ability is measured rather than proxied, repeated rather than fixed, and read across both countries and ancestries on one instrument. It bounds how much of the genetics of attainment is ability as the test measures it, states which readings are identified and which wait on a design device, and produces a genetics of cognitive change measured on the same people over time at a size no clinic reaches.

Inside the system it changes what every other axis says: the bank's items carry a residual discrimination, the trait scales are read with ability partialled out, the guessing record can name what it measures, and the ability predictor has a phenotype to be judged against.

The boundary of the ambition is a choice, not a limit. An imported file contains clinically meaningful variants whether or not any phenotype is ever collected, so a system that reads it for traits it measures and nothing else has taken a position on findings it declines to read, and states that position in advance rather than discovering it when someone asks.

**8 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

Whether imputed common-variant coverage from consumer files saturates before it reaches the architecture beyond the largest effects, and how fast that saturation moves with ancestry. Whether staggered first exposure can be achieved in a system people join when they choose, or only approximated. Whether an outside anchor for selection exists for a global voluntary population at all. How much a national education band costs 4.1 against a years-of-schooling variable, and whether the ladders can be mapped. And whether the modal-versus-deviation split of a guess is stable across tie types, or itself a property of how well two people know each other.
