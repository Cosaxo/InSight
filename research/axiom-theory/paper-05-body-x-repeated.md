# Which changes are the body

*Paper 5 of the axiom series: occasion reports of the body crossed with every measurement a person repeats.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 0 carries the general argument and the level separation this paper lives inside; paper 0a the repetition schedules and the randomized order it needs.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because everything measured about a person is measured on a day the measurement ignores, a person's answers move from day to day, and without a record of what the body was doing on each day that movement cannot be read; any population that reports its body daily beside measurements it repeats, across an item bank with no ceiling and every other axis, for years, would read the movement per dimension rather than assert it.

**Abstract.** A measurement of a person is taken on an occasion, and the occasion is almost never recorded. So when a person's answers move between two days, nothing says whether the person changed, the day changed, or the measurement did. Between-person couplings of body to mind carry every difference between two people and are not the quantity of interest; the within-person coupling, what a bad night does to this person against their own usual, is a different quantity that need not agree with the between-person one in size or in sign, and it has been measured on narrow instruments, in small panels, over weeks. What has not existed is that measurement over an item bank with no ceiling, across every other axis on the same people, for years, at a scale where the discrepancy between the two levels is estimated per dimension. This paper describes such a population and argues that occasion reports of the body, sleep, energy and illness, joined where a person consents by a band folded on their device from a worn sensor, are the covariate that makes within-person change interpretable everywhere else in the series. It defines the state share of a repeated item as the fraction of its occasion-level latent variance the body predicts, and states what identifies it: a second indicator of the same dimension inside the contemporaneity window, an allocation the design buys. It states what becomes knowable and what each reading has to beat: the state share on the repeated core, predicted with error for the tail; the timescales a deliberately irregular schedule can resolve; a cross-lagged asymmetry between body and mind, in both directions, that is reported as an asymmetry and never as a cause; what follows a defined illness onset, against matched days of the same person, with regression to the mean as the artefact the curve must beat; who is body-sensitive, as a posterior published with its precision and with the person's own body variance; and how far a between-person finding travels into a person, per dimension. It names the condition that matters most: the days a person feels worst are the days they are least likely to report, and a day-level missing-not-at-random mechanism is not identified by any model, so every coupling is published as a sensitivity band over how much worse the unreported days were.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A questionnaire item is answered on a Tuesday. The Tuesday is not recorded, beyond a timestamp, and the answer is treated as a fact about the person. When the same item is answered differently a month later, there are three explanations and the record cannot distinguish them: the person changed, the day was different, or the measurement is noisy. Every claim about how people change rests on telling those apart, and almost none of the data anyone holds can.

The body is the largest part of what a day is. Sleep, illness, energy, pain, hunger and the monthly cycle move mood, attention, patience and appetite for risk, and they move them within a person from one day to the next. Between-person couplings of body to mind carry every difference between two people and are not the quantity of interest. The within-person coupling, what a bad night does to this person against their own usual, is a different quantity that need not agree with the between-person one in size or in sign. It has been measured on narrow instruments, in small panels, over weeks. What has not existed is that measurement over an item bank with no ceiling, across every other axis on the same people, for years, at a scale where the discrepancy between the two levels is estimated per dimension rather than asserted.

A population that reports its body every day beside items it repeats is that measurement, if the design earns it.

**2 · The two axes alone, and where each stops**

The occasion reports are the body axis: a daily self-report of sleep, energy and illness on a short ordinal scale, and, where a person consents to it, a small set of bands folded on their own device from a worn sensor, sleep duration and timing, resting heart rate, movement, one band a day. Each report names its reference period, and reports over different periods are never pooled: sleep refers to the completed night with its times; energy to the moment of the report; illness to the current day. The reports are modelled as ordinal with person-specific thresholds allowed to drift, so that a change in how a person uses the scale and a change in their state are separate terms rather than one. Alone, the reports are a diary of the body. They can describe a person's sleep and say how it varies, and they can say nothing about what it does. Their ceiling is consequence.

The repeated measures are every other axis on the days it repeats: state-worded items in the bank served on a schedule, trait-scale items re-served, a keyed test taken again, a day of sealed predictions about a friend. Alone, they show change without a cause. A person's answers move, and the record cannot say whether the movement is the person, the day or the noise. Their ceiling is explanation.

Each axis's ceiling is the other axis. The body gives change a cause to be tested against; the repeated measures give the body something to be a cause of.

**3 · The crossing, and the two channels in it**

Paper 0's section 5.2 sets the level: nothing here is a claim about how people differ, and everything is a claim about how a person moves around their own centre. Two parts of the mechanism are specific to this pair.

The body is a covariate for the occasion, and an occasion covariate is what makes within-person change interpretable. Given a person's own history of answers and their own history of body reports, the part of today's answer movement that tracks today's body is separable from the part that does not, and the residual is what remains to be explained by the person's development, by the world, or by response error. Without the covariate, all of it is one quantity. This is paper 0's claim that a fixed axis cannot see change and an occasion axis cannot tell change from noise, instantiated.

The body is reported through two channels and they are two instruments, neither of them the truth. A self-report of sleep is a perception: it tracks how the person felt about the night and it is coloured by the mood of the morning it is asked in, which is a shared occasion method factor between the report and the day's answers, so every perceived-channel coupling is an upper bound on the body's share. A band from a worn device is a second instrument, not the truth: what it reports is a model's estimate from movement and heart rate, it carries its own measurement model with its own bias term, and its error is state-dependent, a restless night the person experienced as wakefulness is scored as sleep, so the device's error is correlated with the perception it is being read against. Disagreement between the channels therefore decomposes into perception bias, device bias and true difference, and only the third is a reading. The device's algorithm version travels with every band, because a vendor's update is an instrument change of the kind paper 0's section 5.4 models. The design does not rank the two channels and discard one; it routes them, reading each against the outcomes it should govern, and the hypothesis it tests is that perceived sleep tracks the affective side of the mind's movement and measured sleep the cognitive and physiological side. Where only the perceived channel exists, every reading is a reading of perception, stated as such.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 The state share, defined, and what identifies it.* For a repeated item, the state share is the fraction of a person's occasion-level latent variance on that item that the body covariate predicts. It is identified only where the occasion carries a second indicator of the same dimension, or the item is re-served inside the contemporaneity window of paper 0's section 5.2. With one response per occasion, true state variance and response error are one quantity and no share is estimable; anchoring on the previous answer makes response error serially correlated, so autocorrelation alone will not separate them either. The within-window replicate is therefore an allocation the design buys in paper 0a's currency, and its cost is what the state share costs.

What is published is the body-attributable share beside the residual occasion variance, and the residual is named as unexplained occasion variance, never as noise: a fight, a deadline or a pay cheque is true state variance the body covariate does not touch. An item is a trait item only when its total occasion variance, body and residual together, is small against its between-person variance, and an item whose occasion variance is large and body-attributable is a state item that measures the day as much as the person. The sorting is a distribution per item rather than a label, because 4.5 makes the share a property of the person and the item together: an item that measures the day for some people and the person for others is a reading, not a failure to sort. Shares are ranked on shrunken posteriors, the ranking is published with its own uncertainty, and the largest shares are read knowing they are the noisiest.

The share is measured directly only on the repeated core of paper 0a's spine, where a person meets the same item many times. A bank with no ceiling does not give any person repeats of most of its items however large the population grows, because adding people adds items as fast as it adds answers; for the tail the share is predicted from the item's content and from the state share of the dimension it loads on, published as a prediction with its own error and never as a measurement.

*4.2 Timescales, and what a daily report can resolve.* The body's effects arrive and fade on timescales, and the repetition schedules of paper 0a are what resolve them: last night against last week, a single poor night against a run of them, an illness's onset against its tail. A regularly spaced daily series resolves oscillations no faster than two days. This design is not regularly spaced: paper 0a's schedules serve items at unequal lags by construction, and unequal spacing is what makes a continuous-time decay faster than the mean interval identifiable at all. So the bound is stated per dimension as a precision rather than as a wall, and the design earns that precision by varying the lag deliberately. What a daily report cannot do is separate a strong daily cycle from the between-day series when the report time varies, which is why the clock time of every report is recorded and modelled. And because a daily series cannot tell a fast process from noise, a randomized minority of person-days carries several reports, a burst, and that subsample is what tells the schedule which dimensions the daily rate is failing.

*4.3 Direction, as a cross-lagged asymmetry in both directions.* Whether a bad night lowers the answers or a low morning lowers the report of the night is not readable from the perceived channel alone, because the report and the answers share a morning. A band that recorded the night before the person woke closes the same-morning path from the morning's mood into the report of the night. That is not exogeneity. The previous day's state, drink, load and stress cause both the night and the following day's answers, and the loop runs in both directions across days. What the band buys is a within-person cross-lagged asymmetry: the band predicting today's answers conditional on the person's own prior-day answers and prior-day body, against the reverse, under an explicit assumption of no unmeasured time-varying confounder. It is reported as an asymmetry between two predictive directions, with the small-sample bias of conditioning on a lagged outcome beside a person effect stated, and never as a cause.

The reverse coupling is a reading of its own and not only a nuisance: what today's answers predict about tonight's measured sleep and tomorrow's resting heart rate, estimated with the same conditioning and reported beside the forward coupling. Because the day an item is served is a decision the policy makes, the exposure in this direction can be randomized where the first class of paper 0's section 6 allows it. The loop, answer today, night tonight, answer tomorrow, is then the object, and the two directions are published as a pair with their asymmetry, never as one arrow.

*4.4 What follows an illness, against the artefact that produces a curve on its own.* An illness is an event of paper 0's second class: the person did not choose it, but neither did the system, and its onset is confounded with sleep, stress and season, while its reporting is itself state-dependent. Onset is defined as the first day of a run crossing a stated threshold after a stated clear window, and the definition travels with every curve. The curve is estimated against matched non-onset days of the same person at the same calendar position, because a threshold crossing on a noisy report selects positive error, and a return to the person's centre is what that selection produces on its own. The pre-onset window is reported as a falsification test, since a drop that begins before onset is reading the prodrome or the selection. Episodes enter with a person-level effect and a severity term rather than as exchangeable draws. Under those conditions, with the onset time as the unit of analysis and an explicit assumption of no time-varying confounder affected by prior outcomes, it becomes knowable what a reported illness does to a person's answers over the following days and weeks, on which dimensions, and how far the return to their own centre is complete, against a baseline of the person's own hundred prior days that no clinic holds.

*4.5 Who is body-sensitive, as a posterior.* The state share of 4.1 is estimated within each person, so it varies across them. Some people's answers move with their body and some people's barely do. That sensitivity is a trait of the person that no one-shot instrument can hold, because it is a property of a series. It is a posterior, not a number: it is published as a distribution with its precision, and every crossing that consumes it consumes the full posterior rather than a point estimate. It is published jointly with the person's own within-person body variance, because a slope is estimated only over the range its predictor moved, and a person whose body barely varied has an uninformative sensitivity rather than a low one, the rule paper 2 applies to a target with no distinctive variance. Because precision rises with occasion count and occasion count is engagement, any group comparison or heritability on this phenotype states the reliability attained in each group and is not read where they differ. So bounded, sensitivity becomes a phenotype for every other crossing: whether it travels with the trait scales, whether it differs in the declared groups of paper 4, whether it is heritable under paper 3's conditions, and whether the people who know a person can see it, under paper 2's readings.

*4.6 The occasion covariate everywhere else.* Every within-person reading in the series inherits this one. Paper 1's sittings gain a covariate that says how unusual the person's body was on each test day, which adjusts a level and states how far a difference between two sittings could be the day; separating a trajectory from a state needs sittings in the dozens, which is a requirement the body covariate makes worth asking for rather than one it supplies. Paper 2's post-reveal dynamics gain a covariate that separates learning how one is seen from a bad week. Paper 4's variability signature gains the covariate it is partly about. The body is the axis the within-person half of the whole series stands on, and it is the only axis whose occasions are as frequent as the days.

*4.7 How far a between-person finding travels.* For every state dimension the between-person coupling and the population mean of the within-person couplings are estimated on the same people from the same reports, and their discrepancy is published per dimension with its sign and its interval. Whether a between-person body result travels into a person becomes a measurement, per dimension, rather than the premise section 1 borrows, and it is the frontier result paper 0's section 5.2 names.

**5 · The return path: what the crossing gives back to every other axis**

*The bank.* Every repeated item gains a body-attributable share beside its unexplained occasion variance, so the bank knows which of its items measure the day and which the person, and for whom. A state item is not a worse item. It is the right item for a within-person reading and the wrong one for a trait, and knowing which is which is a fact about the bank's own vocabulary.

*The trait scales.* Each scale gains two numbers that are not the same number. A state share: the fraction of its variance that sits within persons and moves. And an occasion-level reliability: the fraction of that movement that is true rather than response error, which needs the replicate 4.1 requires. A scale with a large state share and a high occasion reliability is a good state measure, not a failed trait one; the trait it names is then read from the person's centre across occasions, with the reliability of that centre stated separately from the reliability of any one administration.

*The keyed ability axis.* Paper 1's separation of cognitive state from cognitive trait gains the covariate that says how unusual each test day was, within the limit 4.6 states.

*Ties.* Whether a bad night changes how a person reads the people they know is a reading this crossing can pose, and it is answerable under three conditions. The same perceiver meets the same targets across many days, so the target's own movement is a term rather than the error. The perceiver's use of the scale on those days, extremity, latency, tendency toward the modal answer, is estimated beside their accuracy, because a bad night that flattens the scale lowers distinctive accuracy without touching knowledge. And the distinctive component's occasion reliability is stated, since it is a residual and the noisiest part of paper 2's split. No design has held sleep beside graded predictions about friends across days, and none should claim the reading without those three.

*The genome.* Body-sensitivity, as the posterior 4.5 publishes, is a phenotype, and so is the within-person variance of each state dimension. Their genetics, under paper 3's conditions and with the reliability attained stated per group, asks whether how much a person's mind moves with their body is itself heritable, which is a question about the genome axis's object that a one-shot phenotype cannot pose.

*The profile facts.* Body couplings read by age band and by country are a map of how the body's reach changes across a life and across places, and the anchors gain a within-person reading they have never had, over the items and dimensions that pass invariance across the groups compared.

*The design layer.* The state share tells paper 0a's policy which items to repeat and at what lag, the timescales of 4.2 set the schedules, the bursts of 4.2 are an allocation it makes, the replicate of 4.1 is a cost it prices, and the body report is the observation it prices lowest of all, for the reason section 6 gives first.

**6 · What would have to hold**

The days a person feels worst are the days they are least likely to report. This is the condition that matters most, and it is a design condition before it is a statistical one. If the body covariate is missing exactly on the days the body was worst, every coupling is estimated on the good days and understates or misreads the body's reach. So the body report is the cheapest observation the system asks for, priced in the attention units of paper 0: bounded below every other instrument, answerable without opening anything else, and retrospectively enterable the following day with a recall term of its own, which recovers the worst days directly rather than modelling them. It cannot be made cheaper than not answering, so a gap remains. The response propensity is modelled per occasion jointly with the report and the day's answers, and the leaving hazard is modelled beside it as the separate process it is. A day-level missing-not-at-random mechanism is identified by neither, so every coupling is published with a sensitivity band over an explicit non-ignorability parameter, how much worse the unreported days were, and the band, not the point estimate, is the reading.

The order of measurement is randomized within person across days under a recorded probability, so the order effect is an estimand of paper 0's first class rather than a constant. Body-first days supply the precedence 4.3 needs; body-last days measure what the priming cost. Within one morning the two channels share an occasion and a mood whatever the order, so every perceived-channel coupling is stated as an upper bound on the body's share, and the measured channel is what bounds it from the other side.

The channels are named on every number, with the band's algorithm version. A reading from the perceived channel is stated as perception; a reading from a measured band is stated as a device's estimate; and a coupling read across the two is stated as such.

Every within-person coupling carries calendar terms: day of week, photoperiod and season at the person's latitude, public holidays, and travel across time zones where the profile facts supply it. Weekend is a common cause of the body report, the measured band and the day's answers alike, and a coupling estimated without it is a coupling with the week inside it.

A short list of occasion covariates is collected beside the three reports, because each is a common cause of both channels: alcohol and caffeine the previous evening, medication taken, cycle day where a person supplies it, shift pattern and time-zone change. The cycle is a within-person rhythm of about a month and, unmodelled, is read as slow dynamics of the body dimension in 4.2. A person on rotating shifts has no fixed day boundary, so their occasions are indexed on their own wake times and the precedence of 4.3 is defined against those. Where a covariate is not supplied, the coupling states the share left unmodelled.

The body report is the only instrument served every day, so its reactivity is an estimand rather than an assumption: a randomized arm is served the reports on a reduced schedule, and the difference between arms is what daily reporting did to the thing reported.

The repetition schedules exist and are designed, with unequal lags, within-window replicates on a designed share of occasions, and bursts on a randomized minority of person-days, per 4.1 and 4.2.

Illness reporting is modelled as state-dependent, onset is defined, and the event readings of 4.4 carry the assumptions of paper 0's second class explicitly.

Band consent is separate and revocable, and it shapes the population 4.3 is read on: every measured-channel reading is stated for the consenting, wearing subpopulation with the consent model of paper 0's section 5.4 carried, and the direction result is not generalized past it without that model. Revocation stops future folds and states what becomes of estimates already fitted. A folded daily band of sleep timing and resting heart rate identifies a person on its own, so it is held under the custody rule rather than treated as coarse because it is one number a day; and a device worn by more than one person breaks the mapping from stream to person, which the fold detects or the band is not admitted.

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

Occasions per person in the dozens before a within-person coupling is a number rather than a coincidence, and in the hundreds before the lag structure of 4.2 is readable. The same items repeated on the same people, at unequal lags, with within-window replicates on a designed share of occasions. Body reports daily, including on the days nothing else is answered, and enterable the next day. Years, for seasons and for the illness curves of 4.4 to accumulate. A consenting population carrying measured bands, for 4.3's asymmetry. None is a reason to want less. Each is what a fully populated body axis looks like from the inside.

**8 · The potential, at full population**

Fully populated, this crossing is the first within-person map of the body's reach into the mind at population scale: for every repeated item and trait, how much of a person's movement the body accounts for and how much remains unexplained; on what timescales; as an asymmetry in both directions where a measured channel stands beside the perceived one; what an illness does over the days that follow, against the person's own matched days; who is sensitive to their body and who is not, as a posterior with a genetics; and how far a between-person finding travels into a person, per dimension. It sorts the vocabulary of what people say about themselves into states and traits by measurement rather than by belief, and it says for whom.

Inside the system it is the covariate under everything that changes. Paper 1 reads what reasoning is, paper 2 what a self-report is worth, paper 3 which of the things a person says covary with what they were born with, paper 4 the shape of a declared difference; this paper reads which of the ways a person changes are the body, and without it none of the others can read change at all.

**9 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

How wide the sensitivity band over the unreported days is once the report is the cheapest observation and enterable the next day, and whether the recall term recovers what the gap costs. Whether the perceived and measured channels disagree a little or a lot once device bias is in the model, and on which outcomes each is the better instrument. Whether body-sensitivity is one trait of a person or several, one per state dimension. Whether the state share of the bank's items is stable across cultures and ages or itself a moving quantity. How much daily self-monitoring changes the thing monitored. And whether the lag structures of 4.2 are shared across people or are themselves a way people differ.
