# What a person turns toward

*Paper 14 of the axiom series: interests as two instruments for one construct — what a person declares they like and what they attend to — and as the person-side missingness mechanism that mirrors the serving policy's; the negative space of what is muted; homophily against influence; and attention drift as the earliest sign that something has happened.*

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because every measurement a person supplies voluntarily is a measurement of what they turned toward, so interest is not one source among the others but the selection under all of them — the person's own missingness mechanism, as the observer's serving rule is the system's — and a programme that models the second and ignores the first has corrected half its selection; and because what a person declares they like and what they attend to are two readings of one taste whose disagreement is itself a reading, and what they turn away from is the costlier signal of the two.

**Abstract.** Interest is the least consolidated axis in this series and the most pervasive: what a person declares they favour, what they follow and mute, what they read deeper and return to, and a model of all of it. This paper gives it a theory in five facts. Interest is the person-side missingness mechanism: people answer what they turn toward, so every within-person reading in the series conditions on the person's attention as it conditions on the policy's serving, and the two propensities must be modelled together — a programme that enters the serving propensity and ignores the person's is half-corrected. Declared and revealed interest are two instruments for one construct in exactly the sense paper 5 requires and paper 9 lacked, and each carries its own confound: the declared channel carries presentation, the revealed channel carries the policy, since a person can attend only to what was served, so revealed interest is identified only on the stream the policy did not select. The negative space — what is muted, avoided, never returned to — is deliberate and costly where a follow is cheap, so it carries more per act and gives the axis a sign. Whether people who share interests tie or people who tie come to share interests is not identified from the record and is identified by a randomized offer, which paper 13 supplies as its third class. And attention moves before reports do: a change in what a person turns toward is often the first visible sign of an event no one has yet declared, which makes drift a leading indicator read under paper 13's discipline and not a diagnosis. What becomes knowable: the taste geometry, public on the declared side and computed on the device on the revealed side under paper 12's split; the person-side selection made explicit and its bias measured; homophily against influence; drift as an event marker with its lead time; the declared-revealed discrepancy as a presentation parameter; entry into paper 8's arenas explained; and interest's state against its trait. Two decisions are the owner's: whether attention and mutes may be pooled at all, and whether avoidance may be read, since what a person avoids can name what they fear.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A voluntary measurement is a measurement of what a person turned toward. They answered this item and not that one, followed this and muted that, read one thing to the end and left another. So every source in the series that the person supplies at their own choosing has interest under it as a selection, and the selection is not random: it correlates with the traits the sources measure, since a person turns toward what they care about, and what they care about is what the bank, the instruments and the ties are trying to read.

The series has modelled the other selection carefully. Paper 0a's serving rule is a missingness mechanism the system contributes, logged with its propensity so that what was asked is a known function of recorded state. What the person contributes — which of the served items they answer, which suggestions they take, what they attend to — has been treated as response and not as selection. It is both. Interest is the person's serving rule, and a within-person reading that enters the system's propensity and ignores the person's has corrected one of two selections and reported the result as clean.

That is the axis's first claim and its most consequential. The second is that interest, read as a source in its own right, is two instruments and a negative space, and the rest of the paper is what that structure makes knowable.

**2 · The axis alone, and its three channels**

*Declared interest is a self-report of taste.* Favourites picked from a catalogue, topics chosen, a stated liking. It is public where the person made it public, it is cheap to give, and it carries what any self-report carries: paper 9's presentation — a declared interest is partly what a person wants to be seen to like — and paper 8's response style.

*Revealed interest is behaviour.* What a person follows and mutes, reads deeper, returns to, spends attention on. It is not a report and cannot be presented, but it is confounded by something the declared channel is not: the person attended to what was served, so revealed interest is a joint product of taste and the policy, and a follow of what was never shown is not a follow of what was not liked. Under the custody split this axis carries, the attention record and any model of it stay on the device, which makes the revealed channel an immovable column of paper 12's kind.

*The negative space is a channel of its own.* A mute, an avoidance, a topic never returned to. A follow costs nothing and can be idle; a mute is an act against something that was there, so it is deliberate, and deliberate acts carry more per act than idle ones. Avoidance is also the channel that names what a person does not want near them, which is the most sensitive reading the axis has and one of the owner's forks in section 5.

*Alone, interest is a list.* Three channels of what a person likes and avoids, with no criterion for what liking means, no way to tell taste from presentation or from serving, and no reading of what the list is for. Its ceiling is the other axes: what a taste predicts, what it selects, and what it shares with whom.

**3 · Five facts**

*3.1 Interest is the person-side missingness mechanism, and it is modelled beside the policy's.* Each observation in the record was served under the system's propensity and taken under the person's. The second is estimable: from the declared and revealed channels, a person's propensity to engage with an item of a given kind is a quantity, and it enters every within-person reading beside the serving propensity. The bias of leaving it out is measurable — the same reading on items the person sought against the same reading on the stream the policy chose and the person did not, since the never-selected-on stream is where the person's selection is weakest — and the paper predicts the bias is largest on exactly the readings the series cares most about, because a person attends to the items about the traits they hold strongly, which are the items where their answer is most extreme.

*3.2 Two instruments for one construct, each with its confound.* Declared and revealed interest both measure taste, and paper 5's routing applies here in the form paper 9 found it did not apply to a metabolite and a mood — these are two readings of one thing, so their disagreement decomposes into the declared channel's presentation, the revealed channel's serving, and true difference, and only the third is a reading of the person. The revealed channel is identified only on the never-selected-on stream, where what was shown was not chosen for the person; the declared channel is identified only with paper 9's presentation factor entered, since a favourite is partly a self-portrait. Where both are clean, their agreement is taste; where they disagree, the disagreement is a person parameter, read as a discrepancy factor and never a difference score.

*3.3 The negative space has a sign and a cost.* Because a mute is an act against something present, the avoidance channel is denser in information per act than the follow channel and is read with its cost entered: a person who mutes much is not a person who dislikes much but a person who curates, and the rate is a trait beside the content. Avoidance also has the property that it cannot be idle, so it does not carry the follow channel's confound that an old follow is not a live interest.

*3.4 Homophily and influence are not separable in the record.* People who tie share interests. Whether shared interest caused the tie or the tie caused the sharing is the oldest confound on a relation graph, and nothing in an observational record separates them, because both predict the same joint distribution of ties and interests over time. What separates them is an offer the person did not choose: randomize whom a person is offered as a tie — paper 13's third class, read as offered-against-completed under a withholding arm — and the interest similarity before an accepted offer measures homophily while the convergence after it measures influence, on the same pairs. This is the one reading of the interest graph that is causal, and it exists only because the system can randomize an offer.

*3.5 Attention moves before reports.* A person whose life has changed changes what they turn toward before they say so — the topics they read, the times they engage, what they mute — and a shift in the revealed channel is often the first observable of an event nobody has declared. That makes drift a leading indicator and nothing more: it is read under paper 13's discipline, at event time against the person's own series, with anticipation and regression to the mean handled, and it is never a diagnosis, because a shift in attention has a dozen causes and the event is only one.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 The taste geometry, and where it runs.* A latent structure of preference over the declared channel — which favourites travel together, which topics co-occur — is a factor model over public picks with a rotation convention fixed with its version, and it is the cheapest cross-axis map in the series because both the picks and the bank's answers are public, so which opinions travel with which tastes is a join with no custody cost. The revealed channel's contribution runs where it lives: on the device, as paper 12's per-person join, contributing a budgeted term to the population geometry, so the full geometry is a public half joined to a device-side half and the join runs at the device with a release cost. Paper 10's classification lands here: the bank's items it calls taste — the ones with no earned relation — are the items whose variance the taste geometry explains, and the classification is checked against it.

*4.2 The person-side selection, made explicit and priced.* Every within-person reading in the series gains the person's propensity beside the policy's, and the bias of its omission is published per reading as the gap of 3.1. This is the paper's most consequential return path and it is not a new reading of anything — it is a correction to every reading that exists, and the paper predicts where the correction is largest.

*4.3 Homophily against influence.* Under 3.4's randomized offers: how much of the interest similarity on the tie graph was there before the tie and how much arrived after, per interest domain, and whether influence runs from the more-followed to the less. This is the interest graph read causally, and it is available only in a system that offers ties.

*4.4 Drift as an event marker, with its lead time.* Under 3.5 and paper 13: for each event family the series can date — a reported illness onset, a tie ending, a declared change — the lead time by which the revealed channel moved before the report, per person, and the false-alarm rate on event-free periods, which paper 6 demands of any detector. A lead time of days with a tolerable false-alarm rate makes the revealed channel the earliest instrument the series has; a lead time of zero or a false-alarm rate that swamps it makes drift a covariate and not a signal.

*4.5 The declared-revealed discrepancy as a presentation parameter.* Under 3.2: the person parameter of how far a person's declared taste sits from their revealed one, read as a discrepancy factor with its reliability, and crossed with paper 9's presentation factors and paper 8's reported-against-enacted to ask whether self-presentation is one trait across domains or several.

*4.6 Entry into the arena, explained.* Paper 8's central identification problem is that people enter contests they expect to do well in, and interest is most of why: which domain a person turns toward predicts which contests they enter. With the propensity of 3.1 measured, paper 8's entry selection gains a model with a known input rather than an unknown one, which does not repair the selection but names its largest term.

*4.7 Interest and the genome, under two doors.* Tastes have a genetics under paper 3's conditions, and paper 9's two doors apply in full: a taste is shaped by what a person was exposed to, and exposure is treatment, so a coupling between the genome and a favourite is compatible with shared biology and with a lifetime of exposure the genome drew, and is published as compatible with both.

*4.8 Interest's state against its trait.* What a person turns toward on a bad day differs from what they turn toward fresh, and paper 5's separation applies: a slow trait geometry across years, a fast state share across days, identified with paper 5's within-window replicate, and the state share is the part of attention drift that is the body rather than an event.

**5 · The return path, the two decisions, and the ranking**

*Every within-person reading* gains the person's propensity beside the policy's and a measured bias of its omission — the paper's largest return, and one to every axis at once.

*The bank* gains the taste geometry as the home of its taste items and a check on paper 10's classification.

*Ties* gain the only causal reading of their graph the series has, homophily against influence.

*Events* gain a leading indicator, read under paper 13's rules.

*The arena* gains the largest term of its entry selection named.

*The body* gains an attention-state share to read beside its own channels.

*The genome* gains the tastes as phenotypes, under two doors.

*The learned representation* gains the interest model as one more device-side contribution, and the warning that a taste predicted from the anchors is a stereotype, per paper 11's anchor share.

*The two decisions the paper does not make.* Whether the revealed channel — attention and mutes — may be pooled into a population geometry at all, or only ever computed on the device with a budgeted term leaving: the first gets the full taste geometry and pools the record of what people attend to, which is the record most people would least expect to be read; the second keeps it device-side and forgoes the population's revealed half, leaving the geometry on declared picks alone. And whether avoidance may be read, since what a person mutes can name what they fear, what they are recovering from, or whom they are avoiding — the most informative channel in the axis and the one whose reading is a disclosure of the kind the rest of the series asks before drawing. The paper states both and decides neither.

*Ranked by what they make knowable.* First, the person-side selection made explicit, because it corrects every reading in the series and its omission has been silent. Second, homophily against influence, because it is the tie graph's one causal reading. Third, drift as a leading indicator, if its false-alarm rate allows. The taste geometry is the cheapest and the least surprising, and is ranked accordingly.

**6 · What would have to hold**

*The person's propensity is estimated and entered beside the policy's in every within-person reading*, and the bias of omission is published per reading.

*Revealed interest is identified only on the never-selected-on stream*, since a person attends to what was served, and a revealed-interest reading on the adaptive stream is a reading of the policy.

*Declared interest carries paper 9's presentation factor and paper 8's response style*, entered before any taste is read from it.

*The two channels are routed as two instruments for one construct*, with their disagreement decomposed and the true-difference term the only reading, and no raw difference published.

*Avoidance is read with its rate entered as a trait*, so a curator is not read as a hater, and it is read at all only under the owner's fork.

*Homophily and influence are separated only under randomized offers*, per paper 13's third class, and any observational similarity on the graph is published as compatible with both.

*Drift is read under paper 13's discipline* — event time, matched days, anticipation, a false-alarm rate on event-free periods — and is never a diagnosis.

*The custody split holds*: declared picks and follows are public where the person made them so; attention, mutes and the interest model are device-side, contributing to any population quantity only as a budgeted term per paper 12, and pooled only under the owner's fork.

*The taste geometry has a rotation convention fixed with its version*, and its device-side half's adequacy is measured as paper 12 measures a summary's.

*Paper 5's within-window replicate identifies the state share*, and paper 3's conditions and paper 9's two doors govern any genome reading.

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

*Dozens of declared picks per person* for a per-person position in the taste geometry with usable reliability, since a factor score from a handful of picks is a guess.

*The never-selected-on stream carrying enough revealed acts* — follows, depth, returns — to identify the revealed channel apart from the policy; at a few attention acts a day, this is a bounded share of paper 0a's stream and is priced as one.

*Randomized tie offers at a rate that yields hundreds of accepted offers per interest domain*, for homophily against influence with an interval.

*Dated events from other axes* — onsets, endings, declarations — with the revealed series dense around them, for the lead-time reading, and event-free periods for its false-alarm rate.

*A device-side computation of the interest model and its budgeted term*, per paper 12.

*Within-window replicates of attention*, per paper 5, for the state share.

*Presentation and response-style factors from papers 9 and 8*, estimated on the same people.

None is a reason to want less. Each is what a fully populated interests axis looks like from the inside.

**8 · What would decide it**

*How large is the bias of ignoring the person's selection?* One within-person reading the series already publishes — a state share, a cross-lagged asymmetry — computed with the serving propensity alone and again with the person's propensity beside it, on the same people, and the gap reported with its interval. A gap near zero means interest selection is benign and 3.1's claim is small; a gap that reorders the reading means every within-person result in the series carries a correction it has not made. This precedes everything.

*Does attention move before reports, and how often does it move without one?* For one dated event family, the lead time of the revealed channel's shift against the report, and the shift rate on event-free periods, with a pre-registered false-alarm ceiling. Days of lead at a tolerable ceiling makes drift the earliest instrument; a rate that swamps it makes drift a covariate.

*Do the two channels agree once each is cleaned?* The declared channel with presentation entered against the revealed channel on the never-selected-on stream: their agreement is taste, their residual disagreement is the discrepancy factor's variance, and a disagreement that vanishes under cleaning says presentation and serving were the whole gap.

**9 · The potential, priced with each score's own condition**

A ten is a dataset after which a field's standard design changes; a five answers questions the field already had. Every score is conditional on the owner's two forks, since the revealed channel is where the value is.

*The person-side selection made explicit.* Seven, conditional on the first experiment showing a gap that matters, because a voluntary-measurement programme that models both selections is a design no such programme runs; three if the gap is small, since then the correction is a footnote.

*Homophily against influence.* Seven, conditional on randomized offers being permitted and reaching hundreds of accepted pairs per domain, because it is a causal reading of a confound that observational graphs cannot separate; two without the offers.

*Drift as a leading indicator.* Six, conditional on the lead time and the false-alarm ceiling, and on the revealed channel being pooled at all; nothing where it stays device-side and unpooled, since a per-person drift signal read only by the person is a product and not a dataset.

*The taste geometry.* Four, because a preference structure over public picks answers a question the field already has, and rises only with the device-side half the owner's fork decides.

The value concentrates in the first two, and both are decided by experiments that need only the record and the offers.

**10 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

How large the bias of ignoring the person's selection actually is, which decides whether every within-person reading in the series carries a correction. Whether declared and revealed taste agree once each is cleaned, or whether a person's stated favourites and their attention are two different things. How much of the tie graph's interest similarity is homophily and how much influence, per domain, and which direction influence runs. Whether attention drift leads reports by days or by nothing, and at what false-alarm rate. Whether avoidance is a denser channel than following, as the paper argues from its cost, or merely a rarer one. Whether interest has a state share at all, or is slow only. And what a person would say about their attention record being read — which is the first of the owner's forks, and the one that decides whether the revealed half of this axis exists as a population reading or only as a private one.
