# What a person turns toward

*Paper 14 of the axiom series: interests as the content of a propensity three papers already model per occasion — consolidated into an axis, split into the part recorded state identifies and the part that stays a band — and crossed with ties, where a randomized offer separates who tie because they are alike from who become alike because they tied.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 5 supplies the response propensity modelled per occasion, the band, and the routing of two instruments; paper 0a the serving log, the survival term as the place interest already enters, and the spine the uniform stream is drawn from; paper 8 the offer as an instrument with exclusion and monotonicity; paper 13 the exposure mapping and the prodrome-against-anticipation distinction this paper must draw; paper 9 the presentation factors the declared channel carries; paper 12 the custody costs of any join the revealed channel enters; paper 6 the three numbers any detector owes.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because the propensity with which a person supplies a voluntary measurement has content — it is what they turn toward — and treating that content as an axis rather than as a per-occasion nuisance is what lets the part of it recorded state identifies be separated from the part no model does; and because whether people who are alike become tied or people who are tied become alike is not separable in any record and is separable by an offer the person did not choose, which a system that offers ties can randomize.

**Abstract.** Interest is the least consolidated axis in this series: what a person declares they favour, what they follow and mute, what they read to the end and return to. An earlier draft of this paper claimed the series had treated the person's selection as response and not as selection, and that the person's propensity was estimable beside the policy's; both were false against the series' own papers, which model the response propensity per occasion, band the part that depends on the unobserved state, and make interest the survival term's content. What this paper adds is smaller and real. It names interest as the *content* of that propensity and consolidates it into an axis with a structure of its own, splitting it into a component recorded pre-response state identifies — the declared and revealed channels as covariates of engagement — and a component that depends on the latent the item measures, which is missing-not-at-random through the trait and stays paper 5's band, since entering it would condition on the outcome. It states that the earlier draft's mechanism, selection on how strongly a trait is held, is a between-person selection that the person effect absorbs and that biases no within-person reading; within-person bias runs through today's state and is already banded. Its cross-axis reading is with ties: whether people who share interests tie or people who tie come to share interests is not separable in a record, and a randomized offer separates it — read as an intention-to-treat on the offer and a complier effect under an exclusion restriction the paper states and doubts, two-sided where a direction is claimed, under an exposure mapping on the graph. Declared and revealed interest are not assumed to be one construct; a discriminant condition decides, and where it fails each is routed against the outcomes it governs. The serving mechanism is named per sub-channel — an item feed with its uniform stream, a suggestion slate with a withholding arm, unprompted seeking with no arm — because a follow is not served by an item policy and the uniform stream carries no follow opportunities. Attention drift is a prodrome, separated from paper 13's anticipation, read on families whose onset is unpredictable, with a denominator ascertained independently of reporting. Avoidance is a timestamped act with a live cost, or it is an unobserved offer. The custody split is a design assumption with its conclusions marked contingent, and where the attention record lives is the fork.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A voluntary measurement is supplied with a propensity, and the series models it. Paper 5 fits the response propensity per occasion jointly with the report; paper 6 does the same for wear; paper 0a's survival term is where a person's interest enters the design without becoming its goal; paper 8 repairs entry to an intention-to-treat and a complier effect. An earlier draft of this paper claimed that work did not exist, and it does.

What does not exist is a theory of the propensity's *content*. The per-occasion model treats it as a nuisance to be entered or banded; it does not ask what the person was turning toward, whether that has a structure across items and across time, and what the structure predicts elsewhere. Interest is that content. Consolidating it into an axis does two things the per-occasion model cannot: it separates the part of the propensity that recorded pre-response state identifies from the part that only the unobserved latent explains, and it gives the first part a geometry that can be crossed. The second part stays where papers 5 and 6 put it, in a band, because a propensity that is a function of the outcome's own latent is not a correction to enter but a collider to avoid.

The axis's second contribution is its crossing with ties, which is a reading and not a correction, and the paper ranks it first for that reason.

**2 · Two axes alone, and where each stops**

*Interest alone is a list with three channels.* Declared: favourites picked from a catalogue, topics chosen, a stated liking — a self-report, cheap to give, carrying paper 9's presentation and paper 8's response style. Revealed: acts with a timestamp — a follow, a mute, an item read past a stated fraction, a return to an item within a stated window — each operationalized as an act with its threshold and window travelling with every reading, and each excluding what is a device-clock artefact rather than an act, since a slow reader is not a deep one. Negative: a mute or a skip with a timestamp and a live cost; a topic never returned to is not an act of avoidance but an unobserved offer, and an earlier draft's claim that avoidance cannot be idle is withdrawn — a standing mute set once persists without renewal exactly as an old follow does. Alone, the list has no criterion for what liking means and no way to tell taste from presentation or from what was offered. Its ceiling is the other axes.

*Ties alone are a graph that does not know why.* A tie is a fact; the graph of ties carries the strongest regularity a social record has — that tied people are alike — and cannot say which way it runs. A record of ties and interests over time is equally consistent with alike-then-tied and tied-then-alike, because both predict the same joint distribution, so the graph's most obvious pattern is its least readable. Its ceiling is a confound no observation resolves.

*The crossing's mechanism is an offer the person did not choose.* A system that offers ties can randomize the offer, and a randomized offer is what neither axis has: variation in who is tied that did not come from who was alike. Under it the similarity before an offer and the convergence after it are separable in a way the record alone never makes them, subject to the restrictions section 3.4 states.

**3 · Five facts**

*3.1 The propensity has two parts, and only one is entered.* The propensity to supply an observation depends on recorded pre-response state — what the person has declared, what they have followed, the time of day, their tenure — and on the latent the item measures, since a person turns toward what they hold strongly. The first part is missing-at-random given the record and is estimable from it; the declared and revealed channels are its covariates, and entering them is a correction. The second is missing-not-at-random through the trait, is identified by no model, and is paper 5's band; entering an estimate of it conditions on the outcome. The earlier draft's "estimable" is withdrawn as a property of the whole and kept for the first part. And the earlier draft's mechanism — selection on trait strength — is a between-person selection: within a person the trait is fixed, the person effect absorbs it, and it biases no within-person estimand. Within-person bias runs through today's state, which is paper 5's worst-days mechanism and is already banded there; so the correction this paper adds is to between-person couplings, and it says so.

*3.2 The bias of omission is bounded, not measured, without exogenous variation in response cost.* An earlier draft proposed reading the bias from a contrast between items the person sought and the uniform stream. That contrast estimates the policy's contribution, not the person's: on the uniform stream the policy did not choose, but the person still chose whether to answer, and their selection is if anything stronger there, since the items were not chosen to interest them. The bias of omitting the person's propensity is measured only under a design that varies the person's response cost exogenously — a randomized incentive, or a forced-response validation subsample used as an instrument — and without one it is published as a bound.

*3.3 Declared and revealed are not assumed one construct.* Paper 5's routing of two instruments for one construct applies only where both measure one thing, which paper 9 found its morphology and perception did not. Here it is an open question the paper does not prejudge: a person attends to what they dislike — to monitor a threat, from obligation, from outrage — and the paper's own negative channel has people engaging with content long enough to mute it, which is attention that is the negation of taste. So a discriminant condition is stated: a domain in which the declared and revealed channels load with opposite sign on an external criterion falsifies the one-construct model. Where it holds, the three-way decomposition — presentation in the declared, the serving mechanism in the revealed, true difference — applies and the discrepancy is a factor with its reliability. Where it fails, each channel is routed against the outcomes it should govern, as paper 5 routes perceived and measured sleep, and no shared construct is claimed.

*3.4 Homophily and influence, as an instrument with its restrictions.* An offer is randomized; acceptance is chosen; so the design is paper 8's and paper 7's, not a clean contrast. The estimand is an intention-to-treat on the offer, and a complier effect under two restrictions stated per family: that the offer moves the outcome only through the tie formed, which is doubtful because being offered and declining is itself an event that changes what the person believes about the system, and the paper names that as the violation it is; and monotonicity, that no one ties because they were not offered. Similarity before an offer is homophily on the offered set; convergence after an accepted offer is not influence until the exclusion restriction is argued, because people accept offers of those they were already becoming like and convergence is then continued selection. A directional claim — influence running from the more-followed to the less — needs randomization in both directions with the asymmetry as the estimand, and one-sided offers do not identify it. And an offer to one person changes the other's record, so the design lives on a relation graph under an exposure mapping stated per paper 13, not under an assumption of no interference.

*3.5 The serving mechanism is named per sub-channel.* Revealed interest on an item feed is confounded by the item policy, and the uniform stream — drawn from paper 0a's spine — is where that confound is absent. A follow or a mute of a person or a topic is not served by the item policy at all; it comes from a suggestion slate, which is confounded by the suggestion mechanism and is cleaned by a withholding arm on the slate, as paper 13 withholds model-driven pairing. Unprompted seeking — search, a topic the person went to — has no arm and is read as observational. The earlier draft's blanket claim that the revealed channel is identified only on the never-selected-on stream is replaced by three identifications, one per mechanism, and the uniform stream carries no follow opportunities, so it identifies the item sub-channel alone.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 Homophily against influence, on the offered set.* Under 3.4: the intention-to-treat of a randomized offer on subsequent interest similarity, per interest domain, and the complier effect where the exclusion restriction is argued for that family; the pre-offer similarity on the offered set as homophily; and the direction only under two-sided randomization. This is the paper's cross-axis reading and the one thing here neither axis makes alone.

*4.2 The taste geometry, in the shape the fork allows.* A factor model over declared picks, on a rotation convention fixed with its version — estimated with the propensity of 3.1 entered and its sensitivity to that propensity published, since which picks a person supplies is itself an interest act and an earlier draft fitted the geometry on the selected sample its own thesis warned about. The revealed channel joins it in one of two shapes, stated rather than wished: the device projects its revealed record onto the public loadings and contributes a score, which is cheap and adds no dimension; or the population factors are re-estimated over pooled revealed variables, which yields one geometry and needs the record the owner's fork withholds. A rotation convention fixes indeterminacy within one solved space and does not make two separately estimated halves one geometry. And the public-to-public join with the bank is not free: paper 12's sting applies, since linking a named person's public picks to their public answers is the identifying operation, budgeted as one.

*4.3 Drift as a prodrome, on families whose onset is unpredictable.* A change in what a person turns toward before an event is reported. This is not paper 13's anticipation — moving because one knows an event is coming — and paper 13's remedy, reading against a clean pre-segment, would delete the estimand here, because the pre-event movement is the signal. So drift is read as a prodrome on event families whose onset the person could not anticipate, with families that can be anticipated excluded, and with paper 6's three numbers in full: an alert rate per person-week, the specificity that yields a tolerable predictive value at the family's event rate, and the named person who acts on a signal about an event nobody has declared. The denominator is not "periods with no reported event," which is populated with unreported true events that correlate with the states causing drift; it is ascertained independently — a follow-back sample, or a family with a system-controlled onset.

*4.4 The declared-revealed discrepancy, where one construct holds.* Under 3.3's condition: a discrepancy factor with its reliability, crossed with paper 9's presentation factors and paper 8's reported-against-enacted to ask whether self-presentation is one trait or several. Where the condition fails, no discrepancy is published.

*4.5 The between-person correction, with what it produces.* Entering 3.1's first part into between-person couplings changes some of them, and the paper names what that yields as a reading: a coupling whose sign or ordering moves under the correction is a coupling that was partly interest selection, and the list of such couplings is a finding about which readings the series has published on the selected. Within-person readings are unchanged, per 3.1.

*4.6 Interest and entry, as one endogenous term.* Paper 8's entry mechanism is expected outcome, and paper 8 has already repaired entry to an intention-to-treat and a complier effect; interest is one term in the complier population's description, measured from the same voluntary behaviour that produces entry and therefore endogenous, and the earlier draft's "largest term" is withdrawn.

*4.7 Interest's state against its trait.* A slow geometry across years and a fast share across days, with paper 5's identification carried honestly: the state share needs a second indicator of the attention dimension inside the window — a declared interest asked the same day, or a parallel surface — since two attention acts in a window are two observations of a stream and not two indicators of an occasion latent; and the complement of the body's share is body-unexplained occasion variance of every kind plus response error, not an event signal.

**5 · The return path, the decisions, and the ranking**

*Ties* gain the one causal reading their graph admits.

*The bank* gains the taste geometry as the home of the items paper 10 calls taste, and the re-identification cost of that join stated.

*Events* gain a prodrome channel, separated from anticipation and read on unpredictable-onset families.

*Every between-person coupling* gains the first part of the propensity as a covariate and a list of which couplings moved.

*The arena* gains interest as one named endogenous term in its complier population.

*The body* gains an attention state share under paper 5's identification, or none.

*The genome* gains tastes as phenotypes under paper 3's conditions and paper 9's two doors, published as compatible with both — which is a condition, not a reading, and is not ranked.

*The custody split, as an assumption and a fork.* Declared picks and follows public, attention and mutes device-side: that is a design assumption this paper states and marks its conclusions contingent on, not a precondition of the theory. Where the attention record lives is the fork, derived from what each placement makes knowable — device-side with a projected score keeps 4.2's cheap shape and forgoes the pooled geometry and the population half of 4.3's prodrome; pooled keeps both and pools the record of what people turn toward, under paper 12's costs in full. And whether avoidance is read is a second fork, since a timestamped mute can name what a person fears or whom they avoid. The paper states both and decides neither.

*Ranked by what they make knowable, and priced as the same set.* First, homophily against influence, the reading. Second, the prodrome, if its denominator can be ascertained. Third, the taste geometry in whichever shape the fork allows. The between-person correction is a discipline with a by-product and is ranked by the by-product; the state share is ranked last, since its identification may not exist.

**6 · What would have to hold**

*The propensity's first part is entered into between-person couplings from recorded pre-response state; its second part stays a band; no within-person reading is corrected by it.*

*The bias of omission is bounded unless response cost is varied exogenously*, and measured only then.

*The one-construct model is tested by its discriminant condition before any discrepancy is published*, and the channels are routed apart where it fails.

*The offer design states exclusion and monotonicity per family, publishes the intention-to-treat and the complier effect, names the offered-and-declined channel as the exclusion violation, randomizes two-sided for any direction, and carries an exposure mapping.*

*The serving mechanism is named per sub-channel* — feed, slate, unprompted — with the identification each admits.

*Every revealed act is operationalized with a threshold and a window*, device-clock artefacts excluded, and the definitions travel with every reading.

*Avoidance is a timestamped act with a live cost*; non-return is an unobserved offer; the density-per-act claim is a hypothesis with its quantity defined — surprisal per act, or information per act for a named parameter — and not a fact.

*The taste geometry is estimated with the propensity entered*, on a rotation convention fixed with its version, in one of two named shapes, with paper 12's costs carried for the revealed half and the re-identification cost charged for the public join.

*Drift is read as a prodrome on unpredictable-onset families*, with paper 6's three numbers and an independently ascertained denominator.

*Paper 5's state share is claimed only with a second indicator of the attention dimension in the window.*

*The custody split is an assumption with its conclusions marked contingent, and the record's placement is the fork.*

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

*Picks per person derived from a target factor-score reliability*: with an average communality of a few tenths across the geometry's items, on the order of thirty to fifty picks per person for a score reliability near seven tenths, more where the communalities are lower.

*Offers, not accepted offers*: an intention-to-treat on a similarity outcome with a small standardized effect needs offers in the low thousands per domain, and a complier effect inflates that by the inverse square of the compliance rate — at a compliance of one in five, tens of thousands of offers per domain — which is the denominator an earlier draft priced wrongly.

*Attention acts on the uniform stream*, which is a fraction of a fraction: the item sub-channel is identified only there, so the acts that count are those on the spine's items, and their share of the daily budget is priced as such.

*A withholding arm on the suggestion slate*, sized for its own intention-to-treat.

*A discriminant criterion per domain* for the one-construct test.

*Unpredictable-onset event families with an independently ascertained denominator*, and a named responder.

*A randomized incentive or a forced-response validation subsample*, without which the bias of omission is a bound.

*A second indicator of the attention dimension inside the window*, for any state share.

None is a reason to want less. Each is what a fully populated interests axis looks like from the inside.

**8 · What would decide it**

*Is the tie graph's similarity homophily or influence?* Randomized offers in one domain, two-sided, at the counts of section 7, with the exposure mapping stated: the intention-to-treat on similarity with its interval, the complier effect where exclusion is argued, and the pre-offer similarity on the offered set. An intention-to-treat near zero with high pre-offer similarity is homophily; a substantial intention-to-treat is influence on the offered, subject to the restriction.

*Are declared and revealed one construct?* The discriminant condition in three domains against an external criterion from another axis: opposite-sign loadings in any domain falsify the one-construct model for that domain and route the channels apart.

*Does the bias of omission exist, against a criterion?* A randomized incentive arm varying response cost, with one between-person coupling computed under the propensity's first part and without it, against an equivalence bound — the arm supplies the criterion an earlier draft's model-against-model comparison lacked.

**9 · The potential, priced with each score's own condition, on the set section 5 ranked**

A ten is a dataset after which a field's standard design changes; a five answers questions the field already had. Every score is conditional on the custody fork, since the revealed channel is where most of the value sits.

*Homophily against influence.* Seven, conditional on two-sided randomized offers at section 7's counts and an exposure mapping, because a relation graph with randomized ties read against pre-offer similarity does not exist at any size; two without the offers, since the record alone is symmetric.

*The prodrome.* Five, conditional on unpredictable-onset families, an independently ascertained denominator, and a named responder; nothing where the denominator is reported events, since the rate is then uninterpretable.

*The taste geometry.* Four in the projected-score shape, five in the pooled shape the fork may withhold, with the propensity entered and the join's re-identification cost charged.

*The between-person correction.* Three as a discipline, and priced by the list of couplings it moves, which is its only reading.

*The state share.* Priced only where its second indicator exists.

**10 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

Whether the tie graph's similarity is homophily or influence, per domain, and whether the exclusion restriction survives for any offer family once a declined offer is itself an event. Whether declared and revealed taste are one construct or two, domain by domain. Whether the bias of omitting the propensity's first part moves any between-person coupling, against an incentive-arm criterion. Whether attention drift is a prodrome with a usable lead on families whose onset cannot be anticipated, at a tolerable false-alarm rate on an honest denominator. Whether a mute carries more per act than a follow, once the quantity is defined. Whether the attention dimension admits a second indicator in a window at all, on which the state share depends. And where the attention record may live — which is the fork under most of the value here, and the one no data answers.
