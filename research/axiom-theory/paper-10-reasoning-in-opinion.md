# Reasoning in the clothes of opinion

*Paper 10 of the axiom series: a key that lives in the relation between two opinions rather than in either, the bind that makes such a key earned rather than written, and what separates once the same ability is measured under a test and under none.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 1 supplies the keyed test, the residual rule this paper obeys, and the repeated sittings it needs; paper 0a the serving policy, the never-selected-on streams, the repetition schedule and the budget every allocation here is priced in; paper 3 the response-style factor removed first; paper 5 the occasion covariate and the band; paper 2 the reveal as a randomizable event and its unbundling; paper 4 the declaration conditions any group contrast carries; paper 8 the discipline for a contrast without a level.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because a keyed measurement of reasoning carries its situation by construction — the person knows a right answer exists and that someone will check — while two opinions with no right answer to either can stand in a relation that has one, so that a bank of opinions authored to carry such relations measures reasoning with the situation removed; and because the relation that measures best is the one most people violate, which is also the one most likely to have been read another way, so the key cannot be written down and must be earned from the answers it is meant to score.

**Abstract.** A keyed reasoning test measures ability in a situation of its own making, and paper 1 crossed it with an unkeyed opinion bank while stating that an ability tilt gives no opinion a right answer. This paper takes the next step, which is that the key can live in the *relation* between two items: one entails the other given a premise, two positions exclude each other at a stated scope, a likelihood comparison bounds another, one proposition in two frames should draw one answer. A person's two answers are scored on the relation, and a bank authored to carry such relations — not one that merely grew deep — is a reasoning instrument with the test situation removed, so the same ability is measured under a test and under none. The paper's centre is a bind the obvious design misses. A relation is informative in proportion to how many people violate it and defended in proportion to how few could have read it another way, and those are one quantity seen from two sides; the most transparent relations also cue recall best, so they have the narrowest windows. A written defence therefore cannot make a key; a key is earned, by violations that track an independent criterion of reasoning after the person's response style and profile are removed, and by violators who cannot supply a reinterpretation when asked — and a relation that fails either is retired. Even an earned key is common to everyone who meets it, so a key error shifts every rate alike and no level in this paper is readable, only contrasts. The scoring is a constraint region on the joint response space that binds only for some positions, so the binding rate is published beside the violation rate. From there: whether tested and wild reasoning share a construct, read as a residual correlation with the confounded branch named; the situation contrast, identified only by randomizing disclosure, which is also the arm that hosts every cover-breaking probe and measures the cost of the honest consent shape; motivated reasoning as a person-by-topic phenotype, with difficulty from neutral analogues of the same form and the stake from an item that enters no relation, and with topic-specific defence failure named as the observable it cannot be told from; framing by design with frame order randomized; social influence with rational updating separated from conformity by re-asking the first item sealed; and the counts, the budget share and the consent shapes stated as the enumerated things they are.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A keyed measurement of reasoning carries its situation by construction. The person knows a right answer exists and that someone will check, so what the score holds is ability under that knowledge: the effort summoned for it, the state of the day it was taken, and whatever a test does to that person. Paper 1 measured that construct well and named those terms as conditions. No repetition of the same kind of measurement removes them, because they are part of what the kind measures.

The reasoning that decides what a person believes is done without that knowledge — holding two positions that cannot both be true, revising one when another is stated, answering a proposition differently by how it is put. It is not scored, because nothing in the ordinary run of opinion supplies a key.

The key can be supplied without a right answer to any item. Two opinions can stand in a relation that has one, and a person who answers both, days apart, as opinions, has been scored on the relation without being tested on either item. That is the instrument. What the rest of the paper establishes is that such a key cannot simply be written, that it must be earned from the answers, and what an earned key does and does not license.

**2 · The two axes alone, and where each stops**

*The keyed test alone cannot separate the ability from the sitting.* Its reliability and repeatability are real, and everything paper 1 builds on it stands. Its ceiling is that the situation is inside the number, and only a second measurement of the same ability under a different situation can say how much.

*The bank alone has no key, and depth does not supply one.* An earlier draft said a bank at sufficient depth already contains relations. It does not: two items that happen to be related are related by accident, unscored, undefended and unscheduled. The relations this paper needs are authored — each with its premise, its scope, its response format and its lag window — and a bank that was only collected has none of them. What the bank has is the capacity to carry them at any number, and what paper 1 gave it, a residual discrimination per item, which says which items tilt with ability and not whether any answer was reasoned.

*Each axis's ceiling is the other.* The test gives the wild instrument the criterion that decides whether its violations are reasoning; the wild instrument gives the test the second measurement it cannot take of itself.

**3 · The relation as key, and the bind that makes it earned**

*3.1 Four relation types, each with a response format and a premise set.* Entailment is a three-place relation: an answer to one item commits a person to an answer on another only through a bridging premise, and for opinions that premise is itself an opinion the person may reject. So the premise is an item, and entailment is scored as a triple, conditional on the person's own endorsement of the bridge — coherence is relative to each person's premise set and never to the designer's. Exclusion holds only at a scope: a person who endorses a rule and its exception, or a position in one context and its negation in another, is more articulated than the item and not incoherent, so an exclusion item carries an explicit scope qualifier in its wording and is otherwise not a key. A bound — a conjunction is no more probable than either conjunct — is a constraint on likelihood judgements and is expressible only on items whose response is an explicit likelihood comparison, which a bank of option choices does not carry unless it is authored to; a part-whole relation is a different key with a different defence and is not bundled with it. Equivalence — one proposition in two frames — is the weakest of the four, not the strongest, because a frame carries pragmatic information by the fact of having been chosen, and a person who answers to that information is reading, not failing. No ranking of the four by ease of defence survives, and the earlier draft's is withdrawn.

*3.2 The bind: informative and defended are one quantity seen twice.* A relation that nobody could read another way is one nearly everyone answers coherently, so it carries almost no between-person variance and measures almost nothing. A relation that many people violate is, by that fact, evidence that a reading was available under which the two answers cohere — which is evidence its defence fails. Informative relations are presumptively undefended and defended relations presumptively uninformative, and the same transparency that defends a relation cues recall of the first answer when the second is served, so the best-defended relations have the narrowest windows. This is the paper's central structural fact, and it means a key cannot be written. A written defence is a claim by the party that gains from the relation surviving, judged by nobody, overturned by nothing.

*A key is earned, by two tests, and retired by either.* First, its violations must track an independent criterion of reasoning — the keyed score of paper 1, residualised on the profile facts and shared dispositions as that paper requires, after the person's response style is removed — because a violation rate that does not move with reasoning is a rate of something else. Second, violators asked afterwards must fail to supply a reinterpretation under which their two answers cohere, at a rate below a pre-registered bound, because a relation whose violators can say what they meant is one the designer misread. A relation carries both results as its defence; a relation that fails either is retired; and the reinterpretation set every defence must address includes scope, ceteris paribus, the connective read as a sequence, the likelihood read as a frequency or a degree of belief, and the frame read as carrying information. The set is stated in advance, the writing is done independently twice with agreement reported, and none of that replaces the two tests.

*3.3 Only contrasts are readable, because the key is common.* Whatever the key gets wrong — a bridge nobody holds, a scope the wording did not fix, a bound on a format that was not a likelihood — is wrong for everyone who meets the relation, shifts every person's rate by the same amount, and is invisible in every between-person contrast. So no level in this paper is a reading. The population's coherence rate, the mean of the situation contrast, the claim that the bank is a free test because a correlation is near one: each depends on the key's level being right, which nothing inside the data can check. Contrasts between people, between topics within a person, between arms, and between days survive a common key error and are what the paper publishes.

*3.4 The scoring is a region, and it binds only for some positions.* A relation is a constraint region on the joint response space of its items; violation is signed distance outside the region; the pair is modelled with an ordinal measurement model carrying person-specific thresholds, as paper 5 models its ordinal reports, and never as a raw threshold on a difference of options. Response style is removed first, from balanced and reverse-keyed items as paper 3 removes it, because an acquiescent responder agrees with both sides of an exclusion by construction, an extreme responder pushes both answers to where more constraints bind, and a midpoint responder is coherent by never committing — and style travels with education, culture and language, so every group contrast below is a style reading until it is not. And a constraint binds only for some positions: under any implication-shaped relation, denying the antecedent is coherent for free, so a person's expected violation rate is a mixture over their own position profile, people with unusual positions meet more binding constraints, and there is no population difficulty parameter that nets that out. The pair is modelled jointly, each item on its own latent, the relation as a constraint on the pair; only relations that bind for that person are scored; and the binding rate is published beside the violation rate.

*3.5 The lag has two bounds, and the bind of 3.2 narrows the window.* The second item must be answered outside the recall horizon of the first, else coherence is memory; and inside the timescale of the position, else incoherence is change. Both are measured — the recall horizon as the learning curve paper 0a already measures for any repeated item, the position's timescale as paper 5 measures a state item's, and here on repeats of the *items*, since a relation has no occasion of its own. A relation served outside its window is not scored, a person for whom the window is empty is not measured on that relation, and the empty windows are recorded because their share is a property of the instrument. The relation's occasion, where one is needed, is the second item's day, with the first item's day entered as a covariate.

*3.6 The pair rides the never-selected-on stream.* Serving the second item because of the first answer selects on the answer; serving relations preferentially to people whose coherence the model is least sure of selects on the estimate the reading is about, and while paper 0a permits steering on a current estimate — it is a function of the past and selection stays ignorable given the log — what it forbids is steering on anything measured jointly with the response it selects, which a relation served to resolve a person's own coherence rate is; and an adaptively chosen set is not an evaluation surface. So any published rate is computed on relations that rode the eligible-drawn never-selected-on stream — not the anchor-drawn one, which carries the spine's items and no authored relation — both items scheduled in advance under the floor with the realised probability recorded, a stated completion rule for a pair whose second item the floor never served, and half-served pairs modelled rather than dropped.

*3.7 The reveal is a treatment, and it moves the antecedent.* A system that shows a person how the population answered has intervened between the two items of every relation. It does two things at once — it is evidence about the first proposition, on which a person may rationally update, and it is a norm, to which a person may conform — and after it the first position may have moved, so scoring the second answer against the pre-reveal first answer reintroduces the position-change failure of 3.5 deliberately. So on any relation that carries a reveal the first item is re-asked sealed after it, coherence is scored against the post-reveal position, and the two effects are separated by arms the reveal's own dimensions supply — the crowd's size, its dispersion, and which population it is — since informational weight and normative pressure predict following differently across those.

*3.8 A disclosed arm, randomized, and what it does.* A random share of people is told that some items relate to others in ways that have a right answer, and which ones will not be said; the rest are told nothing. The contrast between the arms is the effect of knowing a key exists, which is the one manipulation that identifies the direction of 4.2's situation contrast. The same arm hosts every probe that would break the cover elsewhere — the reinterpretation probe of 3.2, the recognition probe of section 6, any observer asked to predict coherence, any stake attached to a relation — and its relations are never pooled with the blind arm's. And its contrast is the measured cost of the honest consent shape, which the earlier draft asserted destroyed the instrument and this paper measures instead.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 Whether tested and wild reasoning share a construct.* The wild measurement is a person's violation rate over binding, earned relations on the eligible-drawn stream, style removed; the tested one is paper 1's score. The reading is their *residual* correlation, with the profile facts and shared dispositions conditioned out as paper 1 requires of any tilt, because schooling, language and answering effort are common causes of both and a raw correlation near one is what a shared cause produces. Three branches, not two: a high residual correlation, and the bank measures under no test what the test measures under one; a low one, and either the situation is a large part of the tested score or the wild construct differs, which 4.2's arm decides; and a high raw correlation that vanishes on residualising, which is the confounded branch and is stated as a possible answer.

The reliability arithmetic is derived rather than asserted. A rate from n binding relations at violation probability p has error variance p(1−p)/n, and its reliability as a person parameter is the between-person variance of true rates against that error, which the earlier draft never stated. At p of one fifth and a between-person standard deviation of true rates of five hundredths, reliability of seven tenths needs about a hundred and fifty binding relations; at a standard deviation near nine hundredths — a person a standard deviation above the mean violating near three in ten against one in ten below — fifty suffice. The between-person spread is an unknown the first study returns, the count depends on it, and relations that share items are dependent, so the effective count is lower than the served count.

*4.2 The situation contrast, identified by disclosure.* One tested indicator and one wild indicator cannot identify a situation factor — a factor on one side is indistinguishable from that side's unique variance — so the earlier draft's latent was not a reading. Two things make it one. Multiple indicators on each side: relation parcels by type on the wild side, repeated sittings on the tested side, which paper 1 already schedules; with a discriminant test against a method factor as paper 8 requires of its own discrepancy. And the disclosed arm of 3.8: the difference in wild rates between people who know a key exists and people who do not is the effect of the knowledge, randomized, which is the only thing that gives the contrast a direction. Without the arm what is estimable is a contrast between two situations, and it is named as one.

*4.3 Motivated reasoning, with its two conditionings removed and its twin named.* Incoherence that concentrates in the topics a person has a stake in, and not in the topics they are indifferent to, with ability held, is a reading of reasoning that bends where the conclusion matters. Two conditionings the earlier draft made on the outcome are removed. Difficulty is not estimated from the charged topic's own population rate, which is inflated by the population's own motivated reasoning; it is taken from structurally matched neutral analogues of the same logical form. The stake is not read from an item that enters a scored relation; it is read from a declared position on an item that enters none, and from the interests axis. And the twin is named: a violation rate concentrated in one topic is also the strongest available evidence that the relations in that topic failed their defence for that topic — motivated reasoning and topic-specific defence failure are the same observable — so the reading is published only where the probe of 3.2 shows violators in the charged topic could not supply a reinterpretation at a rate different from violators in the neutral analogue, which is the pre-specified distinction, and is otherwise reported as unidentified between the two.

*4.4 Framing susceptibility, by design, with its trade stated.* The same proposition in two frames, frame order randomized within pair, served inside the window: a person reading the proposition gives one answer and a person reading the frame gives two. The clean identification of a framing effect is between people and yields no person parameter; the within-person design yields one and inherits a contamination — the first exposure crystallizes the attitude even when the answer is forgotten, which attenuates the effect toward finding people less susceptible than they are and interacts with attitude strength. So a between-people arm on the same propositions calibrates the attenuation, and the person parameter is published against it.

*4.5 Social influence on reasoning, with updating separated from conformity.* Under 3.7, the effect of the reveal on the coherence of the second answer against the post-reveal position, with the arms that separate the reveal's informational weight from its normative pressure. A person made coherent by a crowd that was itself coherent has either updated or conformed; the arms say which, and where the crowd was incoherent the two come apart on their own.

*4.6 The state-dependence of reasoning, as a density gain.* Paper 1's repeated sittings already carry paper 5's body covariate on the test's day; this adds the second item's day on every relation, which is denser rather than new. Its identification is paper 5's: the state share needs the within-window replicate — of the items, never of the relation, since a replicate inside the window is inside the recall horizon — the bad-day gap is a band, and the coupling is an asymmetry and never a cause.

*4.7 The bank classified by its relations, with what the classification depends on stated.* An item's loading on the coherence structure — the relations it enters, normalized by the authoring effort that put it there so the count is a property of the item and not of the queue; how often those relations are violated; and whether the violations track the keyed score, the stake or the day. The third component depends on the keyed score, and the earlier draft's claim that the classification needs no external score is withdrawn. An item whose violations track ability is a reasoning item in an opinion's clothes; one whose violations track stake is charged; one with no earned relation is taste — and the classification is estimated, revised as relations are earned and retired, never declared.

*4.8 The return to the genome.* Wild coherence, the situation contrast, the motivated-reasoning concentration and framing susceptibility are phenotypes that do not exist at population scale, and each has a genetics under paper 3's conditions — with the reliability attained stated per group, as paper 5 requires, since every one rises with the relations a person has answered, and that count is engagement.

**5 · The return path, the consent shapes, and the ranking**

*The logic test.* A second measurement of ability under no test, and the situation contrast it can be read beside.

*The bank.* A key without any item having one, earned rather than written, a second classification of its items, and a reason to be authored.

*The instruments and the lenses.* Each scale that describes how a person thinks gains a behavioural counterpart in how their opinions cohere, read as a discrepancy factor.

*The declared difference.* A group contrast on wild coherence beside the tested one, under three conditions paper 4 sets and the earlier draft dropped: relation-level invariance across the groups first, that paper's no-screening rule on what exists, and its custody condition on any reading that conditions on a declaration. A gap on the test that is absent in the wild is then a finding about the situation.

*The body.* One more within-person reading, denser than paper 5's.

*Observers, arranged competitions, and every other probe that breaks the cover.* Asking paper 2's observers to predict coherence tells them coherence exists, and they are measured too; attaching paper 8's stakes to a relation announces that it is scored, since a stake must be visible to move anything. Both are real readings and both run in the disclosed arm of 3.8, never pooled with the blind population.

*The design layer.* Relations are scheduled pairs on the eligible-drawn stream with a lag window each, the reveal is a timed or randomized event with a sealed re-ask, the disclosed arm is a randomized assignment — and every one is an allocation paper 0a's policy prices against the spine, the body report, the test and the guessing game, which share the same one observation a day.

*The consent shapes, enumerated.* What is exposed is a person-level cognitive score derived without the person's knowledge, and to whom is a separate decision per reader: the person, an observer asked to predict it, the genome crossing. The shapes: nothing disclosed; general disclosure that some items relate and which ones will not be said; general disclosure with a debrief per relation after it is scored; an opt-in scored stream beside an unscored one; per-item disclosure. The cost of each is not asserted — the earlier draft said per-item disclosure destroys the instrument, and this paper measures what disclosure does through the arm of 3.8 and reports the number. The smallest shape that keeps the blind measurement is general disclosure; whether that is the shape is the owner's decision, made with the measured cost in hand.

*Ranked by what they make knowable.* First, whether tested and wild reasoning share a construct, with the situation contrast beside it, because paper 1 and everything here read differently under each answer. Second, motivated reasoning as a person-by-topic phenotype where its twin can be excluded, which nothing else in the series reads. Third, framing and social influence by design, which need the schedule, the randomization and the sealed re-ask. The state-dependence and the genome are crossings of those.

**6 · What would have to hold**

*A key is earned, and the earning is falsifiable.* The two tests of 3.2, with their bounds pre-registered; the reinterpretation set stated in advance; double independent writing with agreement reported; and retirement on failure of either test, with the retired relations published as such.

*Only contrasts are published.* No level — no population rate, no mean situation effect, no claim that the bank is a free test — because a key error is common to everyone and unidentified from within.

*Style is removed first, and its residual share is published with every rate.*

*Relations are scored only where they bind*, with the binding rate beside the violation rate, on a joint model of the pair.

*The lag window is measured per relation and per person on repeats of the items*, an empty window is recorded, and a relation outside its window is not scored.

*Published rates ride the eligible-drawn stream*, steering never depends on anything measured jointly with the response it selects, and half-served pairs are modelled.

*The reveal is followed by a sealed re-ask*, and coherence is scored against the post-reveal position.

*The pair is not recognisable as a pair*, which is a constraint on the items, tested by asking — in the disclosed arm only.

*Difficulty for a charged topic comes from neutral analogues of the same form, and the stake from an item in no relation.*

*Relation-level invariance is tested before any cross-group or cross-language contrast.* The condition is stronger than item-level invariance: the constraint region must be the same region, and the violation rate net of ability the same rate, in every language and group compared; connectives, quantifier scope and modal force need not survive translation, and a relation is scored only where they did.

*Skipping is data*, modelled per relation beside the answer, with paper 5's band.

*Repetition teaches*, so a coherence rate is a trajectory, first exposure to relations is staggered, and the gain is decorrelated by design rather than subtracted.

*Every cover-breaking probe runs in the disclosed arm*, and the arm's relations are never pooled with the blind population's.

*Paper 1's conditions on the keyed score, paper 4's on any declaration, and paper 0a's on any allocation apply unchanged.*

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

*Relations authored, not found* — with premise items, scope qualifiers, likelihood-comparison formats where a bound is wanted, and both frames — in the hundreds per topic, because the earning process retires a share the design cannot know in advance.

*Binding relations per person derived from the between-person spread the first study returns*: on the order of a hundred and fifty at a modest spread, fifty at a wide one, per person for a person-level rate, and again per topic for a topic map — with the served count above the binding count by the inverse of the binding rate, and the effective count below the served count by the dependence shared items induce.

*Priced in shares of one observation a day.* Paper 0a's budget is one observation per person per day across every axis. At two items per binding relation, a person-level rate at the modest spread is three hundred items; three topics at the same are nine hundred; the window measurement needs repeats of both items of every relation before any is scored; the disclosed arm, the between-people framing arm and the sealed re-asks are further lines. This instrument, fully populated, is a large share of the entire stream for years, and the design says so rather than assuming the day is its own.

*Twenty to thirty framing pairs per person*, order randomized, with a between-people arm on the same propositions.

*The keyed test on the same people, repeated*, since 4.1 is a pairing and 4.2 needs multiple indicators on the tested side.

*A randomized disclosed arm*, sized for the situation contrast and for the probes it hosts.

*Reveal arms* — size, dispersion, population — with the sealed re-ask.

*Neutral analogues authored for every charged relation's logical form.*

*Staggered first exposure, and within-window replicates of items on a designed share.*

None is a reason to want less. Each is what a bank built to carry an earned key looks like from the inside.

**8 · What would decide it**

*Do relations survive their earning?* Before either study below, a set of authored relations is served on the eligible-drawn stream to a cohort with the keyed score, and each is put to 3.2's two tests. The quantity that decides the instrument is the survival rate and, among survivors, the between-person spread of violation rates — which sets every count in section 7. The study is powered on the spread: to estimate a between-person standard deviation of true rates near five hundredths with useful precision needs a cohort in the hundreds, each meeting on the order of a hundred binding relations, and a smaller study returns only that the spread is not large.

*Do tested and wild reasoning share a construct, and what does knowing change?* The same cohort, with the disclosed arm assigned at random and the tested side repeated: the residual correlation of 4.1 against an equivalence bound, and the arm contrast of 4.2, powered so that a situation effect of a stated size in rate units is detectable — which at the error variance above and a cohort of several hundred per arm is an effect of a few hundredths.

*Does incoherence concentrate where the stake is, once its twin is excluded?* Relations in three or more topics with neutral analogues of each form, one topic charged per person by a position declared on an unscored item; the person-by-topic variance component against an equivalence bound; and the reinterpretation probe run on violators in the charged and the neutral topics, with the reading published only where the probe rates differ. A null component closes 4.3 at this grain; a substantial one with no probe difference is unidentified between motivation and defence failure and is published as that.

**9 · The potential, priced against what the earning returns**

A ten is a dataset after which a field's standard design changes; a five answers questions the field already had. Every score is branched on the earning study, because if few relations survive, the instrument does not exist and every score below is zero.

*The measurement of reasoning under no test.* Seven, on the branch where relations survive at a rate that leaves a person-level count reachable within the budget share of section 7 and the first study's spread is wide enough for reliability; four on the branch where the spread is narrow, since contrasts between arms and topics survive while person parameters do not; zero where the earning fails.

*Motivated reasoning as a phenotype.* Six where the variance component survives and the probe separates it from its twin; two where the component survives and the probe does not, since an unidentified reading is not a phenotype; three where it is null, since the topic map still classifies items.

*Framing and social influence by design.* Five, on the branch where windows are non-empty for enough pairs, the reveal can be re-asked sealed, and the between-people arm calibrates the attenuation.

*The situation contrast.* Five where the disclosed arm identifies its direction; nothing without the arm, since a contrast between two situations is not a reading of a test.

*The bank as a self-classifying corpus.* Four: a structural result about any authored bank, conditional on the earning and on the keyed score it turns out to depend on.

The value concentrates in the first two, and both are decided by the earning study before anything else is built.

**10 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

What share of authored relations survives its own earning, which precedes everything and is the paper's largest unknown. How wide the between-person spread of violation rates is among survivors, which sets every count. Whether tested and wild reasoning share a construct once both are residualised, and what knowing a key exists does to the wild rate. Whether incoherence concentrates where the stake is, and whether the probe can tell that from a defence that failed in one topic. How many people have an empty window on the relations that matter. Whether framing susceptibility survives its own crystallization once calibrated between people. Whether a crowd's revealed answer is taken as evidence or as a norm, and whether that depends on which crowd it was. How much of a coherence rate is practice. And what disclosure costs, measured rather than asserted, which is the number the owner's decision on the consent shape waits for.
