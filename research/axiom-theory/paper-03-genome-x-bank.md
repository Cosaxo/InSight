# The genetics of what was never asked

*Paper 3 of the axiom series: imported genomes crossed with a question bank of unbounded depth.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 0 carries the general argument, paper 0a the design layer, and paper 1 the keyed-ability crossing and the genome conditions this paper leans on by reference.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because genotyping is cheap and phenotyping is expensive, so every genotyped population has been measured on a short list of traits chosen in advance, and any population in which asking is the product and the list has no end would carry a measured genetics of everything it asked.

**Abstract.** A genetic architecture is read from a trait measured on many genotyped people, and the traits that have one are the traits someone decided in advance to measure. Phenotyping costs attention per item, so genotyped cohorts are wide and shallow: a committee's list, asked once. Everything never on a list has no measured genetics, and the lists are short and conservative by construction. This paper describes a population that answers an unbounded bank of items over years and holds its own genome files, and argues that the crossing inverts the shape of the field: depth set by the bank rather than by a budget, items admitted by what they add rather than by what a committee predicted, and a battery that grows after the genotypes exist. Five things become knowable, each stated with the artefact it has to beat. An item-level map of which everyday preferences, habits and opinions covary with common genetic variation in the population and which do not, read twice, across unrelated people and within families, disattenuated by each item's own repeatability, defined on the continuum an item's split cuts, shrunk before it is ranked, and cleared of the heritable habit of answering questions in a particular way. The genetic structure of the bank's recovered factors beside their phenotypic structure, compared as two estimated correlations with intervals rather than as one estimate and a silence. How an item's genetic and environmental variances, and the genetic correlation of the item with itself across countries, move at fixed ancestry, which is where the same variation stops producing the same answer. Which of the bank's genetic signal is ability's, read as a conditional genetic covariance under a declared direction. And what depth buys, which is the reliability of a latent and not the size of a sample. The return path gives the bank a tag per item, gives the trait scales an external property to state beside what they predict, and gives the design layer a currency for admitting items that is one term in the bar and never the whole of it, because a bar denominated only in genetic information decides what a bank is for. Three conditions are the paper's own: discovery items are served blind to the genome and the genome-driven part of placement runs where the genome lives, because a served sequence chosen by a genome is a read of it; the import decision is a selection the blind rule does not close; and a finite output-privacy budget against an unbounded bank is spent per frozen cohort snapshot with each release's influence clipped and noised, not by publishing smaller numbers.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

To read the genetic architecture of a trait you need the trait measured on many people who are also genotyped. Genotyping is cheap and getting cheaper; a file from a consumer service costs less than a meal and reaches much of the common variation by imputation. Phenotyping is not cheap, because every item costs a person's attention, and attention is the one input that does not scale with a budget. So a genotyped population is phenotyped on a list, the list is short because each item is expensive, and it is chosen in advance by whoever funds the cohort, which means it is conservative: the traits already known to matter, measured the way they were measured before.

Two consequences follow, and both are structural rather than anyone's failing. Everything never on a list has no measured genetics, and the set of never-listed traits is most of what people are like: what they eat for breakfast, whether they believe in luck, how they take being interrupted, what they find funny, how they argue, what they collect. And the list cannot learn. A cohort phenotyped in one decade is analysed in the next on the items it happened to ask, so the battery is fixed before anyone knows which items would have carried signal.

A population in which asking is the product has the opposite shape. Its battery is as deep as its bank, the bank has no end, and items are admitted by what they add, continuously, after the genotypes already exist. Whatever is worth asking can be asked next week, of the same genotyped people.

**2 · The two axes alone, and where each stops**

The bank is a set of unkeyed items with no ceiling on its size, answered at depth over years. Alone it measures what people say, and with enough items it recovers the structure of what they say: which items go together, which factors organise them, how the factors vary by profile fact. It cannot say why any of that structure exists. A factor may be a disposition, a culture, an age, an economy, or a way of reading questions, and nothing inside the answers separates the cases. Its ceiling is cause.

The genome is a fact about the person that predicts nothing without a phenotype on the same people, and inherits the mixture of whatever phenotype it was trained on. Its ceiling is meaning: it needs something measured, on the same people, to be about. What it lacks is exactly what the bank has too much of.

Each axis's ceiling is the other axis.

**3 · The crossing, and the two roles an item can play**

Paper 0 states the general argument; three of its parts are specific here.

A covariance is a third measurement. For each item, the covariance between answers and common genetic variation across a population is a fact neither axis contains: how much of the variation in this answer covaries with genotype, and which other items' genotype-covarying variation it shares. The first is an item's heritable share in the population sense; the second is a genetic correlation between items; and across an unbounded bank both form a map that exists only in the pair.

Depth buys reliability, not people. Many items answered by the same person are correlated views of a smaller number of latent dispositions, and items inform each other about the latent they share. What depth buys is the reliability of that latent, and the gain is bounded by it: it saturates rather than compounding, and items that only restate each other raise agreement without raising it. The item-level map of section 4.1 is therefore never computed on answers imputed from other items, because an imputed item's share converges on its factor's, which is precisely the difference the map exists to draw. Imputation serves the factor-level reading and nothing else.

An item plays one of two roles, and the roles must not be confused. A placement item is served to place a person in the between-person geometry, and for that purpose the genome may serve as a prior, so that fewer items place the person: paper 0's cycle. That permission is also a disclosure. A sequence of items chosen by a person's genome carries information about it to anyone who can see the sequence, so the genome-driven part of placement runs where the genome lives, the served sequence is drawn under the same probability floor that makes selection estimable, and what leaves the device is the item, never the reason. A discovery item is served so that its genetics can be read, and for that purpose the genome may not influence whether or when the person is asked it, because a sample that answered an item partly because of their genotype is selected on the variable being studied. Every item carries its role, the discovery role is served blind to the genome, and an item may change role only forward, from discovery to placement once its genetics is read.

The blind rule closes one selection and names the other. Who imports a genome is not chosen by the serving policy, and importing is itself a behaviour with a heritable share in the population sense, so an analysis restricted to genome-holders conditions on a variable that genotype and an item's content can both cause, which produces associations that are effects in no population, and naming the population does not repair it. So the import decision is modelled with the same record the policy keeps for serving, the outside anchor of section 6 supplies the marginals it is weighted against, and answering is modelled beside being asked, because whether a person answers an opinion item is not independent of the answer.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 The item-level map, read twice.* For every item in the bank, the share of its variation that covaries with common genetic variation, estimated by one method on one population and compared only within that frame. A heritable share is a property of an estimator, a phenotype definition and a population, and moves substantially when any of the three moves, often enough to reverse a comparison, so the map is comparative rather than absolute: it ranks the bank's items against each other on one scale, and never quotes a bare figure as a fact about a trait.

The frame does not hold four things constant, and each is handled before an item is on the map. Reliability first: an item's heritable share cannot exceed the share of its variation that repeats at all, and a single opinion item asked once is the least repeatable measurement the system takes, so every discovery item carries a retest reliability measured on the repetition schedule the design already runs, the map is drawn on shares disattenuated by it, and an item whose reliability is not yet measured is not on the map. Scale second: the bank's items are answered on a small number of options, so the share is defined on the underlying continuum an item's split cuts, not on the split itself, and every item carries its option distribution with its share; two items that differ only in how lopsided their answers are must not differ on this scale, and neither must one item read in two countries where the split differs. Response style third, and it is the map's first false positive rather than a source of nulls: how a person answers questions at all, agreeing readily, using the ends, sitting in the middle, is a trait of the person present in every item at once, and any part of it that covaries with genotype appears as a heritable share on items whose content has none, so a response-style factor is estimated from balanced and reverse-keyed items and removed before the map is drawn, and its own heritable share is reported as a finding about answering. Selection fourth: the map is a ranking computed on the data that produced it, and an unbounded bank ranked once puts at its top the items whose estimates ran high, so no item enters on one estimate; the share is shrunk toward the bank's own distribution before it is ranked, the published ordering is the shrunken one, and an item's place is confirmed on a sample held out from the one that placed it. The set over which this is controlled grows without end, which makes the error rate a design choice restated each time the bank grows rather than a threshold fixed once.

On that scale, items separate. Some carry a heritable share at the high end of the bank's own distribution. That is covariation with genotype in the population, and it is not yet the person's own genotype acting on them: it also carries the environment a family's genotypes built, and the structure of who lives near whom. So every item is read twice, once across unrelated people and once within families using the relatedness paper 1's section 4.5 reads out of the genomes, and the gap between the two readings is itself a finding, per item, about how much of an everyday trait's genetic covariation runs through the person and how much through the home. Some items carry no share at all: they are asking about circumstances, culture or the year. That separation, item by item across everything anyone ever asked and cleared of the four artefacts above, is the map, and its value is that it sorts items no committee ever funded, which is most of the bank. It is ancestry-conditional twice over: an item's share can differ by ancestry for reasons of tagging rather than biology, and the variant set the map is drawn over is defined against a reference panel and a frequency threshold that are themselves ancestry-specific, so it is read within ancestry before across.

*4.2 The genetic structure of the bank's own factors beside the phenotypic one.* A bank of unbounded depth recovers its factors from how people answer rather than from what an author named. That does not make them author-free: they depend on which items were admitted, on how many factors are taken and on how the solution is rotated, and an admission bar denominated in genetic information selects the pool before structure is read out of it, so the structure is reported with the pool and the rule that built it. Recovery also needs the design: nobody answers more than a slice of an unbounded bank, so cells are missing by a policy rather than at random, structure is estimated conditional on the policy's own record, and the items whose loadings are fixed across everyone are what put the slices in one space.

Crossing with the genome asks two things of that structure. Whether the recovered factors are genetically coherent, so that the items that load together also share genetic architecture. And where the genetic and phenotypic structures disagree. The comparison is between two estimated correlations, not between one estimate and a silence: the phenotypic correlation between two items is decomposed into a genetic and a non-genetic part on the same true-score scale, after both items' reliabilities are in the model, and a disagreement is a genetic correlation near zero with a non-genetic one that is not, stated as intervals with the region inside which the two count as agreeing. A genetic correlation that is merely imprecise says nothing. Where the two structures agree, one source of variation is making both; where they disagree, they are being made by different things, and the reading says so without settling which one is the trait. That disagreement map is the finding, and it is available only because the bank is deep enough to recover structure and the genome is present to test it.

*4.3 Where the same variation stops producing the same answer.* Held at one ancestry and read across countries, an item's variance decomposition can change. A heritable share is a ratio, and a ratio falls when its denominator grows: a country whose environments vary more shows a lower share with every genetic effect identical, allele for allele, and nothing has been overridden. So the country reading is three numbers rather than one ratio: the genetic and the environmental variance separately, and the genetic correlation between the item as answered in one country and as answered in another. A correlation below one is the strong finding, that the same variation does not produce the same answer there; a ratio that moves while that correlation holds at one is the weaker and commoner finding, that the environment varies more there. The two-experiment discipline of paper 1's section 4.2 applies: across countries at fixed ancestry reads environment, across ancestries within a country reads tagging. And the batch condition points at this reading in particular. Consumer genotyping is sold by different companies in different countries, so vendor and country are one variable unless the design makes them two: the comparison runs only over vendors present in both countries compared, and a country whose genomes come from one source is not on the map.

*4.4 How much of the bank's genetics is ability's.* Answers to opinion items tilt with reasoning ability through many routes, and ability has a genetics of its own, so an item can carry a heritable share that is ability's rather than the item's. The quantity that separates them is the item's genetic covariance conditional on the ability genetic factor, disattenuated for the keyed score's reliability, and not a regression of the item on an observed score, which leaves ability partly in and biases the residual toward the items that loaded on it most. It is also two estimands, and which one it is has to be declared. If reasoning is a common cause of the item and of its genetics, the conditional covariance is the item's own. If the genetics reaches the item through reasoning, conditioning removes the effect rather than a nuisance, and what remains is the part that does not travel that route. Reasoning wearing the clothes of taste is the first case, and an item is entitled to that reading only where the direction is argued rather than assumed. This is paper 1's return-path reading with the genome as the third axis.

*4.5 Depth as a number, and the bar it sets.* For a latent disposition the bank recovers, the precision of its genetic signal rises with the number of items that measure it and the number of people who answered enough of them, saturating as section 3 says, and the two trade against each other under a fixed attention budget. It becomes knowable, per disposition, how many items and how many people bought a given precision, which is the price list of the genetics of that disposition in the currency of paper 0a. The same currency sets a bar for admitting a new item, in two halves. A new item's genetic information cannot be known before it is asked; it is the quantity asking would learn. So before serving, an item is admitted on a prediction from what the bank knows about items like it, their topic, format and neighbours, and that predictor is stated, fitted out of sample, and reported with how well it has predicted so far, because it will otherwise reproduce whatever the map already believes. After serving, the item is kept or retired on what it actually added. The first half is a guess the design is honest about; only the second is a measurement.

**5 · The return path: what the crossing gives back to every other axis**

*The bank.* Every item gains a tag: heritable share within its frame and within families, its genetic neighbours, its response-style loading, its reliability and its role. No bank computes any of that about itself. The tag tells the bank which items covary with the person's own genotype, which with the home, and which with the world, and the admission bar of 4.5 tells it what to ask next, under the rule below about what a bar may be denominated in.

*The trait scales.* Authored scales cut the bank into traits by an author's theory. The genetic structure of 4.2 is an external property of the cut, stated beside what the scale predicts and not on its own a reason to break it: a scale whose items share genetic architecture is carrying one source of variation under one name, and a scale whose items split across genetic factors is carrying two. A construct can be genetically heterogeneous and still be the right unit for what it is used to do, so the scales are read with that property attached, and revised only where the recovered structure, the genetic structure and what the scale predicts agree against the author.

*The keyed ability axis.* Paper 1 gave the bank a residual discrimination per item, how much its split moves with ability. This paper gives the same item its genetic decomposition under a declared direction, and the two together say whether an item's ability-loading is itself genetic, which is a question about the item and about ability at once.

*The profile facts.* Comparing a trait across countries is two questions, not one: whether the item means the same thing there, which invariance answers, and whether the same variation produces it, which the country reading answers. An item can pass the first and fail the second, and a comparison that has checked only the first is incomplete.

*Interests and the body.* Declared interests are items, and the map of 4.1 covers them: which of what people are into covaries with genotype, item by item, before any inventory is designed. And the body's occasion reports raise the within-person question this paper cannot answer alone: whether the variability of an answer across days is heritable, which needs occasions and belongs to paper 5.

*The design layer.* The item-role rule of section 3 and the two-half bar of 4.5 are constraints on the serving policy of paper 0a, and one more is a choice about what the bank is for, stated rather than optimised into. A bar denominated only in genetic information grows the bank toward heritable, reliable, evenly split items and away from the ones a culture cares about most, which are often near-consensus, fast-moving or about circumstance, the half of 4.1's map that no committee funds either. It also eats its own evidence: once admission conditions on expected genetic signal, the separation 4.1 reports is partly a fact about what was let in. So genetic information is one term in the bar and never the whole of it, a stated share of the discovery set is admitted on other grounds and served regardless of what it is expected to add, and the map records which items entered by which route.

**6 · What would have to hold**

Consumer genome files are genotyped at common markers and imputed; imputation quality falls with allele frequency and with distance from the reference panel; chip, version and vendor are batch structure aligned with country and ancestry; the files carry no quality metrics and no proof of whose they are. Paper 1's section 6 states these conditions and they apply here unchanged: every claim is a common-variant claim against a stated reference and threshold, ancestry is modelled, batch enters every model and bounds 4.3, and identity is established from the data before a file is a phenotype's partner.

Every item on the map has a measured reliability, an option distribution, and a response-style loading, and is read on the continuum its split cuts.

Mating is not random on the bank's own subject matter. People pair on opinions, tastes and values more than on almost anything else the system measures, and that assortment inflates an item's heritable share and can manufacture a genetic correlation between two items that share no mechanism. Both readings are stated with the item's assortment measured on the population's own partner pairs, and a genetic correlation between two items on which people visibly pair is not read as one disposition until the within-family contrast survives it.

Items mean the same thing across the groups compared and across the years compared. An item's meaning and its split are tested for drift across answer epochs on the same footing as across languages and countries, the epoch travels with the answer, and structure recovered from a matrix filled over years is read only over the items that hold still in it.

Discovery items are served blind to the genome, the genome-driven part of placement runs where the genome lives, and an item's role travels with it.

Selection is anchored twice. Who answers a voluntary bank, and who among them imports a genome, are both selections; the second is heritable in the population sense and is modelled with the policy's record and weighted against an outside anchor, a probability sample or a register, because a self-selected population cannot validate its own selection model. Every number carries the population it is about and the weighting that made it about anyone else.

Output privacy is budgeted against an unbounded bank, and the safety of a release is not its dimension. A heritable share and a genetic correlation are both functions of every genome in the cohort, so each release clips one person's influence to a stated bound, adds noise calibrated to that bound, and spends against the composition of every release the system has ever made rather than per number. What the unbounded bank makes unbounded is the count of releases, and that is what the budget rations: the system publishes for a frozen cohort snapshot, states which snapshot each number came from, and re-releases on a new snapshot only by spending again. Differencing is not forbidden by a schedule; it is made uninformative by freezing the cohort a number is about. Paper 0's section 3.3 names the mechanism; this paper names the tension that makes it binding.

Consent is familial-aware and scoped to the use. A genome describes relatives who did not consent, its consent is stated at family scope and for the reading of traits the system itself measures, and the within-family reading of 4.1 is available only where each member consented in their own right.

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

Per-item precision needs people, and the bank divides people among items, so the discovery set is served on a matrix design that allocates each discovery item a floor of answerers who hold a genome. Holding one is a fact known before the answer and may be served on; its content may not, and the difference is section 3's rule. What that buys is a floor, and what it costs is that the discovery sample is the genome-holding population by construction, reweighted against the outside anchor and never assumed to stand for anyone else. The country reading needs the genotyped population spread across countries at shared ancestry and shared vendor, and the ancestry reading needs it spread across ancestries within countries. The within-family reading needs relative pairs among the genome-holders, which the genomes themselves identify. The disposition-level precision of 4.5 needs items per disposition and people per item in the balance the price list states. None is a reason to want less. Each is what a fully populated bank beside a fully populated genome looks like from the inside.

**8 · The potential, at full population**

Fully populated, this crossing is a genetics of everyday life: for everything a population was ever asked, whether the answer covaries with the person's genotype, with the home that raised them, or with the world, and how those shares move across the world. It reads the structure of what people are like from how they answer rather than from how an author divided them, tests that structure against the one source of variation that precedes culture without making that source the judge, and grows toward the dispositions it understands least while keeping a stated share of its attention for the questions a culture cares about. It makes the bank the deepest phenotype battery that exists beside genotypes, at a depth set by a population's attention rather than by a committee's budget, on genomes the population already held.

Inside the system it tags every item, states a property of every scale, prices every disposition's genetics in attention, and gives the design layer a new job and a stated limit on it. Paper 1's keyed score says what reasoning is; paper 2's observers say what a self-report is worth; this paper says which of the things a person says about themselves covary with what they were born with, which with where they grew up, and which with neither.

**9 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

Whether the heritable-share map saturates before it reaches the long tail of the bank, or whether the tail is where the surprises are. How far the across-family and within-family readings of 4.1 disagree, item by item, which is the measure of how much of everyday genetics runs through the home. How the placement and discovery roles interact when an item is both the best placement item for a person and a discovery item for the population, and how much attention the blind-serving rule costs against the cycle. How a finite privacy budget is best spent across an unbounded set of items, which is a design problem with no precedent at this shape. Whether the genetic and phenotypic factor structures of a deep bank disagree a little or a lot once both are on one scale with intervals, which decides how much of what people say about themselves is the world talking. And whether a disposition's genetics estimated from many weak items on many people converges to what a purpose-built instrument would find, or reads something the instrument never could.
