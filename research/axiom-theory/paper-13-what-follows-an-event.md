# What follows an event

*Paper 13 of the axiom series: the event as the one thing that gives every axis's within-person series a common origin, so that the order in which axes respond becomes readable — with the two randomized families kept apart, the concurrent arm as the counterfactual, the world's events under named estimators, and a second budget the first cannot price.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product. Paper 0 supplies the two classes of event; G3 the two identification routes, the estimators the observational one admits, and the hierarchical shrinkage; paper 0a the event-triggered serving this paper must read around, and the eligible-drawn stream it reads on; paper 2 the early-against-late estimand at a horizon and the tie-neighbourhood exposure design; paper 9 the choice-set exposure design and the cumulative-dose budget; paper 5 the leaving hazard, the calendar-position match and the band; paper 6 the two conditions any resilience phenotype must carry; paper 7 the offer as an instrument with its restrictions stated; paper 12 the release cap any genome reading spends against; G4 the incommensurability this paper's second budget inherits.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because a population observed continuously across many sources is also a population to which things happen, and an event is the only thing that aligns every source's within-person series at one moment — so the order in which the sources respond is a reading no source makes alone and no lag structure recovers without an impulse; and because a system that itself causes some events, at times or in occurrence it can randomize, holds a causal instrument whose cost is borne by the person on the other side of the assignment, in a currency the measurement budget does not denominate.

**Abstract.** Paper 0 sorted a person's events into those the system controls and those the world supplies, and G3 gave each its route. This paper makes the event the unit of a reading across every axis, and its cross-axis claim is specific: an event aligns every source's series at one origin, so what becomes readable is the *response ordering* — which axis moves first, which follows, which never moves — a quantity no single source carries and paper 5's cross-lagged asymmetry cannot recover, because an asymmetry between two series says nothing about a third and nothing about an impulse. Five facts organize the reading. The first class splits in two: events whose occurrence is randomized, which have an unassigned arm, and events whose timing is randomized because everyone eventually gets them, whose estimand is early-against-late at a stated horizon that shrinks as the late arm arrives — and for both the counterfactual is the concurrent arm at the same calendar position, never the person's own pre-series, which reinstates every trend the randomization was bought to remove and serves only as a precision device and a falsification window. Under staggered timing, using already-assigned people as controls for later-assigned ones weights their effects negatively, so the estimator names its clean comparison group and does not merely read at event time. Exposure runs on a graph, per family — tie-neighbourhood for a reveal, choice-set for a display — so a person's clean baseline is a reserved unexposed block and not an assumed quiet window, and a cumulative dose is read as a dose. The world's events are read under sequential ignorability with the estimators it admits, matched on calendar position, with the pre-event-window test and a negative control that falsifies and never certifies, and with the leaving hazard estimated jointly because the outcome that defines a recovery is censored by the person leaving. And the randomizations are a second budget: what an assignment costs the person is denominated per family in units the measurement budget does not share, so the two are ranked by a stated policy parameter or by judgement, and the paper does not pretend they are one currency. Behavioural resilience is defined only on a fixed, repeated, dose-controlled adverse challenge, with the condition that the instrument — response propensity and the leaving hazard — is itself perturbed by the challenge, and is priced in people times events with a reliability term. The offer designs the series already runs are stated as the instruments they are, with exclusion, monotonicity and the complier estimand named, and the earlier draft's third class is withdrawn as a class. Which events may be caused, with what is imposed on whom and the smallest shape that still identifies, is the owner's.

**1 · The problem, stated structurally**

A population measured continuously across many sources is also a population to which things happen, and each event is followed by readings on every source at once. The series has read events throughout: a reveal, an illness onset, a challenge, an assigned stake, a display, a disclosure, an offer, a withheld pairing. Each paper built its own event and its own reading, and none states what they share.

What they share is an origin. Every within-person series in the series moves on its own clock, and paper 5 reads the lag between two of them as a cross-lagged asymmetry that is reported as an asymmetry and never as a cause, because nothing aligns the two series at a moment. An event aligns them all. After it, each source's series is at a known distance from one origin, and the order in which they move — the body's channel first, the bank's answers days later, the ties weeks later, or the reverse — is a quantity defined only across sources and only relative to an impulse. That ordering is the reading this paper adds; the rest is the discipline that makes it honest.

**2 · Each side alone, and the mechanism of the crossing**

*An event alone is a timestamp.* A reveal happened; a tie ended; a contest was lost. Without the series on the other sources it is a fact with nothing to explain, and without a comparison it is not even an effect. Its ceiling is a date.

*A source's series alone has no origin.* Paper 5's occasion series, the bank's repeated items, the body's stream, the tie graph's changes: each moves, and the movements can be lagged against one another, but a lag between two series is symmetric in what it does not know — it cannot say what moved either — and it says nothing about a third. Its ceiling is a correlation in time.

*The crossing's mechanism is alignment.* An event, and only an event, gives every source's series one origin. With it, the responses can be laid on one axis of distance-from-event and compared across sources: which responds within the day, which within the week, which never. That comparison is the cross-axis reading no source makes alone, and it is available only under the identification the rest of the paper states — because a response ordering read against the wrong counterfactual is an ordering of trends, not of responses.

**3 · Five facts about reading an event**

*3.1 The first class is two families, and the counterfactual is the concurrent arm.* Some events the system can randomize in occurrence: an offer made or not, a display shown or withheld, a stake assigned or not. Those have an unassigned arm at the same calendar position, and the effect is the contrast between arms, read at each distance from assignment. Other events everyone eventually gets — a reveal, a challenge, a disclosure — so what is randomized is their timing, and the estimand is paper 2's: early against late, at a stated horizon, a contrast that shrinks as the late arm's event arrives and vanishes once it has. No never-treated group exists for a timing family and the paper does not read as if one did. In neither family is the person's own pre-event series the counterfactual. Randomization licenses a contrast between assigned and unassigned units at the same time; it does not license a before-after within a person, which reinstates season, tenure, practice and the reactivity paper 5 measures in its own arm — every trend the randomization was bought to remove. The own pre-series is a precision device, entered as a covariate, and a falsification window, in which an effect before assignment is a design failure.

*3.2 Under staggered timing, the already-assigned are not controls, and reading at event time does not fix it.* When people are assigned at different times and everyone's effect depends on distance from their own event, an estimator that uses already-assigned people as the comparison for later-assigned ones combines their effects with weights that can be negative, and a pooled coefficient can carry the wrong sign. An earlier draft misnamed the mechanism as pooling across distances — which is a convex combination and cannot change sign — and proposed reading at event time as the remedy, which relabels the comparison without cleaning it, since relative-period coefficients are contaminated by effects at other periods unless the comparison group is clean. The remedy is the estimator: a comparison group of the not-yet-assigned or the never-assigned at the same calendar position, and an interaction-weighted or imputation-based estimator that uses only clean comparisons. Which summaries of the resulting curve are admitted as estimands — a value at a stated distance, a time to return, an excursion as a ratio — is stated, and they are shrunk across distances hierarchically rather than refused, because a blanket refusal to summarize forfeits the borrowing of strength the design cannot spare.

*3.3 Exposure runs on a graph, per family, and a clean baseline is reserved rather than assumed.* A reveal exposes a tie neighbourhood; a display exposes every candidate in a choice set; a pairing exposes a dyad. These are different units of assignment with different estimands, and paper 2's tie-neighbourhood design and paper 9's choice-set design with its placeholder arm are not interchangeable, so the exposure mapping is stated per family. Two consequences an earlier draft missed. On a dense graph with several families running, a person's neighbours' events fall inside any window called their pre-period, so no window is quiet by assumption; a clean baseline is a reserved unexposed block per person, as paper 2 buys one, or a staggered neighbourhood assignment, and the paper says what happens to a reading when no clean window exists. And a withheld face is not an event: it is a cumulative dose paper 9 denominates in impressions and days and bounds per window, so it is read as a dose-response and never forced into an event frame.

*3.4 The world's events are read with named estimators and one-sided checks.* An illness, a tie ending, a move: observational, with onset confounded by state, reporting state-dependent, and past outcomes affecting future exposure. The route is paper 0's — sequential ignorability, onset as the unit, the report modelled — and G3's, which says adjusting for the intervening state as a covariate is not valid under confounders affected by prior outcomes and names the estimators that are: inverse-probability weighting of the onset probability, or standardization over the period sequence. The match is on calendar position, as papers 5 and G3 match, and not on the pre-event state, because matching on a noisy pre-measure of the outcome does not remove regression to the mean and can invert it; state enters through the model. Three checks travel with every world reading. The pre-event-window test papers 5 and G3 require, in which an effect before onset is a failure. A negative-control outcome the event cannot affect, which falsifies the assumption when it moves and certifies nothing when it does not, since it detects only confounding that acts on it. And the leaving hazard estimated jointly with the response, because a recovery is censored by the person leaving and leaving is the most extreme value of failing to recover — so any time-to-return is a competing-risks quantity and carries paper 5's band over the censored.

*3.5 The randomizations are a second budget, and it is not the measurement's currency.* An assignment costs the person on the other side of it: a withheld face, an assigned loss, a delayed reveal. Paper 9 denominates one such cost in impressions and days, paper 8 another as a per-person budget for being the mismatched side, and harm to a person is in neither; paper 0a's measurement budget is bits per unit of attention. G4 states that summing across such units needs a policy parameter no theorem supplies, and an earlier draft dressed the sum as a measurement. It is not. The causal budget is a second budget, each family's cost denominated in its own unit, ranked against measurement either by an exchange rate stated as a policy parameter or by judgement named as one, and logged with the realised assignment probability like any serving decision — but the assignment is not free because it is clean, and it is not priced in bits because it was not an observation.

**4 · What becomes knowable**

*4.1 The response ordering across sources.* After an occurrence-randomized event, read against the concurrent unassigned arm on the eligible-drawn stream — because paper 0a's event-triggered serving changes what is served after an event, so a post-event series on the adaptive stream is measured on items chosen because the event happened, and only the never-selected-on stream is composition-stable across the window — the distance at which each source first departs from its arm, per source, with the ordering across sources as the reading. Which moves first after a loss: the body's channel, the bank's persistence items, the tie graph. This is the paper's cross-axis claim, stated as a claim: the ordering is stable across events of one family within a person if the sources respond through a common path, and differs across families if they do not, and a population in which the ordering is the same for every family has one path where a population with family-specific orderings has several. For a timing family the same ordering is read as early-against-late at each horizon and fades with it.

*4.2 Behavioural resilience, on the stimulus paper 6 requires.* Paper 6 defines resilience only against a fixed challenge repeated identically, and only where the challenge does not perturb the sampler; an earlier draft cited that paper and dropped both conditions. So the behavioural phenotype is defined on one fixed, repeated, dose-controlled adverse challenge — an assigned contest against an opponent at a fixed relative strength, say, under paper 8's arm — and not across families that differ in kind and dose. Its second condition is stated and it binds: the instrument here is the person's response propensity and their leaving hazard, and an adverse event perturbs both, so the ruler and the signal move together unless the propensity model bounds it, which is the response-propensity model paper 5 fits jointly. Time-to-return is then a competing-risks quantity against leaving, its between-person variance is estimated on people, and the count is derived in paper 8's form: from a single-event reliability of the time-to-return functional and a minimum meaningful between-person standard deviation, roughly eight over the square of the per-person reliability in people, each carrying enough events for that reliability — which at plausible single-event reliabilities is hundreds of people with dozens of events each, tens of thousands of assigned adverse events, and years. Whether that phenotype shares a clock with paper 6's metabolic functional on the same people is a claim the pair can test: one clock if the two time-to-returns covary within person across challenges beyond what the body's state share explains, several if they do not.

*4.3 Effect modification by genotype, under its own conditions and paper 12's cap.* Randomizing an event identifies its effect within each genotype stratum, so the difference across strata is identified as effect modification by the stratifying variable — not as an effect of the genotype, because within ancestry the strata still differ by assortative mating, by indirect genetic effects, and by the lifetime differential treatment paper 9's treatment door describes, which is itself genetically correlated, and the moderator's own confounding is named as such. Its power is stated in paper 6's overlap-times-reliability form, and an interaction needs multiples of a main effect's sample, so below a stated target the reading is unidentified rather than low. And every such reading is a genome release that spends against paper 12's finite lifetime cap, with the ancestry pipeline's own releases counted first, so the reading exists only as an allocation from that cap and is conditioned on it.

*4.4 Chains, with what randomization identifies.* An event on one source followed by movement on a second and a third is a chain. Randomizing the event identifies the total effect on each; it does not identify a mediation, and an earlier draft's escape clause — a mediation is publishable if a later link was randomized — was wrong, because randomizing a mediator identifies controlled direct effects and not the natural decomposition mediation names, which needs cross-world assumptions no randomization supplies. So the paper reads each source's total response marginally, names controlled direct effects where the mediator was jointly randomized, and never conditions a later link on an earlier one, since that conditions on a post-treatment variable and opens the collider paper 8 names.

*4.5 The world's events, under 3.4.* Illness, tie endings, moves: onset as unit, the named estimator, calendar-position match, the pre-window test, the negative control as falsification, the hazard jointly, the band over the unreported — and the read population named as events severe enough to be reported, since the onset's own selection makes it so.

*4.6 Offer designs, as the instruments they are.* An offer the system randomizes and the person completes or declines — a pairing, a suggestion, an entry — is a first-class assignment with non-compliance, which is what an earlier draft called a third class and is not one. It is read as paper 7 reads its offer: an intention-to-treat on the offer, and a complier average effect under an exclusion restriction — that the offer moves outcomes only through completion — and monotonicity, both stated per family and both doubtful where a declined offer is itself an event that changes what the person believes about the system, which the paper says rather than assumes away. The completer mean is not the complier effect; it mixes always-takers in. And the selection the system's own suggestions induce is the contrast between the complier effect and the completer mean, never the gap between the intention-to-treat and the completer mean, which is produced by the compliance share alone.

*4.7 Anticipation, as a discrepancy factor within one family.* A person who knows an event is due moves before it. For events the person must attend — a contest, a challenge — unpredictable timing does not remove anticipation, because consent to the family tells the person one is coming and the system can randomize the date but not that knowledge; date-anticipation becomes hazard-anticipation, roughly constant across the window, and is absorbed into whatever baseline is used, which biases toward zero rather than removing. So a hazard-anticipation term is carried for every attended family. Anticipation as a trait is then read within one family randomized to a scheduled arm and an unscheduled arm, as a latent discrepancy factor with its reliability — never as the difference of two slopes across families that differ in kind, which is the difference score the series refuses.

**5 · The return path, the forks with their arithmetic, and the ranking**

*Every source* gains a place in the response ordering and a total effect per randomized family on the eligible-drawn stream.

*The body* gains the behavioural resilience phenotype defined as paper 6 requires, and the clock claim of 4.2.

*The genome* gains effect modification by stratum, named as such, under paper 12's cap.

*Observers, ties, the arena, the bank and the learned representation* gain their randomized events read under one discipline — the concurrent arm, the per-family exposure mapping, the eligible-drawn stream — and their offer designs read as instruments with restrictions stated.

*The design layer* gains the second budget, named as one it cannot price in bits.

*The forks, each with what is imposed, on whom, the smallest shape that still identifies, and its cost.* Which families may be randomized at all: a delayed reveal imposes a delay on the person delayed and identifies at a shrinking horizon; a withheld display imposes a dose paper 9 bounds in impressions and days and identifies an interaction at the choice-set level; an assigned adverse contest imposes a likely loss and identifies resilience — the owner sets the set, per family, with each family's denomination. Whether adverse events may be assigned deliberately, with a middle shape an earlier draft omitted: randomize the timing or the notification of an adverse event the person would have incurred anyway — paper 8's assigned-opponent arm already produces likely losses — or a graded severity ladder consented in advance, which identifies less than deliberate assignment and imposes less, and is priced as the middle option rather than pricing only the extremes. What consent shape each family carries — general, per family, per event — with the cost that per-event consent makes anticipation structural and general consent makes it uniform. And whether unpredictable timing is an acceptable experience per family, with the finding of 4.7 that it does not buy what an earlier draft claimed. The paper states each and decides none.

*Ranked by what they make knowable, and priced as the same set.* First, the response ordering across sources, which is the cross-axis reading and exists only under this discipline. Second, behavioural resilience and its clock claim, on the stimulus paper 6 requires. Third, the offer designs read as instruments, which repairs readings papers 7, 8 and 11 already make. Fourth, the world's events under named estimators. Fifth, anticipation within one family. Effect modification by genotype is ranked by paper 12's cap, which is its binding constraint.

**6 · What would have to hold**

*The first class is two families and the counterfactual is the concurrent arm at the same calendar position* — unassigned for an occurrence family, not-yet-assigned or late for a timing family at a stated horizon — with the own pre-series as covariate and falsification window only.

*Assignment is independent of the pre-event state conditional on the logged propensity*, since paper 0a's policy is state-dependent, and calendar terms are entered on randomized families as paper 6 enters season.

*The estimator uses only clean comparisons* — never the already-assigned as controls — and the admitted summaries of the curve are stated and shrunk hierarchically across distances and, per G3, across items nested in factors with items as their own estimands, with the event-time grid counted in the multiplicity.

*Post-event outcomes are read on the eligible-drawn stream*, or with the serving propensity held fixed across the window and entered otherwise, because event-triggered serving changes what is served.

*The exposure mapping is stated per family with its unit of assignment*, a clean baseline is a reserved unexposed block or a staggered neighbourhood, a cumulative dose is read as a dose, and the paper says what a reading becomes when no clean window exists.

*Concurrent events are spaced or jointly estimated with cross-family terms*, since one policy schedules every family and their windows overlap.

*The world's events carry the named estimator, the calendar-position match, the pre-window test, a negative control that falsifies only, the joint leaving hazard, and the band.*

*Behavioural resilience is defined on one fixed, repeated, dose-controlled challenge*, with the response-propensity model bounding the instrument's own perturbation, as a competing-risks quantity.

*Offer designs state exclusion and monotonicity per family and publish the complier estimand*, with never-takers named unidentified and the selection quantity defined against the completer mean.

*A hazard-anticipation term is carried for every attended family*, and anticipation as a trait is a discrepancy factor within one family.

*A chain publishes total effects marginally and controlled direct effects only under joint randomization.*

*Effect modification by genotype names its moderator's confounding, its power target, and its allocation from paper 12's cap.*

*The second budget is denominated per family and ranked against measurement by a stated policy parameter or a named judgement*, never summed in bits.

*Consent to be randomized is per family and is a fork, and every fork carries its arithmetic.*

**7 · The requirements the design generates**

*For resilience, people and events derived from a reliability*: at a single-event time-to-return reliability of a few tenths, hundreds of people with dozens of assigned adverse events each, tens of thousands of events, over years — in paper 8's form, and stated as the requirement it is rather than as dozens and hundreds.

*A concurrent unassigned arm per occurrence family and a horizon schedule per timing family*, with the clean-comparison estimator.

*A reserved unexposed block per person on the graph*, per paper 2, sized to the number of families running.

*Eligible-drawn-stream capacity across every event window*, which is a standing charge on the daily budget priced as one.

*A per-family exposure mapping maintained as ties change*, and a dose ledger for cumulative exposures.

*A response-propensity and leaving-hazard model estimated jointly with every event reading.*

*Calendar-position matched controls, a pre-window test, and a negative-control outcome per world family.*

*An allocation from paper 12's cap before any genotype reading*, with the ancestry pipeline's releases counted.

*A second-budget ledger per family in its own unit*, with the exchange rate or the judgement recorded.

*One family randomized to scheduled and unscheduled arms*, for the anticipation factor.

None is a reason to want less. Each is what a population read event by event looks like from the inside.

**8 · What would decide it**

*Is the response ordering a reading?* One occurrence family with a concurrent arm, on the eligible-drawn stream, with the per-source departure distances and their ordering estimated per person and its stability across events of that family within person — against a null in which the sources depart in random order. Stable ordering is the crossing's claim confirmed; random ordering says the sources respond through no common path, which closes 4.1 for that family and is itself the finding.

*Does behavioural resilience vary between people, on a fixed challenge, net of leaving?* One dose-controlled adverse challenge repeated under paper 8's arm, the time-to-return as a competing-risks quantity with the hazard jointly, its between-person variance against an equivalence bound at the counts of section 7, and the response-propensity model's perturbation term reported. Null, and there is no phenotype; substantial, and the clock claim of 4.2 is testable on the same people.

*Do the world's curves survive their checks?* One family — illness, since paper 5 reads it — under the named estimator with calendar-position matching: whether the pre-window test is flat, whether the negative control is flat, and whether the curve survives the band. A moving pre-window or a moving control falsifies the family and it is published as unidentified; a flat pair certifies nothing and the curve is published with its band.

**9 · The potential, priced with each score's own condition, on the set section 5 ranked**

A ten is a dataset after which a field's standard design changes; a five answers questions the field already had. Every score is conditional on the owner's forks, since an assignment is imposed on a person.

*The response ordering across sources.* Seven, conditional on occurrence families being permitted and on the ordering proving stable within person, because a population with randomized events read on every source at once against a concurrent arm does not exist at any size, and the ordering is a reading no cross-lagged design recovers; three if the ordering is random, since then the total effects survive and the crossing does not.

*Behavioural resilience and its clock.* Six where a fixed adverse challenge may be assigned and the variance survives its bound at section 7's counts; three under the middle shape of timing-randomized natural losses, which identifies less; nothing where no adverse assignment is permitted, since the phenotype is then undefined.

*Offer designs as instruments.* Five, because it repairs readings the series already makes and names what they were.

*The world's events.* Four, conditional on the band and the checks, because sequential ignorability is untestable in general and the reading is honest only with its band.

*Anticipation within one family.* Three, conditional on both arms in one family.

*Effect modification by genotype.* Priced by its allocation from paper 12's cap and its power target, and unidentified below either.

**10 · Open questions the data would have to answer**

Whether the response ordering across sources is stable within a person, which is the crossing's claim and closes or opens 4.1. Whether behavioural resilience on a fixed challenge varies between people once leaving is a competing risk, and whether it shares a clock with the body's. How much of a post-event series on the adaptive stream was the serving policy rather than the person, which the eligible-drawn stream measures. Whether any clean baseline exists on a dense graph with several families running, or whether the reserved block is the only one. How large hazard-anticipation is for attended families, and whether a scheduled and an unscheduled arm within one family can be consented at all. Whether the exclusion restriction survives for any offer family once a declined offer is itself an event. And which events the owner permits at all, with what imposed on whom — which is the question under every score above and the one no data answers.
