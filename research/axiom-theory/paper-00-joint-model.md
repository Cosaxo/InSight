# The joint model as the data structure

*Paper 0 of the axiom series: how to hold many-axis data so that connections are discoverable, how to find the patterns at the least cost in human attention, and what the structure is for.*

**Status: theory research.** Nothing here describes the app or proposes a change to it; `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how one reaches the product.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because no discipline has yet had to design the data structure and the pattern-finder for a population measured on many axes, across many occasions, with its ties recorded, under a fixed daily budget of attention per person. The design questions here belong to that dataset, whoever assembles it.

**Abstract.** Each shape of human measurement exists in part. Biobanks now hold many people and some occasions, but the occasions are of the body and the clinic rather than of what a person thought that day, and the ties are absent. Diary studies hold the thought and the day for a few hundred people. Network studies hold the tie without the trait. What no resource holds is all four at once, at the magnitude each separately requires. A system in which each person spends a small daily budget of attention on keyed tests, self-report items, sealed predictions about people they know and reports on their body, and can bring a genome they already hold, is that resource, and its binding constraint is the attention budget. This paper argues that the structure of the data and the method of finding patterns both follow from designing around that budget. It proposes that the data structure is the joint latent model itself: a between-person geometry every axis measures into under tested rather than assumed invariance, a population distribution of within-person geometries aligned by shared anchor items, and a tie geometry whose relationship term is the distinctive part of a prediction after the population's normative answer is removed; each axis a measurement model carrying a bias model whose corrections are admitted only against an axis that does not share the bias's mechanism; and a custody rule under which first-order statistics are aggregated from devices while pairwise quantities name the pairwise primitive they need, with input privacy and output privacy treated as the two different problems they are. It argues that the choice of what to observe is the pattern-finder for population couplings and that partial pooling is the pattern-finder for a person, states the serving objective as an explicit scalarization with an exploration share and a probability floor, and sets out the estimation layer, the causal layer split into what the system controls and what it merely observes, the single currency read on a policy-independent stream, and the conditional form of the self-extending property. It closes with what the structure is for, at four levels, and the open problems that make this research rather than engineering.

**1 · The problem, stated as research**

Human variation is measured in three shapes, and each exists in part. Population genetics and biobanks measure very many people on a handful of phenotypes, and increasingly on some occasions, wearable weeks and linked records and repeat waves; but the occasions are of the body and the clinic, not of what the person thought or preferred that day, and the ties between people are absent. Psychology's within-person studies hold the thought and the day, for a few dozen or a few hundred people, on a handful of items. Network studies hold who is tied to whom and rarely what either is like. The literatures have a gap between them that each names about the others: what is known about how people differ says almost nothing about how any one person changes, and what is known about pairs says almost nothing about the people in them.

A system that asks every participant to spend a small budget of attention each day, on items with keys and items without, on sealed predictions about people they know, on reports of their own body, and that can hold a genome the person already owns, holds all four shapes at once. Its rate of arrival is fixed by attention. The constraint is a daily attention budget per person, with a calibrated attention cost per instrument: a keyed test costs an hour, a sealed prediction costs a tap, a genome import costs one afternoon once. One question a day is one allocation of that budget, not the budget, and every quantity in this paper is per unit of attention spent, in the units the budget is stated in. So the total information the system can ever gather is bounded by persons times attention, and the theory of how to structure and mine it is the theory of spending that budget well. The data are not given. They are chosen, one observation at a time, and the choice is the most powerful instrument in the system.

**2 · What the data is**

The units, named generically so that a reader outside any product can follow. A keyed instrument is one graded against an answer the person does not control. A self-report bank is a set of items with no key, unbounded in size. A dyadic sealed-prediction record holds, for a pair of people who know each other, each one's sealed answer and each one's sealed prediction of the other's, graded when both are revealed. A fixed biological column is a genome or another molecular file the person already holds. An occasion report is a self-report of state, sleep or illness or energy, on a day. A profile fact is a slowly moving attribute, an age band, a country, an education band, snapshotted onto everything the person does.

The native record is not the person and not the answer. It is the person-occasion: one person, one moment, one instrument, one response, and the state the person was in when they gave it. State means everything about the person that varies and was known at that moment: their profile facts, their recent occasion reports, their estimated position in the between-person geometry as of then, and the context in which the item was served. A response without its occasion is a fact stripped of the only thing that lets change be read from it later.

Three units share the ledger. The person is the unit of the fixed columns and the slowly moving traits. The occasion is the unit of everything that varies. The tie is the unit of the dyadic record: a pair or a group keyed by its members and a day, and nothing in it reduces to the person unit, because a prediction is a claim by one person about another and belongs to neither alone.

Seen whole, the data is a tensor of persons by items by occasions, with pairs by items by occasions beside it and the fixed columns as context on the person mode. The tensor is almost entirely empty, and the emptiness is not a defect to be imputed away. The design variable is the weighted co-observation graph over items: which pairs of items were answered by enough shared people. The identification condition for the whole structure is that this graph be connected, with sufficient weight on every edge the model reads. Which cells get filled, for whom, in what order, is how that graph is built, and it is what paper 0a is about.

**3 · The structure: the joint model is the database**

The claim of this section is that the right data structure for many-axis data is not a schema but a model, and that storing the model's sufficient statistics, with the record of how they were collected, is what it means to store the data well.

*3.1 Three geometries, and what makes them one.* Every axis measures into a shared between-person space: a latent geometry in which a person has a position and a trait is a direction. Genome, keyed score, self-report, observer prediction, interest and body all load onto it, each through its own measurement model. The loadings are group-varying by default. Invariance across language, country and cohort is an estimand tested per item, never an assumption, the invariance status of every item travels with every number that used it, and a latent mean is comparable across groups only over the items that pass. Beside the shared space, each person has a within-person state geometry: how that person varies from day to day. Per-person spaces are not free fits. They are draws from a population distribution over geometries under a shared prior, aligned by anchor items whose loadings are fixed across people, with per-person dimensionality itself shrunk toward a population value; what is estimated is the distribution of geometries and each person's shrunken posterior, never millions of independent networks. And the tie has its own geometry: a pair's position is given by its members' positions plus a relationship term, and the relationship term is the distinctive component of prediction accuracy after the population-normative prediction is removed. A right prediction for the modal answer measures cleverness about people in general; a right prediction of this person's departure from the mode measures knowledge of this person, and only the second belongs to the tie. Ties form assortatively, so the relationship term is estimated only over the range of similarity the tie sample spans, and that range is stated with it. Connections between axes are cross-loadings in the first geometry, couplings in the second, and relationship terms in the third, and the structure keeps them in different places so that a pattern found between people is never read as a pattern within one.

*3.2 Axes as measurement models with bias models.* An axis is not a column. It is a function from the latent position to an observed response, plus a model of how that function lies. Self-report lies by self-presentation and by recall. A keyed test lies by familiarity and by the effort the taker brought. An observer lies by closeness and by projection. A genome does not lie, but it is read through a prediction whose quality decays with ancestral distance and through a file whose chip and vendor are batch structure. Serving lies by selection: who was offered which item and why. The structure holds each axis's bias model as part of the axis. A bias term is admitted and written back only when it is identified against an axis that does not share its mechanism: self-presentation against an observer's rating, recall against a same-day state report, familiarity against a keyed retest under staggered exposure. A bias identified only against the model's own latent is a residual that has been fitted and renamed, and it is not written back.

*3.3 Custody, and what it costs.* Raw data has a custody. A public answer may travel anywhere. A body stream should not leave the device. A genome must not. The structure makes the unit of exchange the sufficient statistic rather than the row: for each person, the device holds the raw and computes what the joint model needs from it, a posterior over the person's position, a summary of their trajectory, and their contribution to each population estimate, and the server aggregates contributions under secure aggregation and never holds what produced them. Three things must be said about this at once, because each is where a version of this idea goes wrong.

First, composition by summation reaches first-order statistics only. Quantities defined on pairs of people, genetic relatedness, ancestry components, a within-family contrast, need a product of two people's data that no sum of per-device contributions contains. The structure names the pairwise primitive those quantities need, a two-party computation between the two devices or a bounded enclave that holds only the relatedness block, rather than assuming aggregation reaches them. Without that primitive the custody rule would forbid ancestry control, and section 3.2's own warning says what follows.

Second, secure aggregation bounds what the server learns from a contribution. It says nothing about what the published estimate reveals. Per-variant summary statistics are the classic surface for inferring who contributed, and a model fitted continuously makes it worse: an estimate published before and after one person joins is a difference that names them. Output privacy is a separate mechanism from input privacy: a stated noise budget per release over the whole publication history, and a release schedule that forbids differencing an estimate across a changing cohort.

Third, custody costs statistical efficiency, and the cost is priced rather than hidden. Sufficiency is relative to a model, so a model revision costs a round-trip to every device and no question can be asked of the past. Each aggregation round samples the devices that were online, which is a selection by timezone, battery and connectivity that enters every estimate. Individual-level diagnostics, outlier inspection and record linkage are unavailable by construction, and secure aggregation hides exactly the contributions one would inspect to detect poisoning, so robustness is bought with clipping and robust aggregation, which bias the estimate by a stated amount. The partition is therefore explicit: public columns are fitted centrally and are the diagnostic surface; only the biological and bodily columns run under custody, and the cost above is paid for the joins that cross into them and for nothing else. A constraint that would have shrunk an axis instead shapes the architecture around it, at a price the structure states.

*3.4 The pairing ledger.* A coupling between two axes is identified only by the people who supplied both. Two axes never observed on the same people pin their relationship to an interval, not a number, and no population size fixes that. So the structure keeps, for every pair of axes, the count of people jointly observed on them and the coupling's current identification interval. The count is a tally. The interval is a posterior object that moves as the model moves and is recomputed with it. Together they are the map of what the system can currently know, and they are what the design layer acts on.

*3.5 Bitemporal state.* Every response carries the state the person was in when it was given, frozen, and the time the response entered each derived estimate. The first lets a response be read later against what was true then rather than what is true now. The second lets any published number be reproduced as it stood on any date. A structure without the first cannot read change. Without the second it cannot be audited.

*3.6 The honesty grammar as the shape of a number.* A published coupling is not a scalar. It is an object carrying its level, its population and basis, its decomposition where one is known, the validity status of the instruments that produced it, the design that fed it, and the date as of which it was true. Rendered on one number: a coupling between sleep report and a self-report item reads, in full, as within-person, contemporaneous within a two-day window; over people present for at least thirty occasions in the past year, with the continuation hazard carried; not decomposed, direction unidentified; the sleep item invariant across the languages compared and the self-report item not, so the comparison runs over the sleep item's groups only; collected under an adaptive policy with logged propensities, so the interval is likelihood-based conditional on the log and is not a frequentist coverage claim; as of a stated date. The design field is where adaptivity is declared, because adaptive selection is ignorable for likelihood-based inference conditional on the logged policy inputs and only conditional on them, and it is not ignorable for frequentist coverage; the number says which kind of interval it is. The structure stores numbers in this shape so that nothing downstream can strip a number of what it means.

**4 · Pattern theory, first layer: the choice of what to observe**

Paper 0a develops this layer in full. This section states it, and states the two regimes in which it does and does not dominate.

*4.1 The objective, as a stated scalarization.* Each candidate observation, for each person, on each day, has an expected information gain: the bits it is expected to save in predicting what the system will later observe, with log-loss named as the utility that makes bits the unit. Three targets share the currency: the population's couplings, the person's placement, the person's dynamics. They are not one number by nature. The objective is a stated scalarization, a weight on person-level gain against population-level gain that is a policy parameter rather than a discovered quantity, plus an exploration allocation outside the current model class, because information gain is defined inside the current model and cannot value an observation whose worth is that it would show the model wrong. The exploration share is what funds section 7's self-extension. All of it is per unit of attention, and it is discounted by the continuation hazard the observation induces, because a person who leaves yields nothing forever.

*4.2 The policy is stochastic by construction.* A deterministic choice of the best item gives every other eligible item a serving probability of zero, and with that it destroys the overlap that makes selection estimable: no counterfactual arm to estimate the selection term against, no residual on never-served items, no off-policy reading of what a person would have answered. So every eligible item carries a serving probability bounded below by a floor, the realised probability is logged with the response, and a fixed fraction of each person's days is drawn uniformly from the anchor set and never selected on. The floor is what makes selection estimable rather than merely recorded, and the uniform stream is the evaluation surface of section 7.

*4.3 The constraints.* Comparison needs linking, so an anchor set is served to everyone: it is what places people who answered disjoint items in one space, it is never retired, because retiring an anchor breaks every comparison that ran through it, and a new item enters the bank against anchors already placed. Within-person readings need repetition on schedules set by the timescale to be seen and bounded by what repetition teaches. Pairing needs allocation where the ledger shows a gap, and steering depends only on pre-response variables, which axes a person holds and the counts, never on the current fit; where it must depend on the fit, the coupling is reported with the steering propensity. And the policy records itself: every served item carries the target it was chosen for, its expected gain, its realised probability and the person's state, so that selection is a known function of recorded variables.

*4.4 The two regimes.* Design dominates for population couplings. Where a pair of axes was never jointly observed, no estimator recovers the coupling, and the choice of what to observe is the whole answer. Estimation dominates at the person level. A person's own observations under a daily budget carry a few hundred bits a year, so most of a person's posterior is borrowed from the population, and partial pooling is worth more than the next item. The two regimes are separated by how much of the target's information lives in the unit versus in the population, and a system that confused them would either starve its couplings or over-ask its people.

*4.5 The open problem.* Population-scale sequential design under a per-person attention budget, a growing bank, three geometries, a scalarized objective with an exploration share and a hazard term, and custody limits on what the policy may see, is not a solved problem. Approximations exist for each piece. The joint problem is research.

**5 · Pattern theory, second layer: estimation**

*5.1 One hierarchical joint model, fitted continuously.* The between-person geometry, the population distribution of within-person geometries and the tie geometry are estimated jointly, so that every axis borrows strength from every other; joint scoring of correlated instruments is far more precise than one-at-a-time scoring, and the gain grows with the number of axes. The model updates as observations arrive, so today's serving decision uses yesterday's answers. The policy conditions on the fit and the fit is built from data the policy chose; this is ignorable for likelihood-based inference conditional on the fully logged policy inputs and a model correctly specified for that history, which is the architecture's defence and the reason the log exists, and it is not ignorable for frequentist coverage, which is why the honesty grammar's design field says which interval is being published.

*5.2 Level separation is not optional, and the budget bounds what it can resolve.* Any estimator blind to the difference between how people differ and how a person changes returns an amalgam that means neither. The model keeps a between-person network and within-person networks as separate estimands, and it says what within-person contemporaneity means under a daily budget: two items are treated as simultaneous when served inside a window of stated width per state dimension, under the assumption that the dimension is approximately constant across the window, with the width itself estimated. The sampling rate bounds the dynamics that exist as estimands at all: a state process faster than twice the sampling interval is aliased into the noise term and is not something this design measures. What is then estimable, per trait, is the distribution across people of the within-person parameters and its relation to the between-person structure: a measured discrepancy per trait, how far a between-person finding travels into a person. That is the frontier result, and it is a distribution and a distance, not a verdict on whether a process is ergodic.

*5.3 Learned representations, and the layer that keeps them honest.* Above a volume of data the fully populated system exceeds, a model trained to predict each person's next event from their entire stream, with the fixed columns as context and reveals, occasion reports and tie changes as events, will learn structure no factor model expresses: sequential, nonlinear, context-dependent. Its internal state is the richest representation of a person the system can have, and it is unreadable. So the structure keeps a distilled factor layer beside it, fitted to the learned representation in named dimensions. Its faithfulness is reported as the divergence between the surrogate's and the teacher's predictive distributions on a named distribution, including shifted and counterfactual inputs. That number licenses use of the surrogate for prediction on that distribution. It does not license reading the surrogate's factors as the teacher's structure, and the theory claims no such reading. The classical model is kept permanently as the auditable floor the learned one must beat out of sample, continuously, on the uniform stream, to keep its place.

*5.4 Honesty failures are model terms.* Selection into the system, selection into each item, practice on repeated tests, drift in what an item means over years, the consent-shaped population of any axis that requires consent, and dropout: each is a term in the model, estimated and reported, not a footnote. Dropout is the one most often treated as cleaning. Survival in an attention-priced panel is correlated with half the traits being modelled, so the response process and the leaving hazard are estimated jointly, every within-person estimand is stated for the population still present at that time with the hazard carried, and the policy's own record is what lets the hazard be estimated on everyone who was served rather than on those who stayed.

**6 · Pattern theory, third layer: causal patterns, in two classes**

Couplings are not causes, and a system that only found couplings would be a very large correlation table. What this data has that found data does not is variation inside a unit that approaches an experiment, and it comes in two classes that must not be confused.

The first class the system controls. The timing of a reveal, the order of items, the day a person is asked a given question, the timing of a notification: each can be randomized outright, and what follows a randomized event is identified by design. This is the one genuinely exogenous source the structure owns, and it is deliberately exploited rather than left to convenience.

The second class the person or the world controls. An illness, a tie ending, a move: these are observational. Onset is confounded with sleep, stress, season and behaviour; the reporting of the event is itself state-dependent, because a bad day is reported on days a person also answers differently; and past outcomes affect future exposure, which biases the ordinary within-person estimator. For this class the estimand is defined under an explicit assumption of no time-varying confounder affected by prior outcomes, the onset time is the unit of analysis, and the reporting of the event is modelled alongside the event. Relatives with genomes belong here too, with their own bound: a within-family contrast holds shared background and population structure fixed, and the non-shared environment, which is the larger component, remains.

The structure records events as events, with times and with who controlled them, so the estimation layer can ask what changed after them under the identification each class admits. The body axis pays most, because occasion reports are the most frequent events a life contains; the tie axis pays second, because a relationship event is the most consequential; and the system's own events pay cleanest, because they are the only ones that were randomized.

**7 · The currency, and the conditional self-extending property**

Everything in the system is valued in one unit: bits of predictive information about future observations, per unit of attention spent to obtain it, under log-loss. An axis's worth is the bits it saves in predicting all the others, which is the exact meaning of making other data stronger. An item's worth is its expected gain. A structure's worth is whether it lets the model be fitted at all under custody, at the efficiency cost section 3.3 states. And the whole system's worth is its compression of human behaviour on observations it has not yet seen.

The measurement is honest only on a stream the system did not choose. A model's code length on tomorrow's answers is gameable by the policy that picks tomorrow's answers: serve predictable items and the number improves while the model does not. So code length is read on the uniform anchor stream of section 4.2, drawn without selection, and reported per unit of attention on that stream; the adaptive stream is reported separately with its propensities, and the two are never averaged.

The currency closes the cycle in a measurable form: the number of observations required to place a newly arrived person to a fixed posterior precision falls as the population prior sharpens, and that count is reported. It also makes the system self-extending, conditionally. Where the joint model's residuals cluster, structure may exist that no axis captures. A residual cluster is a candidate, not a specification. It is admitted as a proposed axis only if it survives on the uniform stream, predicts held-out observations of a different kind than those it was found in, and does not disappear when the serving propensity is entered into the model, because residual structure on an adaptively sampled and heavily missing tensor reliably manufactures factors out of the missingness pattern itself. Under that test, the model's failures tell the system what to measure next, and the exploration share of section 4.1 funds finding out.

**8 · What the structure is for**

At the level of the person, it is a reading of them corrected by the people who know them and read against the people like them: which of their beliefs are reasoning and which are taste, how they are changing, and which of the changes are the body, each with the honesty grammar attached. At the level of science, it is the resource that is deep and wide at once, on which the crossing papers' questions become measurable, and the first within-person dataset large enough to measure, per trait, the distribution of within-person parameters against the between-person structure. At the level of society, it reads change in two ways that are stated separately: change within a fixed cohort present across both dates, which is identified without any external frame, and change in the population's composition, which is identified only against an external frame the system does not own; no headline number combining them is available, and the structure does not print one. At the level of the system, it is a machine whose count of observations needed to place a new person falls as it learns, and whose residuals, under the test of section 7, propose its own next instrument.

Each use has a requirement. The person's reading needs the dyadic record at inventory grade and repetition on state items. The science needs the pairing ledger to show overlap on the axes it crosses, and consent built where an axis requires it. The composition reading needs a frame. The self-extension needs the uniform stream and the residuals kept.

**9 · Conditions, and the requirements scale generates**

The structure assumes a person's device can compute a posterior and a contribution, which is true of a phone, and that the pairwise primitive of section 3.3 exists where relatedness and ancestry are read. It assumes secure aggregation with enough contributors that an input is hidden, and a stated output-privacy budget over the whole publication history, which is a requirement on population and on discipline, not a constraint on ambition. It assumes items can be repeated without teaching themselves, or that the teaching is decorrelated by design. It assumes the anchor set is answered, and that the holes in it are reported as holes. It assumes years, and the same people across them, with dropout modelled rather than cleaned. And it assumes consent scoped to a use rather than to a mechanism: custody is how a use is honoured, not what was agreed. A genomic column implicates relatives who did not consent, so its consent is stated at family scope, and the within-family reading is available only where each member consented in their own right.

None of these is a reason to want less. Each is what a fully populated system looks like from the inside.

**10 · The potential, and what remains open**

Fully populated, this is a single living model of a population in which every axis strengthens every other, in which what to ask next is decided by what is not yet known, in which the deepest crossings run without any raw datum leaving the person who owns it, at a stated cost in efficiency and under a stated privacy budget, and in which the model's own failures, tested, are the research agenda. It would hold the four shapes of human measurement at once.

What remains open is the frontier. The joint design problem of section 4.5, and the setting of the weight between person-level and population-level bits, which has no theorem behind it. The estimation of a population distribution of within-person geometries at a scale of millions, with the window widths and aliasing bounds of section 5.2 made explicit. The output-privacy budget over an unbounded publication history, and the pairwise primitive at population scale. Causal identification for the second class of events when the events are selected by the person's state and their reporting is too. And the calibration of the admission test for a residual-proposed axis: not whether such a test should exist, which section 7 settles, but how strict it must be before a proposed axis is cheaper to measure than to ignore. These are the problems a research programme built on this data would spend its first years on, and each is answered by the data itself once the structure exists to hold it.
