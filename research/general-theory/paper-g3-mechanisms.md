# Pattern-finding mechanisms

*General theory, paper G3: how structure is estimated, discovered, tested and judged in many-source, adaptively collected, custody-partitioned measurements of a population.*

**Status: theory research.** General track: this paper is about any population measured by many sources, and it names no particular product or source. `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how the tracks relate. It stands on paper G1, which chooses the data, and paper G2, which holds it.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because the methods for finding structure in measurements of people were built for one instrument at a time, on data nobody chose, at one level of analysis, and a population measured by many sources on chosen data across periods and relations needs a mechanism that estimates jointly without letting its priors contaminate its couplings, separates levels, treats its own biases as terms, learns what a factor model cannot express while staying auditable, reads causes where the design allows it, proposes its own next measurement, and is judged on a stream it did not choose.

**Setting.** A population of units, standing in relations, measured across periods by K sources as paper G1 defines them, with observations chosen by a logged policy that assigns each record a role, blind or prior-informed, per pair of sources, and held in the structure paper G2 describes, refusals and expiries included. The targets are the coupling structure at its levels, between units, within a unit across periods, between and within relations, and shared by units in a group or on an occasion; the measurement model and bias structure of every source; the dynamics of each unit; the causes that within-unit and within-relation variation can identify; and the structure no current source captures. The mechanisms may run centrally on public values and must run at the custodian for values that may not leave it. Models are revised over time and judged continuously. Nothing in this paper depends on what the units or the sources are.

**Abstract.** A pattern in this setting is not a correlation among observed responses. It is a coupling in latent structure at a named level, identified by a design, estimated by a model that carries its own biases as terms and keeps its priors out of its couplings, and judged by its predictive code length on observations the system did not choose, with its structural and causal claims settled by identification and falsification rather than by that number. This paper states nine propositions with their arguments. The estimand hierarchy, with a time on every level, cross-level terms across them, and the pooled coefficient named for what it is: the marginal regression, well defined for prediction and carrying no structural reading. One hierarchical joint model over every source, carrying two nested information sets, the full one for placement and the prior-free one for every published coupling, with borrowing strength conditional on each source's model being right and a tripwire for when it is not, and with inference on chosen data ignorable of the service decision only under positivity and distinctness, for that decision alone, and only by an analysis that enters the log. Level separation as mechanics: a centre estimated jointly with the dynamics, windows, irregular sampling, and a defined per-dimension discrepancy computed net of each level's error. Bias as model terms, each with the design that identifies it, written back only where a third mechanism makes the shared-mechanism assumption testable. Learned representations where the classical class is misspecified, judged per estimand against the strongest classical floor, beside a distilled layer whose faithfulness is an expected log-ratio under a fixed distribution and licenses substitution, never a structural reading. Causal readings in two classes, randomized under a stated exposure mapping and consent where the system controls the event, and under sequential ignorability with the estimators that assumption admits where it does not. Discovery from residuals under a five-clause admission test with a published failure rate. Evaluation on the non-adaptive stream for predictive claims, with adequacy checks beside the ranking. And computation at population scale under custody, with the two approximation errors of amortized inference, the participation selection of every aggregation round, and the two binding points named.

**1 · What a pattern is here**

In found data a pattern is often a correlation between two observed columns. In this setting that is not a pattern, for three reasons the previous two papers establish. Observed responses carry each source's bias, so a correlation between two responses is partly a correlation between two biases. The data were chosen, so a correlation between two responses is partly a property of the choosing. And the responses were taken at different levels, some describing how units differ and some describing how a unit changed, and a correlation that mixes them describes neither.

A pattern is therefore a coupling in latent structure, at a named level, between quantities each source's measurement model recovers from its responses net of its bias, identified by a design that observed the right units on the right sources in the right roles, estimated by a model that conditions on the record of that design, and judged by how much it improves prediction of observations the design did not choose, where the claim is predictive, and by identification and falsification where it is not. Each clause of that sentence is a mechanism, and the rest of this paper is those mechanisms in order.

**2 · The estimand hierarchy**

*Proposition 1.* The structure to be found has levels that are different objects: how units differ, how a unit moves across periods, how relations differ from each other and how one relation moves, and what units sharing a group or an occasion share. Beneath them sit the measurement model and bias model of every source; across them sit the cross-level terms, chief among them whether a unit's position predicts the strength of its own within-unit coupling. Every level carries a time, since the between-unit structure of a population whose members arrive and leave is itself a moving object. An estimator blind to the levels returns a quantity that is none of them.

*Argument.* The between-unit coupling of two quantities is a fact about the population's joint distribution of positions, at a time. The within-unit coupling is a fact about how one unit's deviations from its own centre co-move, and it comes in two forms, contemporaneous, within a window short enough that the state is constant across it, and temporal, across a lag. A relation has both a between and a within: how pairs differ from each other, and how one pair moves. Units that share a group or an occasion share an effect that is neither theirs nor the population's. Nothing forces the levels to agree in magnitude or sign, and for many pairs of quantities they will not: a coupling that is positive between units because both quantities track a third stable attribute can be negative within a unit because one quantity's rise draws on the other.

*What pooling estimates.* An estimator that pools across levels estimates the regression of the marginal distribution, whose weights are set by the design's mix of units and periods and which, where the design is unbalanced or the within-unit couplings differ across units, need not lie between the levels it mixes at all. That quantity is well defined and is the right one for predicting a further observation from an arbitrary unit; it is not either level's coupling, and it carries no structural or causal reading. Each level is estimated as its own object and reported as such, and the hierarchy is the first fact about the mechanism because every later proposition is stated inside it.

*Relations.* A relational term is identified only where both members report, since what a pair carries separates from what each member brings to every pair only when both directions are observed, and from error only when the pair is observed more than once, as paper G1's section 2 requires.

*The measurement layer.* A source's measurement model maps a latent quantity to a response distribution, per item where the source has items, and its bias model says how the mapping lies: by presentation, by recall, by familiarity, by closeness, by the conditions under which the observation was taken. Invariance of the measurement model across the groups the system compares is an estimand rather than a property assumed, but it is not testable item by item without a reference: a set of items is held invariant to fix the metric, that set is stated with every comparison, and the design buys the observations that would show it has drifted. Invariance is tested across the groups compared and across time within a unit, since an item whose meaning moved and a unit that moved produce the same series. Where invariance is partial, the comparable item set is named per pair of groups compared, and no chain of comparisons is formed across differing sets. The invariance status travels with every number in the type paper G2 defines.

**3 · One hierarchical joint model, with two information sets**

*Proposition 2.* All sources are estimated in one hierarchical joint model rather than one at a time. Each source borrows strength from every other so long as each source's model is right; the model carries two nested information sets, the full one for placement and prediction and the prior-free one for every coupling it publishes; per-unit dynamics are draws from a population distribution conditional on the unit's attributes and budget, aligned by anchors held invariant under continuous test; the model updates as observations arrive; and inference on the chosen data is ignorable of the service decision only under positivity and distinctness, for that decision alone, and only by an analysis that enters the log.

*Why joint, and its tripwire.* Sources are correlated views of a smaller latent structure. Estimating each alone discards the information the others carry about it; estimating them jointly recovers it, and the gain grows with the number of sources so long as each source's measurement and bias model is right, because a joint fit carries one source's misspecification through the shared latent into every other source's estimates. So the saving each source shows on the evaluation stream is read in both directions, as its value and as the tripwire that it has begun to cost the others, and a source that fails is cut from the shared likelihood and fitted apart until its model is repaired. For a unit whose own observations under a budget are few, the joint model is most of what the system knows about them: the population's couplings and the units like them carry more information about a unit's position than the unit's own responses do, which is paper G1's second regime stated as an estimator.

*Two information sets.* The joint fit is not one information set. A coupling between two sources is estimated on the records taken blind to the other, and on population quantities those records alone support, because a unit's value shrunk toward a quantity that prior-informed records helped compute carries the prior into the blind estimate, which is the leakage paper G1's section 7 forbids. The joint model therefore carries two nested information sets, the full one for placement and prediction and the prior-free one for every coupling it publishes, and every published coupling states which it was computed on.

*Alignment.* Per-unit within-unit spaces are not free fits. They are draws from a population distribution over dynamics under a shared prior, conditional on the unit's recorded attributes and on its budget, since an unconditional prior shrinks the units that differ from the majority toward the majority and the units able to give the most attention are not a random sample of the population. They are aligned by anchor items whose loadings are held equal across units under continuous test, the anchors being the only place the invariance assumption is made and the place where its failure is bought detection, with per-unit dimensionality shrunk toward a population value. What is estimated is the distribution of dynamics and each unit's shrunken posterior, never millions of independent models.

*Continuity.* The model updates as observations arrive rather than being refit from nothing, so that the policy of paper G1 conditions on the current estimate and today's observations enter tomorrow's choices.

*Inference on chosen data.* The policy conditions on the fit and the fit is built from data the policy chose. For likelihood-based inference the serving decision is ignorable conditional on the logged inputs and realised probabilities, provided every eligible observation carried probability above the floor and the selection parameters are distinct from the model's. Ignorability covers what was served and nothing else: refusal, expiry and departure are separate mechanisms carried by their own terms in section 5. It does not license an analysis that ignores the log, since an estimator fitted as though the data were drawn at random is biased even with every probability known, so the propensity enters by weighting or by conditioning on the history. A fixed-sample interval computed as though the data were independent draws does not attain nominal coverage on adaptively collected data; an interval constructed to be valid under sequential selection does, and every published interval says which it is, in the type paper G2 defines.

**4 · Level separation as mechanics**

*Proposition 3.* Within-unit structure is estimated on deviations from a centre estimated jointly with the dynamics, contemporaneity is defined by a window whose width is estimated per dimension, the sampling schedule bounds which dynamics exist as estimands, irregular lags are what make faster dynamics identifiable, and the relation between levels is a defined per-dimension discrepancy computed on a common scale net of each level's error.

*Centering.* A unit's within-unit series is read as deviations from that unit's centre, and the centre is a parameter estimated jointly with the dynamics rather than a mean subtracted first: with a lagged term in the model, deviations taken from a unit's own sample mean correlate with the residual and attenuate the temporal coupling by an amount that grows as the unit's occasions become few, which under a budget is the ordinary case. The treatment of the first occasion is stated with the dynamics, since at short series it is not a detail. How the centre is defined, a fixed level, a slow trend, a seasonal profile, selects which band of variation the within-unit coupling is about, so the choice is stated with every within-unit number.

*Windows.* Under a budget, two quantities are rarely observed on the same unit at the same instant. Two observations are treated as contemporaneous when they fall inside a window of stated width per dimension, under the assumption that the dimension is approximately constant across the window, and the width is estimated rather than assumed. A dimension that moves faster than the window cannot have a contemporaneous coupling estimated at all.

*Sampling.* A regularly spaced series resolves oscillations no faster than twice its interval. The schedules paper G1 designs are not regularly spaced, and unequal spacing is what makes a continuous-time process faster than the mean interval identifiable, so the bound is stated per dimension as a precision rather than a wall, and its floor is the shortest lag realized. What no schedule can do is separate a fast process from response error without occasions dense enough to see it, so a designed minority of unit-periods carries bursts of observation, and the burst subsample tells the schedule which dimensions the ordinary rate is failing. The time of day of each observation is recorded and modelled, because a daily series taken at a varying clock time folds any daily cycle into the between-day series.

*The discrepancy.* For each dimension the between-unit coupling and the population distribution of within-unit couplings are estimated on the same units from the same observations, on the common scale the measurement model fixes and net of each level's own error, since within-unit variation is smaller relative to response error and an uncorrected comparison reports attenuation as a level difference. What is published is the between-unit coefficient, the mean and spread of the within-unit ones, the difference between the first and the second with its interval, and where in the within-unit distribution the between-unit value falls. Whether a between-unit finding travels into a unit becomes a measurement rather than a premise, and it is a distribution and a distance, not a verdict on whether any process is ergodic, because the within-unit parameters are heterogeneous and the population of them is the object.

**5 · Bias as model terms**

*Proposition 4.* Every way a source lies is a term in the model, estimated and reported, each with the design that identifies it, and a bias term is written back into a source's measurement model only where a third mechanism makes the assumption that two sources do not share a mechanism testable.

*The terms, with what identifies them.* Selection into the system and into each observation, estimable because the log carries the policy's inputs and probabilities. Practice on repeated keyed observations, staggered in first exposure so that it is not collinear with calendar time, and identified only up to the linear dependence of exposure, age and period, one of which is fixed by a stated assumption whatever the design. Drift in what an item means over years and in what an instrument version measures, with instrument changes as time-stamped boundaries. Dropout, modelled beside the response process: leaving is correlated with what is measured, and no data identify how, since the link between the two processes is an assumption whether it is made by shared random effects or by an extrapolation over the patterns not observed; so the identified estimand is the one conditional on the units present, and it is what is published by default, while any statement about the whole population is published as a range over the assumed link, with the range stated. Day-level non-response, modelled per occasion as a separate process from leaving. Response style, a unit's tendency toward the extremes or the middle, identified across content domains whose positions are not expected to be extreme together, or against items whose response distribution is known, and reported with which of the two identified it. Consent-shaped populations, where a source requires consent and its population is therefore a selected one. The occasion method factor, where sources answered in one sitting, a session within an occasion, share the sitting's conditions, identified because the design sometimes places the same two sources in one sitting and sometimes in two, at random. Attentiveness, a unit-occasion term for responses given without engagement, estimated from the response pattern and from occasions the design plants for the purpose, because at population scale careless responding is a larger structure than most of the biases above. And feedback, where a unit was told what the model reads of it and answers the reading rather than the question, identified by the randomization of when it was told.

*Admissibility.* A bias identified only against the model's own latent is a residual that has been fitted and renamed. A bias is admitted and written back only when it is identified against a source that does not share its mechanism: a presentation bias against an observer's report, a recall bias against a same-occasion state report, a familiarity bias against a keyed retest under staggered exposure. Whether two sources share a mechanism is an assumption and not a measurement: what it asserts is that their residuals are independent given the latent, which is checkable only where a third source of a further mechanism exists. Where one does, the assumption is tested and the term written back. Where none does, the term is not written back; it is published as a bound over an assumed range of shared residual correlation, and the range is stated with it.

**6 · Learned representations, and the layer that keeps them honest**

*Proposition 5.* Where the classical class is misspecified, a model trained to predict each unit's next observation from its entire stream learns structure that class cannot express, and above some volume of data its code length on the evaluation stream is shorter. Whether that volume has been reached is a measurement, run per estimand and per axis of growth. The learned representation is kept beside a distilled layer whose faithfulness is an expected log-ratio under a fixed distribution, licensing substitution and never a structural reading, and beneath the strongest classical model the mechanism can fit, which remains the auditable floor the learned one must beat, out of sample, continuously, to keep its place.

*What it learns, and when.* Sequential structure, how an event changes what follows it; nonlinear structure, where the effect of one quantity depends on another; context-dependent structure, where the same response means different things after different histories. A static linear factor model expresses none of these; a dynamic one expresses some. If the classical class is correctly specified the learned model can at best match it; the crossover exists only where the class is misspecified, which is an assumption tested by the race itself. Units, occasions per unit and sources grow separately and occasions per unit are capped by the budget, so a class may cross for between-unit structure and never for within-unit sequential structure, and the crossover is measured per estimand and per axis of growth rather than declared once. The floor is the strongest classical model the mechanism can fit, dynamics and interactions included, not the simplest one, or the crossover measures the floor's poverty rather than the learned model's reach. The model that drives the serving policy is named and versioned, since the floor and the learned model are otherwise fitted on data one of them chose, and where the policy is driven by either, a share of the exploration budget is allocated by the other or the comparison is stated as conditional on the serving model.

*The distilled layer.* The learned representation is unreadable. A factor layer is fitted to it, in named dimensions, and its faithfulness is reported as the expected log-ratio of the teacher's predictive distribution to the surrogate's, under the distribution the surrogate will be used on, which is what makes it a bound on the extra code length substitution costs. It is a licence to stand in for the teacher there and not a statement of either model's accuracy, which is measured directly against outcomes; it is recomputed as the serving distribution moves; and the inputs it is computed over, shifted and counterfactual included, are named and fixed in advance, since a number computed on inputs chosen afterwards bounds nothing. Two models can agree on every prediction on a distribution and disagree entirely about why, and a faithfulness number is silent on the why.

*The floor.* The classical model is the auditable layer: every published pattern is recomputable by a reader from the model's published parameters, under the identification and rotation convention recorded with the model version, and from the public statistics; where a pattern was fitted from values under custody, what the reader recomputes is the fold over the aggregated contributions rather than the fit over the raw. The learned model earns its place by beating the floor on the evaluation stream, and loses it the moment it does not, and both are reported.

**7 · Causal patterns, in two classes**

*Proposition 6.* Couplings are not causes. What this setting has that found data does not is variation inside a unit or a relation that approaches an experiment, in two classes that must not be confused: events the system controls, which can be randomized and read by design under a stated exposure mapping and consent, and events the unit or the world controls, which are observational and read under sequential ignorability with the estimators that assumption admits.

*The first class.* The time an observation is served, the order of items within a sitting, when a unit is told something the system knows, when a relation is prompted: each is the system's decision, so each can be randomized, and what follows a randomized event is identified by design. These are the only genuinely exogenous sources of variation the system owns, and the mechanism exploits them deliberately. Randomization identifies a direct effect only under a stated exposure mapping over the relation graph, since a disclosure to one unit reaches the units it stands in relation to; where the graph is dense the assignment is made on clusters of relations and the estimand names the direct and the spillover parts separately. Randomized disclosure is consented to in advance, so the effect is identified for the consenting population and is published as that population's, under the consent-shaped term of section 5. Randomized co-assignment of sources to sittings is the standing device against the occasion method factor, and randomized order the device against order effects.

*The second class.* An illness, a change in a relation, a move, a loss: the unit did not choose it, but neither did the system, and its onset is confounded with what preceded it, its reporting is itself state-dependent, and past outcomes affect future exposure. For this class the estimand is defined under sequential ignorability: given the recorded past, onset at each period is independent of the potential outcomes. Because past outcomes do affect future exposure, adjusting for the intervening state as a covariate is not valid, and the estimate is computed by reweighting each period by the inverse of a fitted onset probability, or by standardizing over the period sequence, either of which remains valid when the time-varying confounder is itself a consequence of prior outcomes. The onset is defined as a threshold crossing after a clear window, with the definition travelling with every result; the effect is read against matched non-event periods of the same unit at the same calendar position, because a threshold crossing on a noisy report selects positive error and a return to the unit's centre is what that selection produces on its own; the pre-event window is reported as a falsification test; and the event's reporting is modelled beside the event. A within-relation contrast holds shared background fixed and leaves the non-shared remainder, and the mechanism says which is which.

**8 · Discovering structure no source captures**

*Proposition 7.* Where the joint model's residuals cluster, structure may exist that no source captures. A cluster is a candidate, not a finding, and it is admitted only when the units whose residuals proposed it are excluded from the units on which it is tested; when it survives on the evaluation stream; when it predicts observations on a source other than the one its residual was computed in; when it survives the serving propensity by weighting or matching rather than by entering it as a term; and when it is not absorbed by a widened bias model, since a mis-modelled response style clusters residuals as readily as unmeasured structure.

*Argument.* Residual structure on an adaptively sampled, heavily missing tensor reliably manufactures factors out of the missingness pattern itself: units served similar observations for similar reasons share residuals for reasons that are about the design. So a candidate is tested on units that did not propose it, on the stream the design did not choose, on observations of a kind other than those that produced it, with the propensity handled by a method whose form does not limit what it removes, and against the rival that the bias model was wrong. A candidate that survives specifies what would measure it: the items that would explain it, the population that would fund it, the custody it would need. Admission is a comparison of code lengths that charges the candidate for stating it and for the number of candidates screened, and the realised rate at which admitted candidates later fail on the evaluation stream is published as a running number. Under that test the mechanism is self-extending, and the exploration share of paper G1 funds finding out whether a candidate pays.

*Multiplicity.* A system that estimates thousands of couplings and residual clusters at once will find its largest estimates among its noisiest. Every ranking is over shrunken posteriors, every reported extreme is reported with the shrinkage that produced it, and no coupling is read as a finding on its adaptive-stream value alone; shrinkage bounds the size of extreme estimates and the published failure rate of admissions is what bounds their error rate.

**9 · Evaluation as the arbiter, and adequacy beside it**

*Proposition 8.* Predictive claims, a model's replacement, a source's marginal value, a candidate's admission, are settled in one currency, the code length of future observations on the evaluation stream, per unit of attention, under log-loss. Structural and causal claims are not: code length is nearly flat in the parameters they live in, so they are settled by identification and by their own falsification tests, and published with which of the two settled them. Beside the ranking, the model is checked for adequacy against the features code length is insensitive to.

*The stream.* The evaluation stream is the one the policy never chose, which is what makes it comparable, and it is not uniform over sources: a rarely served source is drawn at a raised rate so that its value is resolvable at all. The stream drawn from the anchors calibrates the anchors; pricing any other source needs the stream drawn from the eligible set, and the two are reported separately. On the adaptive stream the policy chooses tomorrow's observations, so a model can shorten its code length by serving predictable observations without predicting anything better, which is why nothing is compared there. Code length on the evaluation stream compares models; it does not bound a model's loss on the adaptive stream where it serves, which is reported beside it as its own number.

*What is priced.* A source's worth is its saving relative to the current portfolio, per attention unit it cost, on the evaluation stream, and it is a set function: a source's value beside another that carries the same structure is not its value alone, and both readings are named where either is used. The system's progress is the number of observations required to place a newly arrived unit to a fixed posterior precision, which falls as the population prior sharpens, and that count is reported as the measurable form of the system getting cheaper as it learns. Models compete continuously and out of sample, and a model that stops winning is replaced.

*Adequacy.* Code length ranks models and does not say the best is good enough, so beside it the mechanism checks the fitted model against features the code length is insensitive to: the variance at each level, the autocorrelation at the schedule's realized lags, the distribution of within-unit parameters, and whether the fitted propensity reproduces the pattern of what was served, refused and never taken. A failure there is reported whether or not the model is currently winning, since the specification the ignorability of section 3 rests on has no other test.

**10 · Computation at scale, under custody**

*Proposition 9.* The mechanisms above run at population scale by three devices, amortized inference, incremental estimation and aggregated contributions, each carrying an error or a selection the mechanism measures, and computation binds in exactly two places, which are named.

*Amortized inference.* A posterior for every unit, recomputed exactly whenever anything changes, is not a computation a population supports. A shared inference network is fitted that maps a unit's observations, with the current model's parameters, to an approximate posterior, so that inference for a unit is a single pass rather than a fit. The approximation carries two errors, both measured. The amortization gap is the difference between the amortized posterior and a posterior optimized for that unit alone at fixed population parameters, computed on a sampled subset, since no exact posterior for a subset of a hierarchical population exists. The family's own gap is separate, does not close with a larger network, and is measured by the calibration of the posterior against held-out observations. Both are published, and every interval and every shrunken ranking the mechanism reports states which posterior produced it, because an approximate posterior that is too narrow makes an interval too tight and a ranking too confident.

*Incremental estimation.* The joint model's parameters update as observations arrive, on the model-relative summaries paper G2 defines where the model admits them and on stochastic updates where it does not, so that today's choice conditions on yesterday's observations without a refit of the whole, at the cost that a summary is sufficient for one iterate and a model revision costs a round-trip.

*Aggregated contributions.* For values under custody, each custodian computes its unit's contribution to the model's update on its own raw values, and the server aggregates contributions under the protections and at the costs paper G2 states. Each round is answered by the custodians that were available, which is a selection by time zone, power and connectivity that no policy chose and no design log covers, so the round's participation is recorded and modelled as its own mechanism, and a parameter fitted across rounds is stated for the population that answered them. A contribution computed at a stale parameter enters with the version it was computed under, since the delay is correlated with the same availability. Clipping and robust aggregation bias the estimate by an amount that is stated with it. The model is then fitted across a population none of whose private values were ever pooled.

*Where computation binds.* Two places, and the theory names them rather than pretending otherwise. Pairwise quantities across units under custody, relatedness and population structure among them, need a pairwise primitive whose cost at population scale is not known. And non-myopic design, valuing an observation by the future observations it enables, is intractable exactly at any scale, so every design is an approximation whose distance from the objective is itself a number to be measured. Elsewhere computation decides how fast the mechanism runs rather than what it can know, with the standing exception that every approximation above, the amortized posterior, the incremental update, the horizon term, bounds what is known by an error the mechanism measures rather than assumes.

**11 · What would have to hold**

The log is complete for the service decision: every variable the policy used is recorded with every attempt, refusals and expiries included, or the ignorability of section 3 does not hold; and positivity holds, every eligible observation having carried probability above the floor.

The anchors exist, are answered, and are under continuous invariance test, or the alignment of section 3 fails and per-unit dynamics are incomparable.

The evaluation stream is large enough to measure the marginal value of a rarely observed source, which is a requirement on the stream's size that the source's rarity sets, and which is stated with any source value published.

The model class is rich enough that a bias term is not confused with content, and honest enough that a term is written back only where a third mechanism tests the assumption.

Randomization of the first class of events is actually carried out, consented to, and recorded with its exposure mapping, or the causal readings of section 7 are the second class wearing the first's clothes.

Custodians can compute a contribution, the pairwise primitive exists where a pairwise quantity is read, the participation of every round is recorded, and the two approximation errors of amortized inference are measured rather than assumed small.

**12 · The potential, and what remains open**

Fully realised, this is one mechanism that estimates every source jointly at every level without letting its priors contaminate its couplings, carries its own biases as terms with the designs that identify them, learns what a factor model cannot express while staying auditable, reads causes where the design permits and says so where it does not, proposes its own next measurement under a test that resists its own missingness and publishes how often it was wrong, and is judged every day on observations it did not choose, with adequacy checked beside the ranking. It is what turns the data paper G1 chose and paper G2 holds into knowledge, and it is a different object from a correlation table.

What remains open is research. The estimation of a population distribution of within-unit dynamics at the scale of millions of units, with the windows and sampling bounds of section 4 explicit and the centre estimated jointly at short series. A psychometrics of learned representations, in which faithfulness is a measured property with known behaviour under shift rather than an expected log-ratio on one fixed distribution. Causal identification for the second class of events when the events are selected by the unit's state and their reporting is too, and the exposure mapping for the first class on dense relation graphs. The calibration of the admission test of section 8, which sets how often the mechanism chases noise against how often it misses structure. The link that dropout modelling assumes, which no data identify. And the approximation error of non-myopic design, which every real policy carries and which decides how far the mechanism's actual discoveries fall short of what its objective says they could be.
