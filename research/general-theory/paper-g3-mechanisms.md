# Pattern-finding mechanisms

*General theory, paper G3: how structure is estimated, discovered, tested and judged in many-source, adaptively collected, custody-partitioned measurements of a population.*

**Status: theory research.** General track: this paper is about any population measured by many sources, and it names no particular product or source. `research/README.md` says what a paper is and how the tracks relate. It stands on paper G1, which chooses the data, and paper G2, which holds it.

**Perfect-form test.** If the app did not exist, this would still be worth saying because the methods for finding structure in measurements of people were built for one instrument at a time, on data nobody chose, at one level of analysis, and a population measured by many sources on chosen data across periods and relations needs a mechanism that estimates jointly, separates levels, treats its own biases as terms, learns what a factor model cannot express while staying auditable, reads causes where the design allows it, proposes its own next measurement, and is judged on a stream it did not choose.

**Setting.** A population of units, standing in relations, measured across periods by K sources as paper G1 defines them, with observations chosen by a logged policy and held in the structure paper G2 describes. The targets are the coupling structure at three levels, between units, within a unit across periods, and on relations; the measurement model and bias structure of every source; the dynamics of each unit; the causes that within-unit and within-relation variation can identify; and the structure no current source captures. The mechanisms may run centrally on public values and must run on the unit's device for values that may not leave it. Models are revised over time and judged continuously. Nothing in this paper depends on what the units or the sources are.

**Abstract.** A pattern in this setting is not a correlation among observed responses. It is a coupling in latent structure at a named level, identified by a design, estimated by a model that carries its own biases as terms, and judged by its predictive code length on observations the system did not choose. This paper states nine propositions with their arguments. The estimand hierarchy: between-unit structure, within-unit contemporaneous and temporal structure, relational terms, and per-source measurement and bias models, with level conflation as the failure that defines the hierarchy. One hierarchical joint model over every source, fitted continuously, in which each source borrows strength from all the others and per-unit dynamics are draws from a population distribution aligned by anchors, with inference on chosen data ignorable only conditional on the log and only for likelihood-based claims. Level separation as mechanics: centering, contemporaneity windows, irregular sampling, and a measured per-dimension discrepancy between how units differ and how a unit changes, in place of any verdict on whether one carries to the other. Bias as model terms, written back only when identified against a source that does not share the mechanism. Learned representations above a data-volume crossover, beside a distilled layer whose faithfulness is a predictive divergence on a named distribution and licenses prediction, never a structural reading, over a classical floor the learned model must beat continuously. Causal readings in two classes, randomized where the system controls the event and observational with stated assumptions where it does not. Discovery of new structure from residuals under an admission test that survives the uniform stream. Evaluation as the arbiter of everything, in one currency, on the policy-independent stream. And computation at population scale under custody, by amortized inference, incremental updates, and aggregated contributions, with the two places where computation does bind named.

**1 · What a pattern is here**

In found data a pattern is often a correlation between two observed columns. In this setting that is not a pattern, for three reasons the previous two papers establish. Observed responses carry each source's bias, so a correlation between two responses is partly a correlation between two biases. The data were chosen, so a correlation between two responses is partly a property of the choosing. And the responses were taken at different levels, some describing how units differ and some describing how a unit changed, and a correlation that mixes them describes neither.

A pattern is therefore a coupling in latent structure, at a named level, between quantities each source's measurement model recovers from its responses net of its bias, identified by a design that observed the right units on the right sources, estimated by a model that conditions on the record of that design, and judged by how much it improves prediction of observations the design did not choose. Each clause of that sentence is a mechanism, and the rest of this paper is those mechanisms in order.

**2 · The estimand hierarchy**

*Proposition 1.* The structure to be found has three levels that are different objects: how units differ from each other, how a unit moves across periods, and what a relation carries beyond its members. Beneath them sit the measurement model and bias model of every source. An estimator blind to the levels returns a mixture that describes none of them.

*Argument.* The between-unit coupling of two quantities is a fact about the population's joint distribution of stable positions. The within-unit coupling is a fact about how one unit's deviations from its own centre co-move, and it comes in two forms, contemporaneous, within a window short enough that the state is constant across it, and temporal, across a lag. The relational term is what a pair's joint quantity carries beyond the two members' positions. Nothing forces the three to agree in magnitude or sign, and for many pairs of quantities they will not: a coupling that is positive between units because both quantities track a third stable attribute can be negative within a unit because one quantity's rise draws on the other. An estimator that pools across levels estimates a weighted average of unrelated quantities, with weights set by the design's mix of units and periods, and the average has no interpretation at any level. Each level is estimated as its own object and reported as such, and the hierarchy is the first fact about the mechanism because every later proposition is stated inside it.

*The measurement layer.* A source's measurement model maps a latent quantity to a response distribution, per item where the source has items, and its bias model says how the mapping lies: by presentation, by recall, by familiarity, by closeness, by the conditions under which the observation was taken. Invariance of the measurement model across the groups the system compares is an estimand, tested per item, never an assumption; a latent mean is comparable across groups only over the items that pass, and the invariance status travels with every number in the type paper G2 defines.

**3 · One hierarchical joint model, fitted continuously**

*Proposition 2.* All sources are estimated in one hierarchical joint model rather than one at a time. Each source borrows strength from every other, per-unit dynamics are draws from a population distribution aligned by anchors, the model updates as observations arrive, and inference on the chosen data is ignorable of the choosing only conditional on the log and only for likelihood-based claims.

*Why joint.* Sources are correlated views of a smaller latent structure. Estimating each alone discards the information the others carry about it; estimating them jointly recovers it, and the gain grows with the number of sources because each new source is a source of strength for all the rest. For a unit whose own observations under a budget are few, this is most of what the system knows about them: the population's couplings and the units like them carry more information about a unit's position than the unit's own responses do, which is paper G1's second regime stated as an estimator.

*Alignment.* Per-unit within-unit spaces are not free fits. They are draws from a population distribution over dynamics under a shared prior, aligned by anchor items whose loadings are fixed across units, with per-unit dimensionality shrunk toward a population value. What is estimated is the distribution of dynamics and each unit's shrunken posterior, never millions of independent models, and without the anchors two units' within-unit dimensions would be unrelated objects that no population statement could assemble.

*Continuity.* The model updates as observations arrive rather than being refit from nothing, so that the policy of paper G1 conditions on the current estimate and today's observations enter tomorrow's choices.

*Inference on chosen data.* The policy conditions on the fit and the fit is built from data the policy chose. For likelihood-based inference this is ignorable conditional on the fully logged policy inputs and on a model correctly specified for that history, which is the reason paper G2's ledger carries the reason and probability of every observation. It is not ignorable for frequentist coverage: an interval computed on adaptively collected data does not cover at its nominal rate, and every published interval states which kind it is, in the type paper G2 defines.

**4 · Level separation as mechanics**

*Proposition 3.* Within-unit structure is estimated on deviations from each unit's own centre, contemporaneity is defined by a window whose width is estimated per dimension, the sampling schedule bounds which dynamics exist as estimands, irregular lags are what make faster dynamics identifiable, and the relation between levels is a measured per-dimension discrepancy rather than a verdict.

*Centering.* A unit's within-unit series is read as deviations from that unit's centre, and the centre is itself an estimate whose uncertainty enters the deviations. How the centre is defined, a fixed mean, a slow trend, a seasonal profile, changes the within-unit estimand, so the choice is stated with every within-unit number and is not a preprocessing detail.

*Windows.* Under a budget, two quantities are rarely observed on the same unit at the same instant. Two observations are treated as contemporaneous when they fall inside a window of stated width per dimension, under the assumption that the dimension is approximately constant across the window, and the width is estimated rather than assumed. A dimension that moves faster than the window cannot have a contemporaneous coupling estimated at all.

*Sampling.* A regularly spaced series resolves oscillations no faster than twice its interval. The schedules paper G1 designs are not regularly spaced, and unequal spacing is what makes a continuous-time process faster than the mean interval identifiable, so the bound is stated per dimension as a precision rather than a wall. What no schedule can do is separate a fast process from response error without occasions dense enough to see it, so a designed minority of unit-periods carries bursts of observation, and the burst subsample tells the schedule which dimensions the ordinary rate is failing. The time of day of each observation is recorded and modelled, because a daily series taken at a varying clock time folds any daily cycle into the between-day series.

*The discrepancy.* For each dimension the between-unit coupling and the population distribution of within-unit couplings are estimated on the same units from the same observations, and their discrepancy is published per dimension with its sign and interval. Whether a between-unit finding travels into a unit becomes a measurement rather than a premise, and it is a distribution and a distance, not a verdict on whether any process is ergodic, because the within-unit parameters are heterogeneous and the population of them is the object.

**5 · Bias as model terms**

*Proposition 4.* Every way a source lies is a term in the model, estimated and reported, and a bias term is written back into a source's measurement model only when identified against a source that does not share its mechanism.

*The terms.* Selection into the system and into each observation, estimable because the log carries the policy's inputs and probabilities. Practice on repeated keyed observations, decorrelated from age and calendar time by staggered first exposure as paper G1 requires, because a practice gain subtracted after the fact takes real signal with it. Drift in what an item means over years and in what an instrument version measures, with instrument changes as time-stamped boundaries. Dropout, estimated jointly with the response process because leaving is correlated with what is measured, so that every within-unit estimand is stated for the population present at that time with the hazard carried, and day-level non-response modelled per occasion as a separate process from leaving. Response style, a unit's tendency toward the extremes or the middle of a scale, estimated as a unit-level term so that it does not read as content. Consent-shaped populations, where a source requires consent and its population is therefore a selected one. And the shared-occasion method factor, where two sources answered in the same sitting share the sitting's mood.

*Admissibility.* A bias identified only against the model's own latent is a residual that has been fitted and renamed. A bias is admitted and written back only when it is identified against a source that does not share its mechanism: a presentation bias against an observer's report, a recall bias against a same-occasion state report, a familiarity bias against a keyed retest under staggered exposure. Where two sources share a mechanism, as when an observer's view of a unit is built partly from what the unit presented to them, only the residue the second source does not carry is identified, and the model says so rather than claiming the whole term.

**6 · Learned representations, and the layer that keeps them honest**

*Proposition 5.* Above a volume of data the fully populated system exceeds, a model trained to predict each unit's next observation from its entire stream learns structure no factor model expresses. Its representation is kept, beside a distilled layer whose faithfulness is a predictive divergence on a named distribution, licensing prediction and never a structural reading, and beneath a classical model that remains the auditable floor the learned one must beat, out of sample, continuously, to keep its place.

*What it learns.* Sequential structure, how an event changes what follows it; nonlinear structure, where the effect of one quantity depends on another; context-dependent structure, where the same response means different things after different histories. A factor model expresses none of these, and at sufficient data volume the learned model's code length on the uniform stream will be shorter. Below that volume a tuned classical model wins, so the crossover is a measurement run continuously, not a bet taken once, and the classical model is never retired.

*The distilled layer.* The learned representation is unreadable. A factor layer is fitted to it, in named dimensions, and its faithfulness is reported as the divergence between the surrogate's and the teacher's predictive distributions on a named distribution, including shifted and counterfactual inputs. That number licenses the surrogate's use for prediction on that distribution. It does not license reading the surrogate's factors as the structure the teacher computes with, and the mechanism claims no such reading. Two models can agree on every prediction on a distribution and disagree entirely about why, and a faithfulness number is silent on the why.

*The floor.* The classical model is the auditable layer: every published pattern is recomputable by a reader from the model's parameters and the public statistics, which the learned model cannot offer. So the learned model earns its place by beating the floor on the uniform stream, and loses it the moment it does not, and both are reported.

**7 · Causal patterns, in two classes**

*Proposition 6.* Couplings are not causes. What this setting has that found data does not is variation inside a unit or a relation that approaches an experiment, in two classes that must not be confused: events the system controls, which can be randomized and read by design, and events the unit or the world controls, which are observational and read under stated assumptions.

*The first class.* The time an observation is served, the order of items within a sitting, when a unit is told something the system knows, when a relation is prompted: each is the system's decision, so each can be randomized, and what follows a randomized event is identified by design. These are the only genuinely exogenous sources of variation the system owns, and the mechanism exploits them deliberately, with randomized order as the standing device against the shared-occasion method factor of section 5.

*The second class.* An illness, a change in a relation, a move, a loss: the unit did not choose it, but neither did the system, and its onset is confounded with what preceded it, its reporting is itself state-dependent, and past outcomes affect future exposure. For this class the estimand is defined under an explicit assumption of no time-varying confounder affected by prior outcomes; the onset is defined as a threshold crossing after a clear window, with the definition travelling with every result; the effect is read against matched non-event periods of the same unit at the same calendar position, because a threshold crossing on a noisy report selects positive error and a return to the unit's centre is what that selection produces on its own; the pre-event window is reported as a falsification test; and the event's reporting is modelled beside the event. A within-relation contrast holds shared background fixed and leaves the non-shared remainder, and the mechanism says which is which.

**8 · Discovering structure no source captures**

*Proposition 7.* Where the joint model's residuals cluster, structure may exist that no source captures. A cluster is a candidate, not a finding, and it is admitted only under a test that survives the uniform stream, predicts held-out observations of a different kind than those it was found in, and does not vanish when the serving propensity enters the model.

*Argument.* Residual structure on an adaptively sampled, heavily missing tensor reliably manufactures factors out of the missingness pattern itself: units served similar observations for similar reasons share residuals for reasons that are about the design. So a candidate is tested on the stream the design did not choose, tested on observations of a kind other than those that produced it, so that it is structure and not a re-description, and tested with the propensity in the model, so that it is not the design seen twice. A candidate that survives specifies what would measure it: the items that would explain it, the population that would fund it, the custody it would need. Under that test the mechanism is self-extending, and the exploration share of paper G1 funds finding out whether a candidate pays.

*Multiplicity.* A system that estimates thousands of couplings and residual clusters at once will find its largest estimates among its noisiest. Every ranking is over shrunken posteriors, every reported extreme is reported with the shrinkage that produced it, and no coupling is read as a finding on its adaptive-stream value alone.

**9 · Evaluation as the arbiter**

*Proposition 8.* Everything in the mechanism is judged in one currency, the code length of future observations on the uniform stream, per unit of attention, under log-loss. A model's worth, a source's worth, a candidate's worth and the system's own progress are all that number, and none of them is read on the adaptive stream.

*Argument.* On the adaptive stream the policy chooses tomorrow's observations, so a model can shorten its code length by serving predictable observations without predicting anything better. The uniform stream is drawn without selection and is the only surface on which two models, or a model before and after a change, are comparable. A source's worth is the code length of the other sources' observations with and without it in the model, on that stream, per attention unit the source cost. The system's progress is the number of observations required to place a newly arrived unit to a fixed posterior precision, which falls as the population prior sharpens, and that count is reported as the measurable form of the system getting cheaper as it learns. Models compete continuously and out of sample, and a model that stops winning is replaced, which is the only definition of progress this mechanism accepts.

**10 · Computation at scale, under custody**

*Proposition 9.* The mechanisms above run at population scale by three devices, amortized inference, incremental estimation and aggregated contributions, and computation binds in exactly two places, which are named.

*Amortized inference.* A posterior for every unit, recomputed exactly whenever anything changes, is not a computation a population supports. A shared inference network is fitted that maps a unit's observations, with the current model's parameters, to an approximate posterior, so that inference for a unit is a single pass rather than a fit, and the approximation's error is measured against exact inference on a sampled subset and published as a property of the mechanism.

*Incremental estimation.* The joint model's parameters update as observations arrive, on sufficient statistics where the model admits them and on stochastic updates where it does not, so that today's choice conditions on yesterday's observations without a refit of the whole.

*Aggregated contributions.* For values under custody, each device computes its unit's contribution to the model's update, a gradient or a summary, on its own raw values, and the server aggregates contributions under the protections and at the costs paper G2 states. The model is then fitted across a population none of whose private values were ever pooled.

*Where computation binds.* Two places, and the theory names them rather than pretending otherwise. Pairwise quantities across units under custody, relatedness and population structure among them, need a pairwise primitive whose cost at population scale is not known. And non-myopic design, valuing an observation by the future observations it enables, is intractable exactly at any scale, so every design is an approximation whose distance from the objective is itself a number to be measured. Everywhere else, computation decides how fast the mechanism runs, never what it can know.

**11 · What would have to hold**

The log is complete: every variable the policy used is recorded with every observation, or the ignorability of section 3 does not hold.

The anchors exist and are answered, or the alignment of section 3 fails and per-unit dynamics are incomparable.

The uniform stream is large enough to measure the marginal value of a rarely observed source, which is a requirement on the stream's size that the source's rarity sets, and which is stated with any source value published.

The model class is rich enough that a bias term is not confused with content, and honest enough that a term is written back only under the admissibility rule.

Randomization of the first class of events is actually carried out and recorded, or the causal readings of section 7 are the second class wearing the first's clothes.

Devices can compute a contribution, the pairwise primitive exists where a pairwise quantity is read, and the approximation error of amortized inference is measured rather than assumed small.

**12 · The potential, and what remains open**

Fully realised, this is one mechanism that estimates every source jointly at three levels, carries its own biases as terms, learns what a factor model cannot express while staying auditable, reads causes where the design permits and says so where it does not, proposes its own next measurement under a test that resists its own missingness, and is judged every day on observations it did not choose. It is what turns the data paper G1 chose and paper G2 holds into knowledge, and it is a different object from a correlation table.

What remains open is research. The estimation of a population distribution of within-unit dynamics at the scale of millions of units, with the windows and sampling bounds of section 4 explicit. A psychometrics of learned representations, in which faithfulness is a measured property with known behaviour under shift rather than a divergence on one distribution. Causal identification for the second class of events when the events are selected by the unit's state and their reporting is too. The calibration of the admission test of section 8, which sets how often the mechanism chases noise against how often it misses structure. And the approximation error of non-myopic design, which every real policy carries and which decides how far the mechanism's actual discoveries fall short of what its objective says they could be.
