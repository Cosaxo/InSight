# Requests — genetic

The bridge queue (CHARTER §8): what this theory wants tracked,
gathered or computed in the real product. One entry per wish:
**what · why the theory needs it · what it would make measurable.**
Central writes verdicts to `bridge/VERDICTS.md`; this lane never
implements its own requests.

- 2026-08-27 · **Percept-level items on the core spine** · What: a
  small, enumerable set of core question-bank items asking about
  single-protein percepts — does cilantro taste soapy; how bitter are
  named foods (PROP-class bitterness); do you flush when drinking;
  can you smell asparagus metabolites; morning/evening timing as the
  weak polygenic end. · Why the theory needs it: gen-10 — these are
  the only question-shaped traits where a genotype genuinely predicts
  an individual's answer (the direct-output boundary), so they are the
  highest-value pairing anchors the phenotype battery can hold for
  any future genetic cross; and single validated self-report items
  are proven genetic-grade phenotypes at scale (Niarchou 2022, at
  gen-3). · What it would make measurable: item answer distributions
  comparable against published allele/haplotype frequencies per
  cohort; later, for any consented paired import, direct
  genotype–answer concordance — the cleanest possible calibration of
  the whole genetic axiom.
- 2026-08-27 · **Interest constructs scored at factor level** · What:
  score the interests axis as aggregated factors/superfactors over
  many items, not as single-item signals. · Why the theory needs it:
  gen-3 — aggregation raises the genetic half of interest signal
  (item ~32% → factor ~48% → superfactor ~53% heritability, Lykken
  1993), and no molecular GWAS of vocational interests exists (gen-3
  scout, Aug 2026), so a deep, factor-scored interests phenotype
  paired with imported genotypes would be a genuinely novel research
  asset. · What it would make measurable: factor-level interest
  profiles stable enough to cross against every other axis — and, at
  research grade, the first data able to ask what the molecular
  architecture of interests is.
