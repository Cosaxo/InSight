# The genetic axiom — current theory

*Regenerated from `graph.json` by each run; the graph is the data, this

The perfect genetic axiom, as the graph currently stands: **import,
never assay; keep the file with the person; point everything at the one
asymmetry that matters — the phenotype battery is the scarce asset and
the genome a weak, fixed prior beside it; and connect the axes through
architecture, not gene lists — except at the percept boundary, where a
named gene really does predict a person's answer.**

## Entry — the import door (gen-1 · argued, gen-8 · cited)

The axiom imports molecular files people already hold; it never touches
a sample or a lab (**gen-1**). First argued for consumer genotype files,
then generalized (**gen-8**): the same logic covers **consumer
methylation files**, and the aging half of the theory needs it to.
Consumer-platform methylation cohorts are publication-grade (a
TruDiagnostic cohort of n=14,213 as a *Nature Aging* validation cohort;
a randomized twin trial in *BMC Medicine*). Raw-file access for
consumers is reported at vendor grade only — confirming it is a named
scout task. Methylation is a *state* where genotype is a *constant*, so
the perfect import is a longitudinal series of files, as a property of
what the system accepts — per charter §9, never a suggestion that
anyone test or intervene on a schedule.

## Custody and consent (gen-2 · argued, gen-6 · argued)

The genome stays where the person is; only derived, banded quantities
travel (**gen-2**). Genetic data is never about one person, so consent
is familial-aware and participation itself is confidential (**gen-6**).
A methylation array file is **also a genotype file at reduced density**
(thousands of SNPs recoverable at >98% concordance — MethylGenotyper,
cited at gen-8), so custody and familial consent apply to imported
methylation files at full strength. New support this run from the
analysis side: since individual prediction is additive-PGS-shaped
(gen-5), **keeping prediction on-device costs nothing predictive** —
the heavy models live in the published weight sets and priors, never on
the per-person path.

## The asset (gen-3 · cited)

The scarce asset is the phenotype battery, not the genotypes; the axiom
optimizes pairing quality (people with BOTH a scored file and a deep
phenotype record) over genotype count (**gen-3** — raised to cited
2026-08-27). The instrument premise now has direct demonstrations: a
single well-chosen self-report item carries a genetically valid
phenotype at scale (musical beat synchronization — one item, n=606,825,
69 loci, SNP-h2 13–16%, validated against experimental measures;
Niarchou 2022); web-based self-report phenotyping is the founding
method of consumer-cohort genetics (Eriksson 2010); and aggregation
raises the genetic signal (interest heritability ~32% item → ~48%
factor → ~53% superfactor; Lykken 1993). The interests half of the
battery is an open frontier, stated precisely after the adversarial
pass: vocational interests are twin-heritable (~36–50%), and while **no
GWAS of an administered interest inventory exists**, interest *proxies*
have been GWASed and show the signal is there — educational-field
choice (n=463,134; 17 loci across 7 of 10 fields; latent axes matching
RIASEC's, Cheesman 2025) and STEM occupational choice. A deep,
inventory-grade interests phenotype paired with imported genotypes
would still be first of its kind — and the proxies say it would find
something (REQUESTS row).

## The cross-connection (gen-9 · cited, gen-10 · cited, gen-11 · cited)

This run's main advance — the owner's 2026-08-25 question answered in
three parts.

- **Architecture, not gene lists (gen-9).** For psychological and
  preference traits, "which genes" has a distribution as its answer:
  the fourth law of behavior genetics (very many variants, each tiny —
  Chabris 2015), mechanized by the omnigenic model (Boyle 2017) and
  negative selection flattening the effect-size distribution (O'Connor
  2019). Measured shape: cognitive ability 205 loci / SNP-h2 ~19% / PGS
  4.81% (n=269,867); educational attainment 3,952 lead SNPs / PGI
  12–16% (n≈3M); neuroticism 136 loci (n=449,484); the other Big Five
  at 2–14 loci each as late as 2024, 1,257 lead variants at n up to
  1.14M in 2025 (preprint-grade). The saturated contrast: height needed
  n=5.4M and 12,111 SNPs across ~21% of the genome for ~40% of variance
  — European ancestry only, 10–20% elsewhere. Even perfect capability
  yields a distribution over the genome, ancestry-conditional, never a
  map of named genes. Gene-list thinking has a documented failure
  record (depression candidate genes no better than random genes; 1 of
  32 IQ candidate-gene tests nominally significant; FOXP2's selection
  story an artifact — while the KE-family monogenic *disorder* stands).
  Pleiotropic hubs (CADM2, ~50 traits) are features of the
  architecture, not genes "for" traits. The deliverable per trait is an
  **architecture vector**: SNP-h2, polygenicity/effect-size parameters,
  functional and cell-type enrichment (which maps question- and
  test-measured traits onto the body axiom's tissues), and position in
  correlation structure — plus the exception list (gen-10).

- **The direct-output boundary (gen-10).** Named genes carry
  individual-level cross-axis signal exactly where the trait is the
  direct output of a single protein — a percept, a pigment, a
  secretion: OR5A1 (>96% of β-ionone odor sensitivity — functionally
  Mendelian), ABCC11 (earwax), OCA2/HERC2 (eye color — ~74% for a
  three-SNP OCA2 haplotype, Duffy 2007; R2≈0.68 for rs12913832 alone,
  Sturm 2008), TAS2R38 (~75–80% of PTC/PROP bitterness), ALDH2 rs671
  (alcohol flush), LCT (lactase persistence), OR7D4 (androstenone) —
  all ancestry-conditional in both allele frequency and trait
  variance. Downstream of the percept the signal's fate splits
  lawfully: **mild hedonic percepts collapse** on the way to behavior
  (bitter-taster status barely predicts food intake — 72-study
  meta-analysis, best dz=−0.14; cilantro soap-taste is a weak-effect
  locus, OR 0.81, SNP-h2 ≈0.087; MC1R explains only ~73% of red-hair
  SNP-h2; chronotype is polygenic, 351 loci; coffee polygenic with
  pharmacogenes on top), while **aversive toxic percepts carry
  through** (ALDH2 rs671 is the major genetic determinant of alcohol
  *consumption* in East Asians — the flush is punishing enough to
  dominate hedonic and social modulation). The boundary is predictive,
  not just descriptive. The perfect axiom knows which side each cross
  sits on and prices it accordingly — direct-output items are the
  highest-value per-genotype pairing anchors the battery can hold
  (REQUESTS row).

- **Couplings decompose (gen-11).** A population-level gene–trait
  coupling is a composite. For attainment-type traits a large share is
  not direct: genetic nurture (~30% of the transmitted-PGS effect on EA
  rides on NON-transmitted parental alleles), within-sibship shrinkage
  (EA SNP-h2 0.13→0.04, a 76% attenuation), occupational status
  −54–57% within families (≈ indirect parental + assortative mating in
  near-equal parts). The split is **trait-specific — and for
  personality genuinely unsettled**: the largest within-family
  personality analysis finds no identifiable within-family reduction
  for the Big Five (preprint-grade), while a smaller peer-reviewed twin
  analysis found neuroticism PGS prediction reduced 80.4% within
  families (Selzam 2019, wide uncertainty at n≈2,366 pairs, gap largely
  closed by SES adjustment). Attainment is demonstrably the confounded
  class; for personality the theory carries the tension as data rather
  than picking a side. Genetic-correlation
  structure is genuinely cross-axis connective tissue (computable from
  published summary statistics, coupling traits across instruments at
  the latent level) but partly mating-market artifact (cross-mate
  correlations predict rg estimates at R2=74%; EA–BMI rg → 0 within
  siblings), and it depends on measurement (coffee's rg's flipped sign
  between cohorts — ascertainment and item wording change the genetic
  answer). So the perfect system never publishes a bare correlation:
  every coupling carries its decomposition (direct / nurture / mating
  structure), a within-family evidence grade, and cohort/measurement
  provenance. That is the honesty constraint on cen-1's shared latent
  space.

## Aging (gen-4 · cited, gen-7 · cited)

- **The boundary (gen-7):** a SNP genotype file carries a *prior* on
  aging, never a *measurement*. Every epigenetic clock reads CpG
  methylation from a dedicated array; the newest strong measures read
  plasma proteomics. A genotype call file has no methylation channel,
  and imputing methylation from mQTLs recovers only the genetic
  component — a polygenic score wearing a clock's costume. The skeptic
  pass hunted for a counterexample and found none.
- **The ambition (gen-4):** aging work runs through
  behavior-by-aging-measure crosses over the phenotype battery.
  Genotype-side aging signal is honestly weak (APOE-dominated longevity
  GWAS; ~5-year extreme-decile lifespan gap as a population contrast;
  clock-acceleration instruments at 0.47–4.41% of variance); the
  heritability ceiling of longevity is contested across a 5× range, and
  heritability is not predictability from a common-SNP array in any
  branch of the dispute. What IS established: education, personality
  and psychosocioeconomic stress track second/third-generation clock
  aging in large cohorts. The contribution to aging research is the
  deepest behavioral phenotype battery crossed with imported aging
  measurements and genotype priors — hypothesis generation over
  consented aggregates, never medical advice.

## The analysis engine (gen-5 · cited, gen-12 · cited, gen-13 · cited — fissioned 2026-08-29)

The 2026-08-27 run's single 970-word node split into its three
separable claims (central's 2026-08-28 question, go-7's per-claim-status
argument), on the same touch that **resolved the Epi-PRS scout task**
(dedicated scout pass, 26 searches, 2026-08-29).

- **The empirical finding (gen-12).** As of Aug 2026, **no
  independently replicated demonstration exists of a genomic sequence
  model beating a tuned polygenic score at individual-level
  complex-trait prediction** — and the absence is now corroborated
  structurally, not just asserted: fourteen months after publication,
  Epi-PRS (PNAS 2025, the leading published claim) has zero
  replications, zero critiques, zero benchmark inclusions; three
  substantial 2026 PGS benchmarks (46-tool harmonized, PGS-hub,
  80-phenotype openSNP) all omit it and all crown tuned linear
  LDpred2-class winners; its repository has 0 forks and two
  pre-publication bug reports from one outside user (record-grade,
  fetched and cloned). The attribution resolved as far as snippets
  allow: the reported "~11–12%" is the mean *relative* auROC gain
  (0.6997 vs 0.6399 breast cancer, 0.7575 vs 0.6618 T2D — derived from
  the paper's λ metric, snippet-grade), and the authors' own simulation
  ablation cuts both ways but leaves the alternative explanation
  undischarged: with the designed epigenetic effect at 0%, Epi-PRS and
  plain gradient boosting perform alike (both still beating
  LDpred2/PRS-CS), Epi-PRS pulls ahead only as a designed epigenetic
  effect grows, and **no real-data ablation isolates the sequence
  model**. Design note (this lane's own observation, not a published
  critique): 5 significance-selected LD blocks (record-grade, from the
  cloned repo) vs genome-wide baselines is where a leakage critique
  would land. The adjacent candidates dissolve on classification —
  arXiv 2509.20702 and GENPHIRE are text-LLM embedding papers over
  annotation text / risk-allele summaries, not sequence models, both
  unpublished; PRSformer (the strongest in-window nonlinear claim)
  consumes genotypes, not sequence, on a private cohort, unreplicated.
  The second published sequence-model claim, HFS (Sei-based, eLife
  2024, +16–40% cross-ancestry over LDAK), is likewise unreplicated —
  named in the claim rather than elided. Individual-level failure
  stays directly measured where tested (Enformer — exactly Epi-PRS's
  front-end — poorly explains personal expression variation; the
  2025–2026 generation improved, not solved this). Re-open trigger: an
  independent replication attributing the gain to the sequence model
  specifically.

- **The structural bound (gen-13).** Common-variant genetic variance is
  **predominantly additive** (theory: Hill 2008; biobank measurement
  across 70 traits: additive 0.208, dominance 0.001, epistatic 0.055),
  so a linear score captures most of the common-variant ceiling by
  construction — a bound on nonlinear headroom, not a zeroing of it.
  Three direct predictive tests agree: Bellot 2018 (~100k UKB, deep
  nets don't beat linear); **Kelemen 2025 (new this run — NNs detect
  limited nonlinearity and lose to linear regression across 28 UKB
  traits, apparent nonlinearity attributed to LD joint tagging)**; a
  2025 non-additive benchmark (new this run — additive PGS robust,
  dominance ~0.83% of additive h² across >1,000 UKB phenotypes;
  preprint). One contrary result is counted as contrary rather than
  absorbed (the adversarial pass corrected the first draft here):
  Elgart 2022 reports large *relative* nonlinear gains with the PRS
  retained as a feature — absolute bases unverified by this lane. The
  Epi-PRS/GBRT class, Elgart, and PRSformer all sit in the non-zeroed
  crack: nonlinear methods can win in specific designs; the replicated
  sequence-model win is what hasn't happened, and the nonlinear wins
  that exist are unreplicated, relative-scale, or on non-public data.

- **The architecture (gen-5).** Sequence models are the **annotation
  engine** of the perfect analysis layer, never its individual
  predictor: variant-effect and pathogenicity prediction, regulatory
  annotation, molecular phenotypes (Nucleotide Transformer, Evo/Evo 2,
  Borzoi, AlphaGenome — whose claimed wins are all molecular
  evaluations, none individual-level trait prediction). Fed into the
  additive layer they produce measured gains while the predictor stays
  auditable (+14%/+34% SBayesRC; +9.45% Borzoi-informed fine-mapping;
  protein-LM rare-variant *discovery* yield). This strengthens gen-2:
  an additive score over published weights is exactly what a device
  can compute — the heavy models live on the public annotation side,
  never on the per-person path. If gen-12's re-open trigger ever
  fires, the claim widens to "annotation plus epigenomic-intermediate
  imputation"; the two-layer architecture survives either way.
