- 2026-08-27 · **A recurring one-tap morning sleep micro-report** (a
  core feed question in the daily's own mechanics, e.g. "How did you
  sleep?" on a 4–5 option scale, answerable each morning, with the
  ordinary answer timestamp) · **why**: bod-3 (cited) establishes the
  perception channel as a first-class sensor whose reports predict
  affect/wellbeing, and bod-5 (cited) establishes that the strongest
  body→behavior couplings are within-person — which needs a
  person-period sleep datum sitting next to the same day's answers;
  no hardware, no health-data custody, just one more answered
  question · **measurable**: the within-person coupling between a
  person's own sleep reports and their own same-day answering
  (answer choices on affect-adjacent questions, response latencies,
  duel behavior) — InSight's first body→behavior coupling in bod-5's
  frame, and the concrete anchor central asked for (cen-2,
  2026-08-26). Bounded caveat from bod-3: this is the perception
  channel only, and must be published as such, never as measured
  sleep.

- 2026-09-01 · **A recurring one-tap illness micro-report** (a core
  feed question in the daily's own mechanics, e.g. "Feeling sick
  today?" on a 2–3 option scale, answerable any day, ordinary answer
  timestamp) · **why**: bod-13 (cited) establishes by randomized
  manipulation that acute body states move mood, anxiety and social
  cognition — but only at pharmacological dose; whether naturalistic
  illness days condition a person's answering is exactly the
  hypothesis bod-13 bounds and cannot test, and bod-12 (cited) shows
  the trait grain is too small to carry it, so the occasion grain is
  where the reverse limb is measurable at all; no hardware, no
  health-data custody beyond one more answered question · **
  measurable**: the within-person shift in same-day answering on
  sick days vs a person's own baseline (affect-adjacent answer
  choices, response latencies, engagement) — the naturalistic
  analogue of bod-13's experimental channel, published per bod-3 as
  the perception channel (feeling sick, not measured infection).
  Caveat for central: a sickness report is health-adjacent
  self-report; whether it needs the D330-style consent-at-start shape
  is a needs-owner question, not this lane's call. A
  medication-course flag (glucocorticoids would be the strongest
  lever per bod-13) is noted as the theoretically sharper datum but
  is squarely special-category and belongs to the owner if ever.
