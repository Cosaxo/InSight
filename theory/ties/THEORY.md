# The ties axiom — current theory

*Regenerated from `graph.json` on 2026-09-01. The graph is the data;
this page is its readable face. Status per claim in brackets.*

The relational axiom (CHARTER §2, chartered 2026-09-01 on the owner's
suggestion — *"the 1v1 and group profile"*): the data whose unit is a
tie rather than a person — the 1v1 and group duel record (sealed
answers, guesses, picks, reveals), the membership store and the follow
graph — as one measurement theory of pairs and groups, with its
cross-connection half: what the person-level axioms predict about how
a tie goes, and what a tie reveals about a person that no self-report
reaches. Seven claims at the seed — five `argued`, two `conjecture`.

## The unit

- **A tie, not a person** (tie-1, argued). Every reading — accuracy,
  legibility, likeness, independence, centrality, reputation — is a
  property of the tie or of a person-in-a-tie. Between-tie,
  within-tie-over-days and person-across-ties are three objects
  (pat-3's level discipline), each with its own basis. Falsifier: if
  perceiver variance dominates and relationship variance is
  negligible, the axis collapses into a lens on the tests axiom.

## The instruments

- **The guess, decomposed** (tie-2, argued). A guess is a claim about
  someone else, graded against their sealed answer — the product's
  second key-scored measurement after logic. Kenny's Social Relations
  Model splits it into perceiver, target and relationship variance
  once people hold several 1v1s; the shipped Insight and Legibility
  are the raw perceiver-side and target-side accuracies before that
  split. Ties are a third funding resource beside pat-8's persons and
  occasions.
- **Knowledge versus projection** (tie-3, argued). Guess = their
  answer is reading; guess = your own answer is assumed similarity
  (Cronbach 1955); the record holds all three quantities per day, so
  accuracy is reported only beside the projection baseline — and how
  much a person projects is a new perceiver-level reading.
- **Picks are nominations** (tie-4, argued). Seven of the group bank's
  questions nominate members, with the picked uid snapshotted since
  D224: Moreno's sociometric test in miniature. Reputation is a
  per-group tie reading; the unbuilt `cast` dimension is a status
  score awaiting its floor, not a missing feature.
- **Homophily on chosen ties** (tie-5, argued). Central's own
  second-ranked candidate, measurable today from the public follow
  graph and public answers; what the axis adds is whether chosen ties
  read each other better than unchosen ones. Dyadic consent is gen-6's
  kinship logic transposed to chosen ties.

## Conjectures

- **Tie type is a variable** (tie-6): friend and romantic pairs should
  differ in accuracy, projection and steadiness; the dark romantic
  pool is the instrument.
- **Reading a person and reading the crowd are one family** (tie-7):
  Foresight READ and the Oracle's grade at crowd scale.
