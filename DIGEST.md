## What each theory concluded

- **Genetic** — the genotype file gives a weak *prior* on aging, never
  a measurement: no methylation clock is computable from a consumer
  genotype file, and imputing methylation from genetics recovers only
  a polygenic score in a clock's costume (gen-7, cited). The
  measurement lives in other assays, which the import principle
  reaches — consumer methylation files are publication-grade and
  importable, carrying genome-equal custody duties since genotypes are
  recoverable from them (gen-8, cited). The valuable aging move is
  behavior×clock crosses over the phenotype battery, which is
  established science (gen-4, cited).
- **Pattern** — the LLM-shaped engine is licensed for behavioral
  streams by behavioral-stream evidence at industrial scale, but ONLY
  above a data-volume crossover it must prove: below it, tuned
  classical baselines repeatedly win, so at InSight's scale the factor
  layer is the presumptive engine until a standing comparison says
  otherwise (pat-1, pat-6, cited). Distillation from learned to
  auditable layers is demonstrated technology whose *faithfulness* is
  unsolved — the audit layer should be built as an IRT-grade
  instrument, not a post-hoc explanation (pat-7, cited).
- **Map** — three risers: every visual channel in a frame gets read,
  so each carries exactly one declared meaning (map-2, cited); layout
  stability must be structural — one persistent space, incrementally
  updated, with movement a published, budgeted quantity — never
  re-fit-then-align (map-3, cited); and the display's "thin data looks
  thin" rule narrows to frequency-framed OUTCOME uncertainty in the
  mark itself (map-4, cited).
- **Central** (this run) — the joint person-model is specified: one
  published, auditable coordinate system (the factor-shaped audit
  layer), not one engine — engines may change with scale, the space
  persists (cen-1, argued). The portfolio metric is operationalized as
  marginal prequential surprisal reduction at the paired intersection,
  split into crowd value + personal-lens value; current ranking:
  questions > tests > body/genetic (today mostly personal-lens plus
  option value) (cen-2, argued). New claim, the custody law: raw stays
  where the person is, what crosses into shared computation is derived
  and carries its basis, what is published is recomputable — the
  excluded architecture is pooled raw under operator custody (cen-6,
  argued; central's own adversarial pass demolished a stronger
  "exactly two grains" form before landing).
- **Body, Questions, Tests** — seeds only; their first scheduled runs
  come on the odd-date cadence (from 2026-08-27). The owner's
  cross-connection questions are waiting in genetic's and body's
  queues.
- **Graph optimizer** — built and verified the schema checker
