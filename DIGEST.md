## What each theory concluded

- **Genetic** — the owner's cross-connection question is answered:
  traits map onto genetic *architecture* (heritability, polygenicity,
  enrichment, correlation structure), not gene lists (gen-9, cited);
  the exception is the direct-output boundary — named genes predict
  individual answers only for single-protein percepts/pigments/
  secretions, and downstream the signal collapses for mild hedonic
  percepts while aversive toxic ones (alcohol flush) carry through
  (gen-10, cited); every published gene–trait coupling must ship its
  direct/genetic-nurture/mating-structure decomposition (gen-11,
  cited). The sequence-model question closed: **no independently
  replicated win** of a genomic sequence model over a tuned polygenic
  score exists as of Aug 2026 — the leading claim (Epi-PRS) has zero
  uptake and its own ablation leaves the gain unattributed — and
  additivity bounds the nonlinear headroom; the models' licensed role
  is annotation engine feeding the auditable additive layer
  (gen-5/gen-12/gen-13, all cited).
- **Body** — perception and measurement are two different sensors:
  subjective sleep tracks affect, measured sleep predicts cognition
  and physiology, so the perfect axiom routes channels rather than
  ranking them (bod-3, cited). Within-person is the design-correct
  grain, demonstrated for sleep (bod-5, cited). The new fronts bound
  the enthusiasm: trait–cortisol is near-null between persons, the
  CAR fails its reliability gate, HbA1c is a superb person parameter
  while per-meal glucose responses fail test–retest even in a
  metabolic ward (bod-9/bod-10, cited) — and the channel-routing
  pattern recurs on the metabolic front, with common-method variance
  named as the rival a decisive design must break (bod-11, argued).
- **Questions** — a question is an instrument with estimable
  parameters, including which response process it obeys (que-2,
  cited); cross-cohort comparison is graded by named invariance
  method, never assumed (que-4, cited). The combination bends the
  spine twice — composition toward identifying the joint space, size
  through coupling coverage (que-5/que-6). And the week's sharpest
  new result: the spine-budget tension is priced in ONE currency
  (que-7 cited, que-8 argued) — a graded validity floor outside the
  currency, program-horizon prequential bits above it, with central's
  discount-rate framing confirmed as the reduced form whose very
  *sign* is empirical: future bits can be worth more than present
  ones.
- **Tests** — passive administration is measurement-theoretically
  distinct, with the condition that the advantage attaches to the
  item's referent (momentary-state wording), not the answering
  context (tst-2, cited). The instrument space *embeds* in the
  combination space rather than exhausting it, and the joint model is
  hierarchical — between-person structure does not carry
  within-person couplings (tst-6/tst-7) — the fork central adopted.
  Types: normal-range personality is dimensional, so an archetype can
  only be a density region with a drawn boundary, and region
  solutions are real but fragile — the strongest contrary evidence
  says most people may sit in no distinct region (tst-5, cited); the
  perfect archetype system is therefore similarity-to-region
  reporting over versioned anchors, human-named (tst-8, argued).
- **Pattern** — cross-axiom couplings carry level-specific referents
  a level-blind estimator conflates (three networks, not one —
  pat-3, cited); the information budget is two-dimensional — persons
  fund crowd value, occasions fund person-specific value, and no
  crowd size substitutes for your own occasions (pat-8, cited).
- **Map** — a drawn cross-axis connection declares its population,
  decomposition and basis IN the frame, and draws between-person and
  within-person coupling as different objects — no evidence shows
  readers recover an undeclared population unaided (map-6, cited).
- **Database** — the join is the unit of design, and its currency is
  sufficient statistics carrying their basis (db-6); custody is a
  layout property (db-2, cited); the ledger-and-fold architecture
  with erasure as a cross-store transaction is grounded in shipped

- **Graph optimizer** — the week's governance lesson, measured:
